# Finance Qualifications Research: CFA, ACCA and CA

Research corpus for building exam-prep and learning software around professional finance and accounting qualifications. The main focus is the **CFA® Program**; **ACCA** and **Chartered Accountancy (CA)** curricula are covered for expansion and cross-qualification design.

> **As of:** 22 October 2026. Every file carries its own "as of" date, confidence markers and a Sources section. Syllabi change every year, so re-verify before seeding production content.

## The app

**[`app/`](app/)** is *Ledger*, a mobile-first PWA for passing CFA Level I in 60 days, built on this research and live at https://cfa.tomo.study. See [`docs/app/plan.md`](docs/app/plan.md) for the product plan and [`docs/app/duolingo-research.md`](docs/app/duolingo-research.md) for the UX research behind it.

### ACCA ([index](docs/acca/README.md)) · CA ([index](docs/ca/README.md))

## Key findings

- **CFA 2027 is a big Level I change:** 93 → 102 learning modules, Quant and Equities rewritten, ethics weight 15–20% → 10–15%, GIPS removed, topics renamed. Level II is stable; Level III keeps core (65–70%) + one of three pathways (30–35%).
- **CFA logistics changed:** no enrollment fee since Apr 2025; fees US$1,140–1,590 per level; scale-score reporting since 2025; paid deferrals end Feb 2027; max 6 attempts per level.
- **ACCA is being redesigned:** last current-structure Applied Skills / Strategic Professional sittings in **June 2027**; new qualification from Jul/Sep 2027 with an official old→new mapping.
- **CA bodies have all restructured recently:** ICAP Scheme 2025, ICAI New Scheme 2023 (Final back to twice a year from May 2026), ICAEW Next Generation ACA, ICAS CA24, CA ANZ (T3 2026).
- **Trademark rules matter:** say "CFA® Program candidate" or "CFA® charterholder", never "a CFA" or "CFA certified".
