# CFA Program Curriculum Changes (2023 → 2027) and How the Curriculum Is Delivered

> **As of:** 2026-10-22.
> **Confidence legend:**
> - **[Official]** = confirmed on cfainstitute.org (exam pages, topic outlines, the 2027 update PDF, or a press release).
> - **[Provider]** = reported by a reputable prep provider (300Hours, AnalystPrep).
> - **[Unverified]** = best-known recollection; I could not confirm it this session. Verify before relying on it.

## 1. Summary timeline

| Curriculum year | Headline changes | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| **2023** | L1 Ethics moved to the end of the L1 curriculum. Readings-based curriculum (L1 ≈ 73 readings). L1 weights: Quant 8–12%, Econ 8–12%, FSA 13–17%, Corporate Issuers 8–12%, Equity 10–12%, FI 10–12%, Derivatives 5–8%, Alts 5–8%, PM 5–8%, Ethics 15–20%. | [Provider] |
| **2024** | **Biggest structural overhaul in years.** Readings replaced by **Learning Modules (LMs)**; L1 went from ≈73 readings to **93 LMs**. **Practical Skills Modules (PSMs)** introduced (one required per level). **L1 topic weights rebalanced**: Quant, Econ, FSA and Corporate Issuers down; Equity, FI, Alts and PM up. New L1 LMs include *Introduction to Geopolitics*, *Organizational Forms…/Corporate Structures and Ownership*, and *Business Models*. Corporate Issuers rebuilt; Fixed Income, Quant and Alternatives split into more, smaller LMs. L1 fintech reading replaced by *Introduction to Big Data Techniques*. L2 weights unchanged. L3: 33 readings (from 35); behavioural-finance readings removed from L3 (behavioural biases now at L1 PM). | [Provider] for counts and weights. PSM introduction is [Official] (PSM pages); the exact 2024 LM-level diffs are [Provider/Unverified] |
| **2025** | **Level III restructured into Core + Specialized Pathways** (Portfolio Management, Private Markets, Private Wealth; the pathway is 30–35% of the L3 exam). L1 weights unchanged from 2024, still 93 LMs. L1 Equity company/industry analysis reorganized (*Company Analysis: Past and Present*, *Industry and Competitive Analysis*, *Company Analysis: Forecasting*). L2: 45 LMs, weights unchanged. | L3 pathways [Official/Provider]. L1 Equity re-org is [Unverified as to the exact year — present in 2026 outlines] |
| **2026** | **No LM changes at L1** (93 LMs; weights identical to 2025). **L2: 45 LMs, unchanged**; **one LO removed from Machine Learning** (neural networks, deep learning nets and reinforcement learning). L3 largely unchanged. | [Official outlines] + [Provider] |
| **2027** | **Major L1 update** (Quant and Equities ≈ 25% of the L1 exam), with **new L1 topic weights**. **Ethics split into one LM per Standard at all levels** (reflecting the Standards of Practice Handbook **v12**). **GIPS removed from L1.** **Topic renames:** Corporate Issuers → **Corporate Finance**; Equity Investments (L1) / Equity Valuation (L2) → **Equities**; Portfolio Management → **Portfolio Construction**; exam pages also show Derivatives → **Derivatives and Risk Management**. L1 goes to **102 LMs**; L2 to **51 LMs** (Ethics only). PSM line-up updated. **Equation Explorers** launched in the L1 LES. The Quant prerequisite reading is retired. | [Official] (exam pages, 2027 topic outlines, update PDF, 26 May 2026 press release) |

## 2. Level I changes in detail

### 2.1 Topic weights history

| Topic | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 | 2027 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ethical & Professional Standards | 15–20% | 15–20% | 15–20% | 15–20% | **10–15%** |
| Quantitative Methods | 8–12% | 6–9% | 6–9% | 6–9% | **11–14%** |
| Economics | 8–12% | 6–9% | 6–9% | 6–9% | 6–9% |
| Financial Statement Analysis | 13–17% | 11–14% | 11–14% | 11–14% | 11–14% |
| Corporate Issuers → Corporate Finance | 8–12% | 6–9% | 6–9% | 6–9% | 6–9% |
| Equity Investments → Equities | 10–12% | 11–14% | 11–14% | 11–14% | 11–14% |
| Fixed Income | 10–12% | 11–14% | 11–14% | 11–14% | 11–14% |
| Derivatives (→ Derivatives and Risk Management) | 5–8% | 5–8% | 5–8% | 5–8% | **6–9%** |
| Alternative Investments | 5–8% | 7–10% | 7–10% | 7–10% | **6–9%** |
| Portfolio Management → Portfolio Construction | 5–8% | 8–12% | 8–12% | 8–12% | 8–12% |

Sources: the 2026 and 2027 columns are from the CFA Institute Level I page [Official]; 2023–2025 are from 300Hours [Provider].

> **Conflict to note:** Some third-party summaries (and an AI-search summary of the press release) say 2027 "topic weights remain unchanged". The **official Level I exam page shows the 2027 changes above**, and 300Hours agrees. Treat the official page as authoritative.

**LO counts reported by AnalystPrep [Provider]:**
- Quant: 38 → 30 LOs.
- Equities: 66 → 37 LOs.

**Content themes (CFA Institute 2027 update PDF) [Official]:**
- **Quant**:
  - Organized around real-world applications.
  - More Q&A and knowledge checks.
  - Expanded **estimation and simulation**: historical simulation, bootstrapping and Monte Carlo, with step-by-step Excel/Google Sheets guidance.
  - **Portfolio optimization** expanded and aligned with the Python Fundamentals PSM.
  - **New reading on financial data science, AI and LLMs.**
  - **Equation Explorers**: interactive tools in the TVM lessons, available only in the LES.
  - The Quant prerequisite reading is retired.
- **Equities**:
  - Built around career readiness, with real-world examples (airlines, pharma, technology, consumer goods, utilities).
  - Valuation grounded in **financial statement forecasting** with disaggregated models (revenue, margins, investment, financing).
  - **Probability-weighted scenario analysis** introduced as standard practice.
  - Porter's Five Forces and PESTLE tied to valuation narratives.
  - Coverage of initiation of coverage, **sell-side vs buy-side research**, **activist short sellers**.
  - **Practical CAPM and multi-factor cost-of-equity estimation** with real market data.
- **Ethics**: updated at all three levels to fully reflect the Standards of Practice Handbook v12, with each Standard as its own module with targeted practice problems.

### 2.3 Earlier Level I notes

- **FinTech:**
  - Pre-2024 L1 Quant included a fintech reading; from 2024 it became *Introduction to Big Data Techniques* (fintech, Big Data, AI/ML).
  - In 2027 that LM is replaced by *Introduction to Financial Data Science* (big data, ML, AI, LLMs).
  - L1 still covers digital assets in Alternatives (*Introduction to Digital Assets*, present 2024–2027).
- **ESG:** No longer a standalone L1 reading. ESG appears inside Corporate Issuers (Investors and Other Stakeholders), PM (Basics of Portfolio Planning and Construction) and L2 Corporate Issuers (ESG Considerations in Investment Analysis).

| Year | Changes | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| 2023 → 2024 | Weights unchanged; most content carried over; some chapters revised. LMs replace readings. | [Provider] |
| 2024 → 2025 | Weights unchanged; 45 LMs in 2025. (LM-level diffs not confirmed this session.) | [Provider] / [Unverified] |
| 2025 → 2026 | **No LM changes (45 LMs).** One Machine Learning LO removed (neural networks, deep learning nets, reinforcement learning). | [Provider] + 2026 outline [Official] |
| 2026 → 2027 | **Only Ethics changed:** 3 LMs → 9 (Code and Standards; Guidance for Standards I–VII as 7 separate LMs; Application of the Code and Standards: Level II). Total **51 LMs**. Renames: Corporate Issuers → Corporate Finance; Equity Valuation → Equities; Portfolio Management → Portfolio Construction; (Derivatives → Derivatives and Risk Management on the exam page). **Weights unchanged.** L2 PSM list updated (Equity Analyst Skills; Macro Insights for Investing). | [Official] (2027 L2 outline and exam page) |

**Level II weights (stable 2023–2027) [Official/Provider]:**
- 10–15%: Ethics, FSA, Equity, Fixed Income, Portfolio Management.
- 5–10%: Quant, Economics, Corporate Issuers, Derivatives, Alternatives.

**Level II format** (unchanged recently) [Official]:
- 22 item sets × 4 MCQs = 88 questions (20 scored sets, 2 unscored trial sets).
- 2 sessions × 2h12m.

> **Note:** Private-equity valuation is **not** in the 2026/2027 L2 Alternatives outline (Commodities, two Real Estate LMs, Hedge Fund Strategies only).

## 4. Level III (context only)

- **Format [Official]:**
  - 11 item sets (4 MCQs each, 3 pts each) + **11 essay/constructed-response sets** (each set worth 12 pts).
  - 20 sets scored + 2 trial sets.
  - 2 sessions × 132 min.
  - **Essays have NOT been removed.** Level III remains the only level with constructed response.
- **Since 2025 [Official]:** Core (Asset Allocation 15–20%, Portfolio Construction 15–20%, Performance Measurement 5–10%, Derivatives and Risk Management 10–15%, Ethics 10–15%) + one **Specialized Pathway** (30–35%): Portfolio Management, Private Markets, or Private Wealth.
- **2027:** Ethics split by Standard; the core and pathways are otherwise largely unchanged [Official/Provider]. 300Hours' FAQ contradicts itself on whether L3 Quant and Equities also changed. Treat L3 as Ethics-only per its main text and the CFA press release.

## 6. How the curriculum is delivered

| Component | Detail | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Learning Ecosystem (LES)** | All-in-one study platform (desktop + mobile app), included with exam registration. Access lasts as long as you are registered for that exam. | [Official] |
| Curriculum format | Organized as Topics → Learning Modules → **lessons** with learning outcomes, examples, **knowledge checks** and end-of-module practice questions. Digital-first. | [Official] (LES and exam pages) |
| eBook / print | Downloadable eBook versions of the curriculum have been available via the LES. A printed curriculum is not included (it has been offered only as a separately purchased add-on in past years). | [Unverified] for 2026/2027 specifics |
| Practice questions | **1,000+** practice questions in the LES (per level) | [Official] |
| Topic/module quizzes | The LES has historically included end-of-LM question sets and **Topic Quizzes** (timed, per topic) | [Unverified] naming for 2026/2027 (not mentioned on the current LES page) |
| Mock exams | **2 official mock exams** included, released about **60 days before** each exam window | [Official] |
| Study tools | Customized **study planner**, **progress tracker**, **personalized recommendations**, curriculum **flashcards** | [Official] |
| **Equation Explorers** (2027, L1) | Interactive TVM tools: adjust inputs and watch dynamic tables and graphs, with guided exploration questions. LES only. | [Official] |
| **Practice Pack** (paid add-on) | Extra mocks: **5 (L1), 3 (L2), 2 (L3)**. **One Prometric-style exam-day simulation** for L1 and L2. Extra questions: **1,000 (L1), 750 (L2), 200 core + 100 pathway (L3)**. | [Official] |
| PSMs | Delivered in the LES; required for results | [Official] |
| Topic outlines / LOs | Public PDFs per level and year (Level I/II/III exam pages) | [Official] |
| Errata | Published via the CFA Institute errata page during the cycle | [Official] |

**Exam scheduling [Official]:**
- Exams are offered four times a year (usually Feb, May, Aug, Nov). Not every level sits every window.
- You can sit at most twice per calendar year, not in consecutive windows, and not within six months.
- Per-exam price from USD 1,140. Total program cost USD 3,520–4,570 (early vs standard registration, all three levels).

## 7. Implications for our product

1. **Dual-cycle content.** Through the Nov 2026 window, candidates sit the 2026 curriculum; from Feb 2027 they sit the 2027 curriculum. The L1 bank needs year-versioned tagging:
   - About 75% of L1 LOs carry over unchanged: Econ, FSA, Corp Fin, FI, Derivs, Alts, PM.
   - Quant, Equities and Ethics need **new content** for 2027.
   - Weighting algorithms must use the **2027 weights** (Quant ↑, Ethics ↓).
2. **L2 is stable.** Only Ethics LM tagging changes (split by Standard). All other L2 content can be authored once.
3. **Ethics v12.** Re-review all Ethics items against the Standards of Practice Handbook 12th edition. Guidance changed per Standard.
4. **Renames as aliases.** Corporate Issuers ≡ Corporate Finance; Equity Investments / Equity Valuation ≡ Equities; Portfolio Management ≡ Portfolio Construction; Derivatives ≡ Derivatives and Risk Management.
5. **Competitive baseline.** The official LES already offers 1,000+ questions, 2 mocks, planner and flashcards (more with the Practice Pack). To differentiate, consider:
   - Adaptive LO-level mastery.
   - Item-set (vignette) generation for L2.
   - Calculator-keystroke walkthroughs.
   - 2027-specific new-content coverage (financial data science/LLMs, FS forecasting, CAPM regression).
