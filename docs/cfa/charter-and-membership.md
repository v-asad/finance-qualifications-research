## 1. Requirements to earn the charter

| # | Requirement | Current rule (Oct 2026) | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Enroll and pass Levels I, II, III** | All three exams must be passed in order. Level III is taken in one of three pathways: Portfolio Management, Private Markets or Private Wealth. | ✅ |
| 2 | **Practical Skills Modules (PSMs)** | At least **one PSM per level** (10–20 h each) must be completed **by the results release date**. Otherwise the exam result is voided. | ✅ |
| 3 | **Qualified work experience** | **4,000 hours in a minimum of 36 months.** May be accrued **before, during or after** the CFA Program. Full-time, part-time and remote work all count (🟡 for part-time and remote). The work must be **directly involved in the investment decision-making process, or produce a work product that informs or adds value to that process**. It is self-described in the membership application. Self-assessment tool: /membership/types/work-experience-self-assessment. | ✅ / 🟡 |
| 4 | **Professional references** | **3 references.** Only **2** are needed if one is an active Charterholder/Professional member of the society you are applying to. The charter page words this as "2 if one is an active regular member of the local society, else 3". | ✅ |
| 5 | **Become a Charterholder Member** of CFA Institute (and usually a local society) | Apply at membership.cfainstitute.org after passing Level III. Only Charterholder Members in good standing may use the designation. | ✅ |
| 6 | **Professional Conduct Statement (PCS)** | Filed when joining, on reactivation, and **annually at renewal**. | ✅ |
| 7 | **Annual dues** | US$299/yr for CFA Institute, plus local society dues | ✅ |

**History of the experience rule (🟡):**
- Before 2021: 48 months of full-time qualified work experience.
- From 2021: switched to 4,000 hours in a minimum of 36 months. CFA Institute described this as the same bar as "50% of a full-time job over 48 months".
- Internships: no clear current official statement found ❓. Older rules accepted full-time paid internships. Confirm with the self-assessment tool.

**Not to confuse with Level I entry eligibility.** To *register* for Level I, a candidate needs a bachelor's degree, or to be in the final years of one, **or** 4,000 hours of work experience and/or higher education over three sequential years. Education counts here, unlike the charter requirement. (Details belong in `program-overview.md`.)

### Typical timeline
Passing all three levels takes 2.5–4+ years for most candidates. The charter is awarded when membership is approved, which can be much later if work hours are still accruing. Candidates who finish the work hours early can join as **Professional members** in the meantime.

## 2. Membership types (restructured 2026)

The structure was relaunched in 2026. **Associate** launched on 1 June 2026 (🟡, per CFA Society Hong Kong). Former "Regular" members became Charterholder or Professional members, and the "Candidate member" category was replaced by Associate.

| Type | Eligibility | Dues (US$/yr, CFA Institute only) | Votes? | May use "CFA"? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Charterholder** | Completed CFA Program + 4,000 h / 36 months + references | **299** (reduced in some markets, e.g., ~$159 in Pakistan and some developing economies 🟡) | Yes | **Yes** |
| **Professional** | Has *not* completed all three levels. 4,000 h / 36 months. Bachelor's or equivalent. Passed **one of**: CFA Level I, CIPM Level II, RBA Netherlands exam, the Standards of Practice exam, **or** holds ASIP. Upgraded automatically to Charterholder on completing the program. | 299 | Yes | No ("member of CFA Institute" only) |
| **Associate** | Current or past participant in any CFA Institute education program (CFA, CIPM, any certificate) plus agreement to the Code of Ethics. No application. | **149** | No | No. Not a designation, no post-nominal, no badge. |
| **Affiliate** | Society-level membership for people who do not qualify for the above. Society rules apply. | not listed | No | No |

- Membership year: **1 July – 30 June** (🟡 society FAQ). Dues are not prorated and not transferable.
- Associate membership is **not available in mainland China**. India availability is planned for **late 2026**.
- **Society dues** come on top and vary, e.g., CFA UK US$205 (2026–27), Hong Kong US$120, Seattle US$125, Victoria US$95 (🟡 society pages). Members aged 70+ may get waivers at some societies.

## 3. Professional Conduct Program

### Professional Conduct Statement (PCS) ✅
- **Who:** Members. Candidates file the parallel **Professional Conduct Inquiry (PCI)** at each exam enrollment.
- **When:** On joining, on reactivation, and annually at renewal.
- **Content:** Six yes/no questions covering the past two years: written complaints; investigations by an employer, regulator or professional body; litigation or arbitration involving professional conduct; regulatory or court sanctions; termination for cause (or resigning in lieu); and accusations, convictions or guilty pleas for any crime. A "yes" requires an explanation.
- **Failure to file:** Membership is suspended, including the right to use CFA/CIPM. Candidates cannot sit the exam. Non-disclosure or misrepresentation is itself a violation of the Code and Standards.

### Sanctions (Rules of Procedure, Rule 1.6) ✅
| Sanction | Published? |
|---|---|
| Private reprimand | No |
| Censure | Yes (published for 1 year) |
| Suspension of membership / of right to use the CFA designation (timed) | Yes |
| Revocation of membership / of right to use the designation | Yes |
| Summary suspension (e.g., failure to file the PCS, improper use of the designation) | Listed separately |
| Suspension or prohibition from participating in the CFA/CIPM programs (exam-related) | Exam sanctions not on the public list |

Process (🟡 general knowledge): Professional Conduct staff investigate and may propose a sanction. The member or candidate can accept it or request a hearing before a **Disciplinary Review Committee** hearing panel. The panel decides whether a violation occurred and what sanction applies.

## 4. Using the designation (Trademark Usage Guide) ✅

**Core rule:** "CFA" is a certification mark used **as an adjective, never as a noun**. Never use it in the plural or possessive, and never as an acronym spelled out in running text.

| ✅ Correct | ❌ Incorrect |
|---|---|
| Jane Doe, CFA | Jane Doe, CFA® (no ® after a name) · CFA Jane Doe |
| Jane is a CFA charterholder | Jane is a CFA · "a group of CFAs" · "the CFA's view" |
| CFA® charterholder, CFA Institute (résumé credential line) | Senior CFA Analyst (designation in a job title) · Jane Doe, CFA Wealth Manager |
| CFA Program candidate (only if registered for an upcoming exam or awaiting results) | "CFA candidate", "CFA Level I", "CFA (expected 2027)", "CFA charter pending", "CFA certified", "Proud to be a CFA Level I" |
| "Passed Level I of the CFA Program" | "Jane Doe, CFA Level II" or implying a partial designation |
| Passed all three levels but no charter yet: describe that factually and note the charter depends on experience and membership | Putting CFA after your name on LinkedIn as a candidate |
| Jane Doe, CFA, CIPM (any order of designations) | CFA in company, product, URL, email or social-handle names (e.g., "CFA Wealth Learning") |

- **"Friend words"** to follow the mark: charterholder, Program, exam, designation. For CIPM: certificant, Program, exam, designation.
- Use ® on the first prominent use in running text. Web pages and bios should carry the trademark notice (CFA®, Chartered Financial Analyst®, CIPM® and GIPS® are registered trademarks of CFA Institute).
- **Logo (certification mark):** Charterholders only. Use the official artwork, placed close to the name, in black on light or white on dark. Never alter it or put it in a company logo.
- **Certificates:** List them only after completion, with the full name, year and "CFA Institute" as issuer, in a credentials section. **Never as post-nominals** ("Jane Doe, SIC" is wrong). Never say "certified" or "licensed by CFA Institute".
- **Associate member:** "Associate member, CFA Institute" in an affiliations section only.

> **Product implication:** All marketing copy, UI strings and AI-generated content must follow this guide. Say "CFA® Program candidates", never "become a CFA". The guide also maps directly to **Standard VII(B)**, which is examinable. See `ethics-and-standards.md`. Prep providers must also follow CFA Institute's Prep Provider Guidelines (/programs/cfa-program/prep-providers).

## 5. Continuing education (Professional Learning, PL)

| Group | Requirement |
|---|---|
| CFA charterholders / members | **Recommended, not mandatory:** **20 PL credits per membership year, including 2 SER** (Standards, Ethics & Regulations). Members attest at renewal. ✅ 1 credit ≈ 1 hour (🟡). |
| CIPM certificants | **Mandatory: 15 PL credits per year** (MPL) to keep the designation ✅ |
| Standard I(E) Competence (2024) | Requires maintaining competence but **does not mandate** any specific CE program ✅ |

Certificates earn PL credits (e.g., the Sustainable Investing Certificate = 20 PL credits incl. 2 SER).

## 7. University Affiliation Program (UAP) ✅
- Recognizes degree programs **broadly aligned** with the CFA curriculum. Requirements: a viable student cohort, accreditation or authorization, and a minimum number of semester hours aligned to the curriculum. Historically **≥70% curriculum coverage incl. ethics** (🟡; the current page does not state a percentage).
- **800+ universities** worldwide.
- Benefits: the UAP logo, Learning Ecosystem access for the principal contact, the right to award **Student Scholarships**, professor scholarships, and a newsletter and events.

## 8. Scholarships ✅
| Scholarship | Eligibility | Benefit (awards from 4 Mar 2026) | Windows |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Access** | Need-based; meets registration requirements | Exam fee reduced to **US$400** (Premium package incl. Practice Pack) + tax | 4 windows a year, ~3 weeks each (e.g., 3–23 Nov 2026 for Aug 2027; 9 Feb–2 Mar 2027 for Nov 2027). Decision ~4 weeks after close. |

| **Professor** | Full-time faculty or administrators meeting teaching-hours rules | Discounted fee (amount not captured) | — |
| **Women's Scholarship** | ❓ Historically offered; **not listed** on the 2026 scholarships page, so it appears discontinued or folded in | — | — |
| **CIPM Scholarship** | Exists (/programs/cipm/scholarship) | not captured | — |

Rules: you cannot apply while registered for an exam or awaiting results. One open application at a time. An unused award makes you ineligible for future awards. Two unsuccessful scholarship attempts at the same level (awards after 1 Sep 2024) also make you ineligible. Scholarship holders cannot buy paid deferrals (which end anyway from Feb 2027).
