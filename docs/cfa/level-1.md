# CFA Program — Level I Curriculum Reference (2026 and 2027 cycles)

> **As of:** 2026-10-22
> **Primary sources:** CFA Institute *2026 Level I Topic Outlines* and *2027 Level I Topic Outlines* (official PDFs, LM titles and learning outcomes taken directly from them), the CFA Institute Level I exam page, and the *Curriculum Changes for 2027* PDF. See **Sources** at the end.
> **Scope note:** Learning Module (LM) names and learning outcomes (LOs/LOS) below are taken from the official topic outlines. Key formulas are standard textbook formulas that match the LOs; CFA Institute does not publish a formula list.

---

## 1. Which curriculum applies to which exam window

| Exam window | Curriculum | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Feb, May, Aug, Nov 2026 | **2026 curriculum** (93 LMs) | Content is the same as 2025 (no LM changes 2025→2026). |
| Feb 2027 onward | **2027 curriculum** (102 LMs) | Major rewrite of Quantitative Methods and Equities; Ethics split into one LM per Standard; three topics renamed; GIPS dropped from Level I; topic weights changed. |

Level I is offered **four times a year** (usually Feb, May, Aug, Nov). CFA Institute's Dates & Fees page caps sittings at two per calendar year, with no back-to-back windows and none within six months of each other. Check that page for exact current dates and fees.

## 2. Exam structure

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Format | 180 stand-alone multiple-choice questions (MCQs) |
| Answer options | 3 (A, B, C) |
| Sessions | 2 sessions of **135 min (2h15m)** each, **90 questions per session**, optional break between |
| Pace | About 90 seconds per question |
| Scoring | All questions weighted equally, **no penalty for wrong answers** (no negative marking) |
| Delivery | Computer-based at Prometric test centres |
| Session split (official page) | **Session 1:** Ethics, Quant, Economics, FSA, Corporate Issuers/Finance. **Session 2:** Equity, Fixed Income, Derivatives, Alternatives, Portfolio Mgmt/Construction |
| Results | Pass/fail plus a topic-level performance report. The Minimum Passing Score (MPS) is not published. Your result is withheld until you complete one PSM (see §4). |
| Calculators | Only the TI BA II Plus (incl. Professional) or HP 12C (incl. Platinum and anniversary editions) |

## 4. Practical Skills Modules (PSMs)

- PSMs were introduced in **2024**. You must complete **one PSM per level** (about **10–20 hours** each) by the results release date, or your exam result is **voided**. The PSM is not graded and is not part of the exam.
- The same PSM **cannot be reused** at another level.
- Level I options for 2026 and 2027: **Financial Modeling** and **Python Programming Fundamentals ("Python I")**.
- Level II options: Python I, Python, Data Science & AI ("Python II"), and Analyst Skills (2026). For 2027 these become Python I, Python II, **Equity Analyst Skills**, and the new **Macro Insights for Investing**.
- 2027 note: the L1 Quant portfolio-optimization readings are aligned with the Python Fundamentals PSM.

> **Flag:** The 11th-edition (2014) sub-section structure is shown above. The v12 Handbook adds and updates guidance. **Verify any sub-section renames or additions in the v12 Handbook** before tagging the 2027 question bank. (I could not confirm v12 sub-section text from public sources.)

**Code of Ethics (6 components):**
1. Act with integrity, competence, diligence, respect, and in an ethical manner.
2. Place the integrity of the profession and clients' interests above your own.
3. Use reasonable care and independent professional judgment.
4. Practice in a professional and ethical manner.
5. Promote the integrity and viability of global capital markets.
6. Maintain and improve professional competence.

**Formulas:** none.

**Typical difficulties:**
- Distractors that are almost right (e.g., "disclose" vs "decline").
- The stricter-of-law-or-Code rule.
- Fine distinctions in MNPI/mosaic theory, priority of transactions, and fair dealing vs equal treatment.
- Supervisor duties.
- Performance presentation and GIPS details (2026 only).
- CFA Institute has historically said Ethics performance can act as an **adjustment** for candidates near the MPS, so it carries high stakes. This comes from older CFA Institute statements; I did not re-verify it for 2026.

**2026 LMs (11), the same as 2024–2025:**

| # | LM | Key LO themes | Key formulas |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rates and Returns | Interest rate = real risk-free rate + inflation, default, liquidity and maturity premiums; holding-period, arithmetic, geometric and harmonic means; MWR vs TWR; annualized and continuously compounded returns; gross/net, pre/after-tax, real, leveraged returns | HPR = (P1 − P0 + D1)/P0; geometric mean = [Π(1+Rt)]^(1/T) − 1; harmonic mean = n/Σ(1/Xi); MWR = IRR of cash flows; TWR = [Π(1+HPRi)]^(1/n) − 1; annualized r = (1+r_period)^(c) − 1; continuous r_cc = ln(1+HPR); (1+nominal) = (1+real)(1+inflation); leveraged return R_L = R_p + (V_B/V_E)(R_p − r_D) |
| 2 | Time Value of Money in Finance | PV of fixed-income and equity cash flows; implied returns and implied growth; cash-flow additivity, no-arbitrage, implied forward rates and FX forwards | FV = PV(1+r/m)^(mN); continuous FV = PV·e^(rN); annuity PV = A[1 − (1+r)^−N]/r; perpetuity PV = A/r; growing perpetuity PV = CF1/(r − g); implied g = r − D1/P0; (1+r2)² = (1+r1)(1+f1,1) |
| 3 | Statistical Measures of Asset Returns | Central tendency and location (mean, median, mode, quantiles); dispersion (range, MAD, variance, SD); target downside deviation; CV; skewness and kurtosis; correlation | s² = Σ(X − X̄)²/(n − 1); CV = s/X̄; target semideviation = √[Σ_{X≤B}(X − B)²/(n − 1)]; position of percentile L_y = (n+1)·y/100; excess kurtosis = kurtosis − 3; r = s_XY/(s_X s_Y) |
| 4 | Probability Trees and Conditional Expectations | Expected value, variance, SD; probability trees; conditional expectations; Bayes' formula | E(X) = ΣP(Xi)Xi; σ² = ΣP(Xi)[Xi − E(X)]²; total probability rule E(X) = ΣE(X\|Si)P(Si); Bayes: P(A\|B) = P(B\|A)P(A)/P(B) |
| 5 | Portfolio Mathematics | Portfolio expected return, variance, covariance and correlation; joint probability functions; shortfall risk; Roy's safety-first | E(Rp) = Σwi E(Ri); σp² = ΣΣ wi wj Cov(Ri,Rj); 2-asset: σp² = w1²σ1² + w2²σ2² + 2w1w2ρσ1σ2; Cov = ΣP(s)[RA − E(RA)][RB − E(RB)]; SFRatio = [E(Rp) − R_L]/σp (choose max) |
| 6 | Simulation Methods | Normal vs lognormal (prices lognormal if continuously compounded returns are normal); Monte Carlo; bootstrap resampling | If ln(ST/S0) ~ N(μT, σ²T) then ST is lognormal; r_cc,0→T = Σ r_cc,t |

| 9 | Parametric and Non-Parametric Tests of Independence | Test of correlation = 0 (Pearson t; Spearman rank); contingency-table χ² test of independence | t = r√(n − 2)/√(1 − r²), df = n − 2; Spearman r_s = 1 − 6Σd²/[n(n² − 1)]; χ² = Σ(O − E)²/E, E_ij = (row total × column total)/grand total, df = (r − 1)(c − 1) |
| 10 | Simple Linear Regression | OLS estimation and interpretation; assumptions (linearity, homoskedasticity, independence, normality); residual plots; ANOVA, R², SEE, F-test; t-tests on coefficients; prediction interval; functional forms (log-lin, lin-log, log-log) | b̂1 = Cov(X,Y)/Var(X); b̂0 = Ȳ − b̂1X̄; SST = SSR + SSE; R² = SSR/SST; MSR = SSR/k; MSE = SSE/(n − 2); F = MSR/MSE; SEE = √MSE; t = (b̂1 − B1)/s_b̂1; s_f² = SEE²[1 + 1/n + (X − X̄)²/((n − 1)s_X²)]; PI = Ŷ ± t_c·s_f |

**Removed in 2027:**
- The stand-alone LMs Rates and Returns, Probability Trees and Conditional Expectations, Portfolio Mathematics, Hypothesis Testing, and Parametric and Non-Parametric Tests of Independence. Their content is merged or reorganized into the LMs above.
- Roy's safety-first and the contingency-table χ² test are **no longer named explicitly in the 2027 LOs**. Treat them as low priority unless they appear in 2027 LM content.
- The **Quantitative Methods prerequisite reading is retired**.

**Typical difficulties:**
- MWR vs TWR.
- Choosing the correct test statistic and degrees of freedom; interpreting p-values.
- Regression ANOVA tables.
- Lognormal reasoning.
- Bayes problems.
- Calculator TVM sign conventions.

In 2027 the weight roughly doubles, so Quant becomes a pass-critical topic.

  - CAPM regression
