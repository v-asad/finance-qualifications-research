## 2. Detail by credential

### 2.1 CIPM® (Certificate in Investment Performance Measurement)
- **Audience:** Performance analysts, GIPS and compliance staff, manager-selection and consultant roles, risk staff.
- **Structure:** Two levels. **Level I** covers performance measurement, attribution, appraisal and the GIPS standards (weighted towards measurement and attribution). **Level II** covers advanced application, appraisal, and **manager search and selection**.
- **Format:** Self-study with curriculum in a CIPM Learning Ecosystem. CBT at ~400 test centres in 80 countries. Windows in **March and September**. Results within 60 days.
- **Fees:** From US$675 per exam (early registration). See /programs/cipm/dates-fees. Scholarship available.
- **Shortcut:** CFA charterholders and **Level III passers can skip CIPM Level I** and go straight to Level II.
- **Pass rates:** Mar 2026 L1 31%, L2 44%. Sep 2025 L1 34%, L2 52%. The exam page shows longer-run figures of L1 40% and L2 36% (basis unclear).
- **Earning the designation:** Pass Level II, become a CFA Institute member (Professional membership is accessible to CIPM L2 passers), and meet the experience requirement. Historically this was 2 years of relevant experience (❓; the current page does not state it). **15 mandatory PL credits a year** to keep the designation.
- **Status:** **Active.** No discontinuation notice found. Program administrations fell from ~1,600 (FY2019) to ~900 (FY2025) 🟡, so it is a **niche product with low priority** for an edtech build.

### 2.2 Sustainable Investing Certificate (formerly Certificate in ESG Investing)
- Launched Nov 2019 by CFA Society UK and transferred to CFA Institute in 2021 (🟡). Renamed around 2024 (🟡).
- **Audience:** Front, middle and back office, advisers, wealth, sales and risk staff. No prerequisites.
- **Format:** Self-paced, 1 exam. **20 PL credits (2 SER).** Online-proctored or at limited test centres.
- **Exam:** Commonly cited as **100 MCQ in 2 h 20 min** (🟡; not stated on the page fetched).
- **Price:** $890 (some regions $990 incl. taxes 🟡). Government subsidies: HK up to 80% (100% for full-time study), Macau up to MOP 6,000, Singapore up to 70% (IBF-STS).
- **Demand trend:** Registrations 3.1k (FY22) → 17k (FY23) → 20.5k (FY24) → **12.2k (FY25)** 🟡, so demand is declining.

### 2.3 Climate Risk, Valuation, and Investing Certificate
- Five courses. Intermediate. 100 h. 12-month access. Self-paced or cohort. Online forum.
- Assessment per course: quizzes, an assignment, and two peer reviews. The end-of-certificate exam was dropped in 2025.
- Audience: analysts, PMs, wealth managers, product specialists, and sustainability-policy staff.

### 2.5 Investment Foundations® Certificate
- Introductory course on the investment industry, ethics and roles, aimed at non-investment staff (IT, ops, HR, marketing), students (including **high-school** students), and support roles.
- Refreshed **Feb 2023**. Modular. 35–65 h. 100-MCQ online final with 2 attempts. **$350.** 12-month access.
- Recognitions: **UK ENIC RQF Level 3**; accredited by the **Finance Accreditation Agency (Malaysia)**.
- Completing it makes you eligible for **Associate membership**.
- **Product angle:** an entry funnel. Foundations-level content could double as a free "pre-CFA" funnel in our product.

### 2.6 Data Science for Investment Professionals Certificate
- Launched 2023. Five courses: data and statistics foundations, statistics for ML, machine learning, NLP, and mitigating bias. ~100 h. 90-min MCQ final. 12 months. No coding prerequisites.
- **Status ❓:** The URL `/programs/data-science-certificate` now redirects to the programs index, and the store URL 301-redirects to the same place. It is not listed among 2026 programs. Our best inference is that it was **retired or withdrawn (likely 2025–26)**. Confirm before referencing it. AI and data-science skills now appear in **CFA Level I Quant (2027 curriculum: financial data science, AI and LLM module)** and the **Python PSMs**.

### 2.7 Practical Skills Modules (PSMs), part of the CFA Program ✅
| Level | 2026 exams | 2027 exams |
|---|---|---|
| Level I | Financial Modeling; Python Programming Fundamentals ("Python I") | Financial Modeling; Python I |
| Level II | Python I; Python, Data Science & AI ("Python II"); Analyst Skills | Python II; Equity Analyst Skills; Macro Insights for Investing |
| Level III | Portfolio Development & Construction, **or** the pathway PSM: Practical Macro (Portfolio Mgmt), Due Diligence (Private Markets), Managing Private Wealth Clients (Private Wealth) | Portfolio Development & Construction; Due Diligence; Managing Private Wealth Clients; plus pathway-specific options |

Rules:
- Complete at least 1 PSM per level (10–20 h each) **by the results date**, or the exam result is voided.
- The same PSM cannot count at two levels. A retaker does not need a new PSM.
- PSMs are delivered in the Learning Ecosystem using videos, MCQs, guided practice and cases. They are **not graded for pass/fail**, and their content is **not tested** on the exam (🟡 per 2023 coverage).

## 3. Other CFA Institute offerings (non-credential)
- **Professional Learning** courses and refresher readings for members (PL credits).
- **Learning paths** for Private Markets and Sustainability (bundles of certificates and courses).
- **Institutional Solutions** (from 2026): B2B workforce upskilling for firms.
- **Standards of Practice exam** (self-administered): one of the routes into Professional membership.
- **University programs:** Research Challenge; Investing Simulator Challenge; UK National AI Investment Challenge (2026).

## 4. Implications for our product
1. **CFA Program first.** CIPM and the certificates are small and declining (CIPM ~900 administrations; ESG 12k and falling) compared with ~200k CFA administrations.
2. **Adjacent opportunities:** Private-markets and PE prep (modelling and LBO practice) fits the CFA Level III Private Markets pathway. The Sustainable Investing Certificate has a large historical base, but demand is falling.
3. **Don't build for** the Data Science certificate (status unclear) or the Climate certificate (no final exam since 2025, so little need for prep).
