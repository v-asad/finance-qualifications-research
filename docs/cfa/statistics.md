\*The PDF labels its "2017–2026" row as the 10-year average. It weights by candidate counts: L1 422,652 passed of 1,044,523; L2 207,705 of 459,666; L3 147,441 of 292,511. Note that the Aug 2026 Level III press release gives a 10-year average of **50%**, while the Feb 2026 release gave **51%**. The average moves as windows roll in and out.

**For product messaging:** fewer than half of candidates pass any one sitting. Level III has the highest per-sitting pass rate because only people who passed Levels I and II can sit it. Candidates who have deferred at least once pass at roughly 20 percentage points below first-time takers, every window. CFA Institute cited this gap when it removed paid deferrals from 2027.

**Pass-rate history in brief:**
- **1960s–1980s:** pass rates of 60–90%, with small cohorts in the low thousands.
- **1990s–2000s:** rapid globalization (19.5k in 1995 to 120k by 2012). Level I fell to the 30s–40s.
- **2014–2019:** stable, with Level I ~41–45%, Level II ~44–47% and Level III ~53–56%.
- **2021:** the worst year on record after the CBT transition (Level I July 2021: 22%; Aug 2021: 26%). The MPS was reset because the exam format changed (per CFA Institute's *Understanding your exam results* FAQ).
- **2024–2026:** recovery and stability at ~43–45% (L1), ~42–54% (L2) and ~47–50% (L3).

## 5. Demographics and geography

CFA Institute **does not routinely publish candidate counts by country**. The FY24/FY25 annual report PDFs are encrypted and could not be text-extracted in this research pass. What is officially available:

| Data point | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|

| Women among candidates | 37% (2018); 39% of test takers (2019); "about 40%" (Jun 2019) | CFA Institute press releases 2018–2019 |

| Ecctis benchmarking | The charter is deemed comparable to a Master's degree in 12 markets that "account for over 80 percent of CFA Program candidate volumes" | CFA Institute press release, 16 Nov 2022 |

**Country-level (mostly unofficial; verify before publishing):**
- **China:** widely reported by prep providers and media as the largest candidate market. This is consistent with Asia Pacific being 53% of 2018 registrations. **No official 2024–2026 country count was found.**
- **United States:** the largest charterholder base, and historically a top-2 candidate market. No current official candidate count was found.
- **India:**
  - CFA Institute has run an India country office since at least 2015.
  - 23 test-centre cities in India as of Feb 2023 ("over 400 worldwide").
  - India-specific EMI payment option.
  - India impact study (Apr 2023, Kantar): a new charterholder (~6 years' experience) averages INR 28.6 lakh a year, and income rises 192% on average from Level I candidate to new charterholder.
  - No official candidate count was found.
- **Pakistan:**
  - Active CFA Society Pakistan; CFA Institute MoU with the Institute of Capital Markets (2013).
  - CFA Institute issued a specific notice on 9 May 2025 that exams in Pakistan and India were "proceeding as planned" during regional tensions.
  - No official candidate count was found.
- **UK:** female participation among Level I candidates had "reached or surpassed parity" in the UK and Australia (2019 release). No official current count was found.
- **Australia:** fastest-growing top-10 market in 2019 (+23% year on year).

> **Recommendation:** if country-level market sizing matters (e.g., Pakistan/India go-to-market), request data directly from CFA Institute or the relevant CFA Society, or use paid market research. Do not rely on unsourced blog figures.

## 6. Data-quality notes

1. **Official vs third-party:** every window figure in §2–3 comes from the official CFA Institute historical PDF or a press release. The first-time/deferral splits were compiled from press releases (via 300Hours) and spot-verified.
2. **Rounding:** CFA Institute reports whole-percent pass rates. Aggregates in §2d are recomputed and approximate.
3. **"Candidates"** means people who sat the exam in that window. Registrations are higher because of no-shows and deferrals.
4. **10-year averages differ by source:** the exam overview page says 41/45/52, the PDF says 40/45/50, and press releases give L3 50–51% and L1 40–41%. Cite the specific source and date.
5. **Results-release timing in 2026:**
   - Feb 2026: L1 results ~Mar 2026; L3 results 7 Apr 2026.
   - May 2026: results ~Jun–Jul 2026.
   - Aug 2026: L1 results 24 Sep, L2 30 Sep, L3 20 Oct.
