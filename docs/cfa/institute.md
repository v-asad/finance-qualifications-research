### Mission
The Code of Ethics preamble (2024) states the mission as: *"to continue to lead the investment profession globally by promoting the highest standards of ethics, education, and professional excellence for the ultimate benefit of society."* The current website does not present a separate "mission" or "vision" statement. It uses a "purpose" with four guiding principles: turn learning into leading, principles into practice, networks into connections, and complexity into clarity.

## 4. Leadership change (2026)

- **25 Feb 2026:** CFA Institute announced that **Margaret (Marg) Franklin, CFA**, President & CEO since 2019, would retire effective **1 March 2026**. She stays in an advisory role until 31 Dec 2026. No other reason was given beyond her own decision. ✅
- **Tricia Rothschild, CFA** became **Interim CEO**. She is a long-time Governor and was Vice Chair, previously President of Apex Fintech Solutions, and spent 25+ years at Morningstar (Chief Product Officer). ✅
- A board search committee chaired by Pamela Yang is running a search with an independent firm. **As of Oct 2026 no permanent CEO had been announced** (🟡, based on the Sep 2026 board release and the leadership page still showing "Interim").
- Franklin's tenure, per CFA Institute: CBT, Level III pathways, certificate expansion, a "global delivery model", and stronger financial foundations.
- Earlier CEOs (🟡, general knowledge): Paul Smith (2014–2019). John Rogers (2009–2014); the WSJ reported his 2014 departure followed a disclosed relationship.

## 6. Research & Policy Center (RPC), publications, standards

| Asset | Description |
|---|---|
| **Research & Policy Center** (launched 2023, rpc.cfainstitute.org) | Research and policy arm: reports, surveys, books, comment letters, and consultation responses. Themes: Capital Markets Resiliency; Technology/Big Data & AI; Future of the Profession; Sustainable Investing. MD: Mona Naqvi. |
| **Financial Analysts Journal (FAJ)** | Peer-reviewed quarterly published since 1945 (Vol. 82 in 2026). Graham and Dodd Awards are given annually. A 2026 editor appointment was announced (details not captured). |
| **CFA Institute Research Foundation** | Separate charity that publishes monographs, briefs and literature reviews (e.g., on the total portfolio approach). James R. Vertin Award (2026: Ludovic Phalippou). |
| **Enterprising Investor** / **Market Integrity Insights** | Blogs |
| **GIPS® Standards** | Global Investment Performance Standards: voluntary, ethical standards for calculating and presenting performance, based on fair representation and full disclosure. **2020 edition** (effective 1 Jan 2020 per general knowledge 🟡) has separate provisions for **Firms**, **Asset Owners** and **Verifiers**. **1,600+ organizations in ~53–54 markets** claim compliance, including all of the top 25 global managers. 2026 activity: Exposure Draft on Trade Errors (comments to 15 Dec 2026), guidance reconciling GIPS with the SEC Marketing Rule (updated Oct 2026), a Guide for Return Attribution Reporting, and the 30th GIPS Conference (10–11 Nov 2026). |
| **Asset Manager Code™** | Voluntary firm-level code. The RPC page lists six **General Principles of Conduct**: act professionally and ethically at all times; act for the benefit of clients; act with independence and objectivity; act with skill, competence and diligence; communicate with clients in a timely and accurate manner; uphold the rules governing capital markets. The Code's six operative **sections** (from general knowledge 🟡) are A. Loyalty to Clients; B. Investment Process and Actions; C. Trading; D. Risk Management, Compliance and Support; E. Performance and Valuation; F. Disclosures. Firms self-claim compliance through a notification form. 2nd ed. guidance, implementation guide June 2020. |
| **Global ESG Disclosure Standards for Investment Products** | Launched 2021 (voluntary) |
| **Future of Finance** | Long-running thought-leadership initiative (forums from ~2017). In 2026 it appears mainly as the report "Artificial Intelligence and the Future of Finance" and the "Future of the Profession" theme. Not obviously an active standalone brand any more. 🟡 |
| Other recurring outputs | Mercer/Marsh CFA Institute Global Pension Index; Graduate Outlook Survey; Investor Trust Study; CFA Institute Research Challenge (university competition; 2026 winner University of Waterloo); Investing Simulator Challenge (with AmplifyME); DEI/Inclusion Codes (US/Canada 2022, UK, Germany 2026) |
| Events | CFA Institute Live (annual conference, Chicago May 2025); THRIVE conference (Los Angeles, 2026) |

### Learning Ecosystem (LES)
The **CFA Institute Learning Ecosystem** is the official digital study platform included with registration. It contains the full curriculum, 1,000+ practice questions, **two official mock exams** (released about 60 days before the window), study planner, flashcards, and the **PSMs**. It is available on web, iOS and Android. A paid **Practice Pack** is an add-on (bundled in the "Premium" package). **Competitive note:** CFA Institute now bundles adaptive practice, mocks and flashcards, so third-party products need to beat the LES on question quality, analytics, explanations and AI tutoring. See `exam-format-and-logistics.md`.

### Layoffs and restructuring
- **2018:** ~31 roles cut. **2020:** ~140 positions cut (shift online). Both 🟡 press reports.
- **2024–2026:** No public layoff reports found. The search was limited, so this is ❓. The 2026 moves (new Institutional Solutions division, leadership churn, end of paid deferrals) point to reorientation rather than announced cuts.
- **June 2025:** The Manhattan DA indicted a former managing director (appointed 2016) for allegedly embezzling ~US$6m from two companies, one of them CFA Institute. 🟡
