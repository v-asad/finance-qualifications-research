## 1. What the CFA Program is

- A **three-exam, self-study graduate-level credential program** in investment management, run by **CFA Institute** (HQ Charlottesville, VA). The first exam was given on 15 Jun 1963 to 284 candidates.
- On completion, plus **4,000 hours / 36 months of qualifying work experience** and **regular membership** of CFA Institute, the person may use the **CFA® (Chartered Financial Analyst®)** designation.
- **Scale:**
  - "More than 200,000" charterholders in 160+ markets; 157 local societies; 8 offices.
  - ~155–165k exam sittings per year in 2022–2025; 2026 is on track to exceed that (see `statistics.md`).
- **Recognition:** Ecctis (UK) benchmarked the charter as comparable to a **Master's degree** in 12 markets covering over 80% of candidate volume (2022). Regulators and other bodies grant various exemptions; details are out of scope here.

## 2. Eligibility and enrollment

To enroll you register for the Level I exam. There has been **no separate enrollment fee since 29 Apr 2025** (it was US$350).

| Requirement | Current rule (verified 22 Oct 2026) |
|---|---|
| **Education / experience** (meet ONE) | **(1) Bachelor's degree** (or equivalent) completed; **or (2) undergraduate:** Level I window ≤ **23 months before graduation month**, Level II only when within **11 months** of graduation, Level III only after the degree is completed or with 4,000 hours of work experience; **or (3) 4,000 hours** of work and/or higher education over **≥ 3 sequential years**, achieved by Level I registration, with no overlapping dates and each year of higher education = 1,000 hours. Work need not be investment-related. Internships count only if paid; own or family business counts only if paid professional work. |
| **Passport** | A **valid international travel passport** (original, unexpired, machine-readable) is required to register and to sit. The name must match the CFA account exactly. |
| **Language** | Exams are only in English. |
| **Country** | Not resident in an OFAC-sanctioned or restricted jurisdiction |
| **Conduct** | Agree to the professional conduct statement and policies. Not currently suspended or prohibited. |
| **Proof** | CFA Institute may request evidence. Misrepresentation can lead to cancelled registration, voided results and a disciplinary process. |

**Change history:**
- The undergraduate window was extended by **one year on 1 Nov 2022**, allowing students with two years left to sit Level I. Previously candidates had to be in their final year.
- The US$350 enrollment fee was removed effective 29 Apr 2025 for Feb 2026+ exams.

## 3. Candidate journey (end to end)

```
Check eligibility ─► Create CFA Institute account ─► Register for Level I (choose window; pay early/standard fee)
   │                                                      │
   │                                                      ├─► Learning Ecosystem unlocks (curriculum, practice Qs, 2 mocks ~60 days pre-window, planner, flashcards)
   │                                                      ├─► Schedule Prometric appointment by scheduling deadline
   │                                                      └─► Complete ≥1 Practical Skills Module (PSM) before results day
   ▼
Sit Level I (Feb/May/Aug/Nov) ─► Result in ~5–7 wks ─► Pass ─► Register Level II (May/Aug/Nov)
                                                      └─► Fail ─► Retake (≤2 attempts/calendar yr, not consecutive windows, ≥6 months apart, ≤6 lifetime)
   ▼
Sit Level II ─► Result ~5–7 wks ─► Pass ─► Register Level III (Feb/Aug) + choose Pathway (PM / Private Markets / Private Wealth)
   ▼
Sit Level III ─► Result ~6–8 wks (essay grading) ─► Pass ─► "Level III passed" (congratulatory email, no score report)
   ▼
Accumulate 4,000 h qualifying work experience (≥36 months; before/during/after exams)
   ▼
Apply for Regular (Charterholder) membership: work-experience documentation + 2–3 professional references
   ▼
Charter awarded ─► Use "CFA" designation; annual membership dues; annual Professional Conduct Statement; continuing education (covered elsewhere)
```

### Key journey facts

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Recommended study | "**300 hours per level**" (CFA Institute). The 2019 survey average (cited by 300Hours) was L1 303h, L2 328h, L3 344h. CFA Institute warns candidates to "be wary of anybody giving a number of less than 300 hours." |
| Typical time to complete the three levels | "**three to four years**" (CFA Institute FAQ) |
| Fastest feasible | ~12–18 months of exams, given 4 L1 / 3 L2 / 2 L3 windows a year and results timing. Most candidates take longer because of failures (pass rates 40–50% per level). |
| Completion rate | "less than one in five candidates" who start ultimately earn the charter (CFA Institute CEO, 2019; no newer official figure) |
| Cost of exams (all three, first attempts) | **US$3,520 (all early) to US$4,570 (all standard)**, plus local taxes. Retakes, rescheduling (US$250), Practice Packs and prep courses are extra. |
| Attempts | Max 6 per level; max 2 per calendar year; not in consecutive windows or windows ≤ 6 months apart |
| Deferral | Paid deferral (US$449) existed through 2026 exams and is **eliminated from Feb 2027**. Emergency deferral is narrower (no pregnancy ground from 2027 unless life-threatening), with a US$100 fee. |

## 4. The three levels: cognitive focus and format

| | Level I | Level II | Level III |
|---|---|---|---|
| CFA Institute label | **Learn and describe**: "knowledge of key terms, concepts, and formulas" | **Analyze and evaluate**: "analyze situations and provide solutions" | **Integrate and apply**: "integrate concepts and apply knowledge to real-world scenarios" |
| Bloom-style framing (older CFA Institute wording, 2018–2019 releases) | Investment tools; **knowledge and comprehension** | **Application and analysis** of asset classes (equity, FI, derivatives, alternatives) | Portfolio management and wealth planning; **synthesis and evaluation** |
| Format | Stand-alone MCQs | Vignette item sets (MCQ) | Item sets + **constructed response (essays)**; core + 1 of 3 pathways |
| Session length | 2 × 135 min | 2 × 132 min | 2 × 132 min |
| Ethics weight | 10–15% | 10–15% | 10–15% |
| 10-yr avg pass rate (PDF, 2017–2026) | 40% | 45% | 50% |

## 5. Practical Skills Modules (PSMs)

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| What | Self-contained digital modules (videos, MCQs, guided practice, case studies). **Not graded and not tested on the exam.** |
| Requirement | **At least one PSM per level** to receive the exam result. Required at L1/L2 since 2024 and at **L3 since Feb 2025**. |
| Effort | 10–20 hours each |
| Deadline | Must be **completed by the results release date**, otherwise no result that day (the FAQ says the result is voided until completed) |
| Rules | The same PSM cannot count at two levels (e.g., Python I at L1 means a different module at L2). Failed candidates don't redo a PSM when retaking the same level. You can switch PSMs mid-way. |
| Access | Inside the Learning Ecosystem, any time after registration |

**PSM availability:**

| PSM | 2026 | 2027 |
|---|---|---|
| Financial Modeling (three-statement model) | L1 | L1 |
| Python Programming Fundamentals ("Python I") | L1, L2 | L1, L2 |
| Python, Data Science, and AI ("Python II") | L2 | L2 |
| Analyst Skills (renamed **Equity Analyst Skills** in 2027) | L2 | L2 |
| Portfolio Development and Construction (institutional portfolio, advanced Excel) | L3 | L3 |
| **L3 pathway PSM:** Practical Macro (PM) → **Macro Insights for Investing** in 2027 | L3 (PM pathway) | L3 |
| **L3 pathway PSM:** Due Diligence (Private Markets) | L3 (PMkts) | L3 |
| **L3 pathway PSM:** Managing Private Wealth Clients (Private Wealth) | L3 (PW) | L3 |

**Level III pathway PSM:**
- Since the Feb 2026 Level III window, each Level III candidate can access **one** pathway-specific PSM matching their pathway, launched 20 May 2025. Portfolio Development and Construction is also available.
- The 2027 Level III PSM table on CFA Institute's page is laid out ambiguously. Confirm eligibility rules in the PSM Policy before building features around it.

## 6. CFA Program Learning Ecosystem (LES) and official prep resources

| Resource | What it includes | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Learning Ecosystem (LES)** | Desktop and mobile app. Full curriculum for the registered level (and Level III pathway). **1,000+ practice questions**. **Two official mock exams released ~60 days before each window**. Study planner, progress tracker, adaptive recommendations, curriculum flashcards, and the PSMs. Access lasts while you are registered for an exam. LES progress is lost if you defer or re-register. | Included in the exam fee |
| **Practice Pack** (optional) | L1: +1,000 questions, 5 extra mocks + **1 Prometric-simulated mock**. L2: +750 questions, 3 mocks + 1 Prometric mock. **L3: +200 core + 100 pathway questions, 2 mocks (no Prometric mock)**. Written by the same item writers as the real exam. CFA Institute claims candidates who score 70% on at least one official mock are "nearly 2x more likely" to pass. | Paid add-on (price not captured) |
| **Print curriculum** | Six-volume print set for registered candidates | Paid; non-refundable |
| **Sample questions, Quick Start Guides, exam-day guides** | Public on cfainstitute.org (e.g., the Level III "All about the essay" guide) | Free |
| **Errata** | Curriculum corrections page and submission form | Free |

### Third-party prep providers

- CFA Institute runs a **Prep Provider Program**. Listed providers must:
  - use charterholder tutors;
  - update materials every year;
  - follow its guidelines;
  - **not claim pass rates**;
  - **not use the CFA Institute logo**.
- Only listed providers may use LOS text.
- From 2026, providers who have partnered for 5+ years are labelled **"Approved Prep Providers"**.
- Examples (third party; check the CFA Institute directory): Kaplan Schweser, AnalystPrep, UWorld (Wiley), Bloomberg Exam Prep, Mark Meldrum, IFT, 300Hours (content and community), plus many local providers in India and Pakistan.
- **Pathway coverage gaps:** for example, Kaplan did not offer the Private Markets pathway for 2026 per its brochure. This is a potential gap for new products.

### Candidate community and experience

- r/CFA (Reddit) and the 300Hours forums are the main English-language communities. Common themes from 300Hours articles and community discussion (not official):
  - Level II is often seen as the hardest "volume" exam.
  - The Level III essays are hard to self-grade.
  - Official CFA mocks are rated as most exam-like.
  - Candidates want more question banks and AI explanations.
  - Deferring hurts (official data confirms deferred candidates pass ~20+ pts lower).
- **Caveat:** Reddit was not searched directly in this pass because the web-search budget was exhausted. A dedicated community-sentiment research pass is recommended.

## 7. Scholarships (brief)

Three CFA Institute scholarship types are currently offered:

| Scholarship | For |
|---|---|
| **Access Scholarship** | Individuals who cannot afford the fees |
| **Student Scholarship** | Students at a CFA Institute **affiliated university** |
| **Professor Scholarship** | Full-time professors or administrators at qualified institutions |

**Eligibility rules (post-1 Sep 2024 policy):**
- You cannot apply while registered for an exam or waiting for results.
- Only one application may be open at a time.
- An unused award (not registering, scheduling or sitting) blocks future scholarships.
- Anyone awarded twice who fails the second attempt at the same level becomes ineligible.

**How awards work:**
- The discount is applied automatically at checkout.
- Awards cannot be extended past their expiry.

Older Women's Scholarships (1,000+ awarded in 2019) are no longer listed among current scholarship types. Some governments also subsidize fees, for example Singapore IBF, Hong Kong, Macau and Thailand schemes.

## 8. After Level III (brief; charter requirements are in a separate doc)

1. Passing Level III completes the exam requirement. Passers receive a congratulatory email, and lists of passing candidates go to local societies.
2. **Charter = all three exams passed (incl. PSMs) + 4,000 hours of qualifying investment-decision-related work over at least 36 months + Regular membership** of CFA Institute (and usually a local society) **+ 2–3 professional references.** Two references suffice if one is an active regular member of the local society applied to.
3. The pathway chosen at Level III is **not shown on the charter**.
4. **Ongoing obligations:** annual dues, an annual Professional Conduct Statement and professional learning.
5. Candidates who have taken any CFA Institute program are eligible for **Associate membership**. It does not grant use of the CFA designation.

## 9. Implications for an exam-prep product

- **Primary segments:** Level I (largest volume, about 85–100k sittings a year across 4 windows) and Level III (essay practice is under-served; pathway-specific Private Markets and Private Wealth content is thinly covered by prep providers).
- **Planner logic must encode:**
  - level-specific windows (L1 ×4, L2 ×3, L3 ×2);
  - the early-registration deadline (~7 months out);
  - attempt and spacing rules;
  - the PSM-before-results requirement;
  - no paid deferral from 2027.
- **Pricing context:** candidates already pay US$1,140–1,590 per exam, and prep courses typically cost hundreds to over US$1,000. India and Pakistan candidates are price-sensitive; CFA Institute offers EMI in India.
