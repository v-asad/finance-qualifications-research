# CFA Program — Level III

> **As of:** 22 Oct 2026. This covers the **2026 curriculum** (Feb and Aug 2026 exams) and the **2027 curriculum** (Feb 2027 exams onward; outlines were published by CFA Institute in 2026).
> **Primary sources:** CFA Institute Level III exam page, the Specialized Pathways page, and the official 2026 and 2027 Level III topic-outline PDFs (one PDF per pathway, each also containing the full core). Third-party sources are labelled.

---

## 1. What Level III tests

- **Cognitive focus:** CFA Institute labels the levels "Learn and describe" (L1), "Analyze and evaluate" (L2) and "**Integrate and apply**" (L3). Older CFA Institute wording describes Level III as requiring "competen[ce] in synthesis and evaluation while making investment decisions" with a focus on portfolio management and wealth planning.
- Candidates must **justify** answers by synthesizing vignette information. This is why constructed-response (essay) questions exist only at Level III.
- **Prerequisite:** pass Level II and meet enrollment requirements. Undergraduates must have completed their bachelor's degree, or have 4,000 hours of professional work experience, to sit Level III.
- **Study time:** CFA Institute says successful candidates report on average "over 300 hours" per level. A 2019 CFA Institute survey cited by 300Hours gives **~344 hours for Level III**. 300Hours itself recommends at least 350 hours including mocks.

## 2. Structure: Core + one Specialized Pathway (since Feb 2025)

Specialized pathways were announced on 20 Mar 2023. They were **first tested in the February 2025 Level III window**, and the Feb 2025 cohort was the first to choose one.

| Component | Exam weight | Who studies it |
|---|---|---|
| **Core** | **65–70%** | All Level III candidates |
| — Asset Allocation | 15–20% | |
| — Portfolio Construction | 15–20% | |
| — Performance Measurement | 5–10% | |
| — Derivatives and Risk Management | 10–15% | |
| — Ethical and Professional Standards | 10–15% | |
| **Pathway** (choose ONE: Portfolio Management, Private Markets, or Private Wealth) | **30–35%** | Only candidates who selected that pathway |

- **These weights are identical for the 2026 and 2027 curricula.** They appear on the Level III exam page and on the CBOK page labelled "as of 2027 curriculum".
- Pathway questions are "a mix of item-set and essay, just as for the common core."
- **Pathway descriptions (CFA Institute):**
  - *Portfolio Management:* "the more advanced, public markets focused portfolio management content of the pre-existing CFA Program". This is the traditional Level III.
  - *Private Markets:* "private markets investing primarily from the perspective of the General Partner (GP)". CFA Institute contrasts this with CAIA's LP/asset-owner focus.
  - *Private Wealth:* "global perspective on and universal principles for serving High Net Worth clients (worth US$5M+)". CFA Institute contrasts this with CFP, citing the CFA Program's ethics component and its depth across three levels.

## 5. Exam format

| Element | Level III detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery | Computer-based at Prometric test centres; **no online-proctored option** for the CFA Program | Exam pages; admission policy |
| Total time | **4 h 24 min of content**: two equal sessions of **2 h 12 min (132 min)**. Optional break between sessions. Appointment ~4.5 h overall including tutorial and survey. | Level III page; exam overview |
| Question types | **Item sets**: vignette plus 4 MCQs (3 options in standard MCQs), each MCQ worth 3 points. **Essay (constructed-response) sets**: vignette plus a variable number of items with variable, undisclosed points. | Level III page |
| Count | **11 item sets + 11 essay sets = 22 sets × 12 points each**. **20 scored**; 1 item set + 1 essay set are unscored trial sets (not identified). | Level III page |
| Points | Each set = 12 points, so 240 scored points. The 44 MCQs are each worth 3 points. | Level III page |
| Session mix | Each session has either 6 item sets + 5 essay sets, or 5 item sets + 6 essay sets. Topics are placed randomly; any topic can appear in either session or both. | Level III page |
| Vignette header | Each set opens with "TOPIC: … TOTAL POINT VALUE OF THIS QUESTION SET IS 12 POINTS" | Level III page |
| Language | English only | Policies |
| Negative marking | None ("no penalty for incorrect answers") | Level III page |

### 5a. Constructed-response (essay) mechanics

**Interface:**
- On a standard 17" monitor the case scenario sits side by side with the answer pane, which holds either MCQ options or a **text box**. On smaller screens the candidate toggles a "Case Scenario" icon.
- Keyboards are QWERTY.
- Numerical answers come in two forms. An **essay text box** accepts words and numbers. A **numerical-entry box** accepts only digits, commas and a decimal point, with no room to show work.
- The Level III page also mentions a **math editor** function for typing equations.

**Command words:**
- Command words are **bolded** in the question stem.
- 300Hours (third party) lists the command words used as: analyze, calculate, compare, contrast, define, demonstrate, describe, determine, discuss, estimate, evaluate, explain, formulate, identify, interpret, justify, recommend.

**Answer rules (official):**
- If multiple answers are requested, the stem says "Note: Each explanation should be in a separate paragraph". Bullet points are acceptable.
- **Only the number of answers requested is graded, in the order given. Extra answers are ignored.**
- Example: "Determine which bond… Justify" expects 2 answers (the choice plus a reason). "Determine the most appropriate index… Explain why each of the other two is less appropriate" expects 3.
- Occasionally an essay item uses an MCQ format with **more or fewer than 3 choices**.

**Calculations:**
- A correct number typed alone earns **full credit**; formulas are not required.
- Showing steps (math editor or typed variables) "may" earn **partial credit** if the final answer is wrong.

**Style:**
- From the official Quick Start Guide: "You do not get extra credit for producing a lot of words. Meandering prose can potentially hurt your score. Answer succinctly." Bullets are fine.
- From 300Hours (third party): content counts, not grammar. Abbreviations and arrows (↑/↓) are acceptable. Answering in the wrong place earns zero. It suggests budgeting time by points; with 22 sets × 12 points in 264 min, that is about 1 point per minute.

### 5b. How Level III is graded and scored

**MCQs:** machine-scored.

**Essays:** graded by people.
- Officially: "For Level III grading, **hundreds of CFA® charterholders** will participate in an **essay grading event**," followed by quality control and data-review steps.
- Reported by 300Hours (third party, Sep 2026), not officially detailed:
  - Graders work from guideline answers and grading keys.
  - Teams each grade one question or part.
  - Work is checked through tiers (junior, senior, "grader of graders").
  - Borderline papers are re-graded.
  - Grading has historically been held in Charlottesville, VA (reader comments say it moved to virtual from 2020).

**Passing standard:**
- The MPS is set by the CFA Institute Board of Governors after a **modified Angoff** standard-setting workshop (used since 1996). It is criterion-referenced, so there is no curve.
- Between workshops the MPS is carried forward by **equating**. It was reset in 2021 for the CBT format.
- The raw MPS is **not published**.

**Scale score (since Feb 2025):**
- Level III scale runs **3000–3900**, with the **MPS reported at 3600**.
- **Passing Level III candidates receive only a congratulatory email, with no score report.** Failing candidates get a score report showing overall scale score with confidence band, MPS line, group-average line, and topic-level performance against 50%/70% reference lines with confidence bands.

**Other scoring rules:**
- There is no per-topic minimum.
- The ethics adjustment applies near the MPS.
- No retabulation or rescoring is offered.

**Results timing:**
- Level III results arrive "within **6–8 weeks**" of taking the exam and completing the PSM. Level III takes longer than L1/L2 because of essay grading.
- Actual: Feb 2026 window → 7 Apr 2026; Aug 2026 window → 20 Oct 2026.
- **The PSM must be completed by the results release date**, or no result is released.

## 6. Level III Practical Skills Modules (PSMs)

- **Requirement:** Since Feb 2025, Level III candidates must complete **one PSM** to receive their result.
  - PSMs are not graded and not tested on the exam.
  - Each takes 10–20 hours. The original pathway announcement said 10–15 hours.
  - The same PSM cannot count at two levels.
  - A failed candidate need not redo a PSM when retaking the same level.
- **2026 Level III PSM options:**
  - **Portfolio Development and Construction**, available to all pathways. Uses advanced Excel to build and manage an institutional portfolio.
  - **Pathway-specific PSM** (one, matching the candidate's pathway), launched for the Feb 2026 Level III window:
    - Practical Macro (Portfolio Management). Designed by François Trahan; 15 units.
    - Due Diligence (Private Markets). Private equity transaction due diligence; 6 units.
    - Managing Private Wealth Clients (Private Wealth).
- **2027 table on the PSM page:** Level III lists Portfolio Development and Construction, Macro Insights for Investing, Due Diligence and Managing Private Wealth Clients. The pathway-specific list is Analyst Skills/Equity Analyst Skills, Practical Macro/Macro Insights (PM), Due Diligence (PMkts) and Managing Private Wealth Clients (PW).
  - The 26 May 2026 curriculum press release says "A new Level III PSM on Due Diligence is scheduled for release later in 2026."
  - **Exact 2027 Level III PSM eligibility rules are ambiguous on the page as published. Verify in the PSM Policy.**

## 9. Candidate-experience notes (third party; for product design)

These come from 300Hours articles (2026) and general candidate discussion. They are not official.

- **The essay half is the differentiator.** Candidates who are fluent in MCQs from L1/L2 often struggle to answer concisely and fully under time pressure. Practising timed, typed constructed responses is the main gap prep tools target.
- **Answer exactly what is asked:** the right number of reasons, in order, matched to the bold command word ("justify" means give a reason; "calculate" means a number).
- **Time management:** at 264 minutes for 22 sets, that is about 12 minutes per set. Do not overwrite early essays.
- **Official mocks:** each pathway includes 2 official mock exams in the Learning Ecosystem. The optional Practice Pack adds 2 Level III mocks plus 200 core and 100 pathway questions. There is **no Prometric-simulated mock for Level III**; that is offered for L1/L2 only.
- **Self-grading essays is hard.** A product opportunity is AI- or rubric-assisted grading of typed Level III constructed responses against guideline-answer style keys.
