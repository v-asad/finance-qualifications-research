## Executive summary: the CFA® charter

**What it is.** The Chartered Financial Analyst® designation is awarded by **CFA Institute** (Charlottesville, VA; not-for-profit; founded as ICFA in 1962; named CFA Institute since 2004). It is the most widely recognized global credential for investment analysis and portfolio management. There are **200,000+ charterholders** in **160+ markets**, served by **157 local societies**. CFA Institute has ~214k members, ~US$416m revenue, and ~200k CFA exam administrations a year (FY2025).

**How to earn it.**
1. Pass **Level I, II and III**. These are computer-based exams. Level III has three pathways: **Portfolio Management, Private Markets, Private Wealth**.
2. Complete at least **one Practical Skills Module per level**. Without it, the exam result is voided.
3. Accumulate **4,000 hours of qualified investment-decision-related work experience over at least 36 months**. It can be done before, during or after the exams.
4. Provide **2–3 professional references**, join CFA Institute as a **Charterholder member** (US$299/yr plus society dues), and file the **Professional Conduct Statement** every year.

**Ethics is core.** All candidates and members are bound by the **Code of Ethics and Standards of Professional Conduct**. The current version took effect 1 Jan 2024 and added **I(E) Competence**, a **V(B) cost and services disclosure** requirement, and **VI(A) "Avoid or Disclose Conflicts"**. Ethics weighs **15–20% at Level I in 2026**, falling to **10–15% from 2027**, and **10–15%** at Levels II and III. Prep providers report an **"ethics adjustment"** for borderline candidates (unconfirmed by a current official source).

**Cost and time (indicative).** Exam fees run **US$1,140–1,590 per level** (the enrollment fee was abolished in 2025), about **US$3.5k–4.6k in total**. The typical program takes 2.5–4 years at roughly 300 study hours per level. **Paid deferrals end from Feb 2027.**

**Key 2025–26 changes affecting our product:**
- 2027 curriculum: Level I Quant adds AI/LLM and data science content, Equity becomes more practical, and Level I ethics weight is cut.
- Level III pathways and PSMs are now standard.
- New membership tiers, including **Associate (US$149)** for anyone who has taken part in any CFA Institute program.
- CEO **Margaret Franklin retired** on 1 Mar 2026. **Tricia Rothschild** is interim CEO and the search is ongoing.
- New **Institutional Solutions** (B2B upskilling) division.
- CFA Institute's own **Learning Ecosystem** now bundles mocks, practice questions and flashcards. That raises the bar for third-party prep.

**Designation language (must follow in the product).** Write "Jane Doe, CFA" and "CFA® charterholder" or "CFA® Program candidate". Never write "a CFA", "CFAs", "CFA Level I", "CFA (expected)", or "CFA certified".
