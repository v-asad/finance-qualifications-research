# CFA Program — Exam Format & Logistics

> **As of:** 22 Oct 2026. Dates and fees for Nov 2026 – Nov 2027 come from CFA Institute's own exam-calendar data feed (`/api/1/cfa_exams`, which powers the "Dates & fees" interactive tool), retrieved 22 Oct 2026. Policies are quoted from CFA Institute policy pages, with each page's "updated" date noted where shown.

---

## 1. Delivery: computer-based testing at Prometric

- All levels have been **computer-based tests (CBT) at Prometric test centres** since 2021. Paper exams ended with Dec 2020 (Level I) and the June 2020 cancellation. CFA Institute announced the transition in 2020.
- There are **400+ test centres** worldwide. Aug 2026 had 519 venues in 412 cities across 116 markets for Level I, and 482 venues in 389 cities across 114 markets for Level III.
- **There is no online/remote proctoring for the CFA Program.** The admission policy covers only in-person test centres. CFA Institute's accommodations page mentions online proctoring only for the separate Sustainable Investing Certificate.
- Appointments are **first come, first served**. Each candidate books a date and time inside the level's window via Prometric after registering and paying, and must do so by the **scheduling deadline**.
- **Exam language:** English only.
- **Restricted countries:** CFA Institute applies OFAC sanctions compliance, so exams are unavailable in some countries.

## 2. Exam structure by level

| | Level I | Level II | Level III |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cognitive label (CFA Institute) | Learn and describe | Analyze and evaluate | Integrate and apply |
| Question format | Stand-alone MCQs (3 options) | Vignette-based item sets (MCQs) | Item sets + constructed-response (essay) sets; choice of pathway |
| Content sessions | 2 × **135 min** | 2 × **132 min** | 2 × **132 min** |
| Appointment length (incl. tutorial, break, survey) | ~4.5 h | ~4.5 h | ~4.5 h |
| Results timing (exam page) | within 5–7 weeks of exam + PSM | within 5–7 weeks | within **6–8 weeks** |
| PSM required for result | Yes (1) | Yes (1) | Yes (1) |

Level I and II item counts and details are covered in the companion L1/L2 docs. Level III: 11 item sets + 11 essay sets, each worth 12 points, with 20 scored (see `level-3.md`).

### 2a. Exam-day timeline

From CFA Institute's exam-day guide, which shows Level I timings:

| Segment | Time allotted |
|---|---|
| Introduction: Candidate Pledge & exam software tutorial | 19 min |
| Content Session 1 | 135 min (L1) / 132 min (L2, L3) |
| Break introduction / **optional break** | 31 min |
| Content Session 2 | 135 min (L1) / 132 min (L2, L3) |
| End-of-exam survey | (short; included in the ~4.5 h) |

- **Unscheduled breaks:** "candidates are allowed up to **two unscheduled breaks**; however, the testing time is **not paused**." Candidates may not leave the test centre.
- During breaks there is no access to phones or study materials. Food is kept in the personal-belongings area and is accessible during the optional scheduled break.
- **Partial attempts count:** completing only one session still counts toward the attempt limit.
- **Prometric tutorial:** CFA Institute links to a Prometric software tutorial so candidates can practise the interface in advance. Level II/III show the case and questions side by side on 17" monitors and in a toggled view on smaller screens.

- Registration opens about 9 months before the window.
- The early deadline is about 2 months after opening, roughly 7 months before the exam.
- Registration closes about 3 months before the exam.
- Level III windows are short (4 days) and sit just before or inside the Level I window.

### 4c. Payments and refunds

- **Payment methods:** Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Alipay; offline invoice (subject to an "invoice payment deadline"). India has an EMI option (2023 release). Third parties such as employers or prep providers may pay on a candidate's behalf, but candidates must register themselves.
- **Refund policy** (updated 26 Aug 2025): a full refund of registration fees and taxes is available **within 14 days** of an initial registration payment. After that, fees are non-refundable even for medical or extreme circumstances, and non-transferable. Missed exams forfeit fees.

## 5. Eligibility, attempts and deferrals

### 5a. Enrollment eligibility

Meet **one** of the following:
1. **Bachelor's degree** (or equivalent), completed.
2. **Undergraduate student:**
   - The Level I window must be **≤ 23 months before the graduation month**. Eligibility was extended by one year on 1 Nov 2022 to include students with two years left.
   - Level II requires being **within 11 months of graduation**.
   - Level III requires a completed bachelor's degree **or** 4,000 hours of professional work experience.
3. **4,000 hours of work and/or higher education** over **at least three sequential years**, achieved by Level I registration, with no overlapping dates.
   - Each year of higher education counts as 1,000 hours.
   - Work need not be investment-related.
   - Internships count only if paid.
   - Own or family business counts only if it is paid professional work.

**Additional requirements:**
- A **valid international travel passport** is required to register and to sit the exam (Identification Policy, updated 23 Apr 2026). It must be an original, unexpired, machine-readable document. The name must match the CFA account **exactly** or the fee is forfeited. The passport is checked at check-in for **both sessions**. Passport-assistance letters are available.
- English proficiency is expected (exam in English only).
- The candidate must live in a participating (non-sanctioned) country.
- The candidate must sign the **professional conduct** statement and admission policies.
- **Proof** of degree or experience may be requested. Misrepresentation can lead to cancellation, voided results and Professional Conduct Program action.

### 5b. Attempt limits (Exam Eligibility Policy)

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Max attempts per level | **6** (lifetime, across old and new accounts) |
| Max sittings per calendar year | **2** ("when applicable") |
| Spacing | **Not in consecutive windows** and not in windows **six months or less apart** (the "six-month rule") |
| Partial exams | Count as an attempt |
| After 6 failed attempts | No longer eligible to register for that level |

Older rules you may still see quoted online (e.g., "no lifetime limit") are outdated. The current policy is 6 attempts in total, 2 per calendar year, with 6-month spacing. Not verified: the exact date the 6-attempt cap took effect. The policy text also does not say explicitly whether the per-year and 6-month rules apply per level or across levels. They are commonly read as applying to the same level. Confirm with CFA Institute before encoding this in a planner.

**Fastest legal progression with Level III in Feb or Aug:**
- Example 1: L1 Feb 2027 → L2 Aug 2027 → L3 Feb 2028.
- Example 2: L1 Aug → L2 May (next year) → L3 Feb (following year).
- Results for each level must arrive before registration for the next level closes. Registration usually closes about 3 months before the window, so tight sequences can be blocked by results timing. Check the calendar.

### 5c. Deferrals

| Type | 2026 exams | Feb 2027 onward |
|---|---|---|
| **Paid deferral** | US$449; move appointment up to 12 months forward, subject to availability | **Eliminated** (announced 5 Mar 2026). Candidates who don't sit forfeit fees and must re-register at the prevailing rate. |
| **Emergency deferral** | Grounds: life-threatening illness (self or immediate family), death of immediate family, mandatory military deployment, pregnancy (one per level), or Prometric reschedule within 30 days of the window with no acceptable new slot. US$100 processing fee once approved (none for Prometric reschedules). Documentation is required; ~2 weeks to review; 7 days to pay. | Same, **except pregnancy is removed** unless there are life-threatening complications. Special exam-day arrangements are available instead. |
| Learning Ecosystem progress | Not retained after deferral | Same |
| Nov 2026 emergency deferral request window | Opens 6 Nov 2026 10:00 ET, closes 24 Nov 2026 23:59 ET | |

CFA Institute repeatedly notes that deferred candidates are "consistently the weakest performing group, with as low as 20% passing." In Aug 2026, deferred candidates passed at 29–31% versus 49–56% for first-time takers.

## 6. Calculators and permitted items

**Calculator policy (official): only two model families are allowed.**
- **Texas Instruments BA II Plus**, including the **BA II Plus Professional**.
- **Hewlett-Packard 12C**, including the **12C Platinum**, 25th and 30th anniversary editions, and 12C Prestige.

Calculator rules:
- Calculators are inspected before the exam.
- You cannot borrow a calculator at the centre or share one.
- Instruction manuals are not allowed. Calculator cases, keystroke cards and loose batteries are allowed, plus a small screwdriver for the BA II Plus battery.
- An unauthorized calculator means **results voided**, even if proctors missed it at check-in.
- The policy does not state a limit on the number of approved calculators. Not verified: whether a backup calculator is explicitly allowed. Confirm in the full policy text.

**Personal Belongings Policy (updated 9 Mar 2026): items allowed on the desk, in full view:**
- Approved calculator, calculator case, keystroke cards and loose batteries.
- Eyeglasses without a case.
- Passport.
- Water in a clear container with a lid and no label.
- Items on Prometric's pre-approved personal items list (e.g., some medical items).
- The test centre provides the writing materials. Personal pens and scratch paper are not allowed.

**Not allowed:**
- Phones, smartwatches, fitness trackers, any Bluetooth or recording devices.
- **Smart glasses** (explicitly banned).
- Any watch, clock or timer.
- Wallets and bags of any kind, including transparent bags and passport covers.
- Study materials.
- Food (kept in the belongings area).
- Jewellery other than wedding or engagement rings, ornate hair accessories, tie clips and cufflinks.

## 7. Exam-day rules

- **Arrival:** arrive **30 minutes early**. Late arrivals may be refused admission, which means fees are forfeited.
- **Check-in:**
  - Passport check before each session.
  - Security inspection of belongings, clothing and eyeglasses.
  - Metal-detector wand.
  - CCTV/DVR (and audio where legal) throughout the centre.
- **Candidate Pledge:** shown at the start of the exam within the 19-minute introduction. Candidates also agree to CFA Institute's candidate rules and policies at registration.
- **Confidentiality / NDA:**
  - Exam content is confidential. "Detailed exam scores and responses to exam questions are measurement information… owned solely by CFA Institute" and are never released.
  - Disclosing exam content after the exam (e.g., on Reddit or forums) breaches candidate rules and can be referred to CFA Institute's Professional Conduct Program.
  - Not verified in this pass: the exact text of the current Candidate Pledge or Candidate Responsibility Statement, and the specific sanctions, were not retrieved.
- **Disruptions:** keep working while waiting for a proctor; exams are designed to tolerate minor pauses. If you leave the test centre, no reschedule or deferral is approved.
- **No penalty for guessing** at any level.

## 8. Scoring and results

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Standard | **Criterion-referenced Minimum Passing Score (MPS)**. There is no curve and no fixed pass percentage. |
| Who sets it | The CFA Institute **Board of Governors**, on recommendation from a **modified Angoff** standard-setting workshop (used since 1996). The MPS is set after an administration whenever curriculum or population changes significantly (e.g., reset in 2021 for CBT). Otherwise it is maintained by **equating** across exam versions. |
| Published? | The **raw MPS is not published**. Candidates cannot learn their raw score or number correct. |
| Scale scores (since Feb 2025) | L1 1000–1900 (MPS **1600**); L2 2000–2900 (MPS **2600**); L3 3000–3900 (MPS **3600**). These are reporting scales; the pass decision still rests on the equated raw cut. |
| Score report contents | Pass/Do not pass, scale score with confidence interval, MPS line, and group-average line. Topic-level bars show % of available points with a confidence band, with reference lines at **50%** and **70%**, plus the topic group average (added Aug 2025). |
| 10th/90th percentile display | **Removed.** The old report showed 10th/90th percentile markers; CFA Institute removed them when scale scores were introduced (2025). |
| Level III passers | Receive **only a congratulatory email**, with no score report. Level III failers get the full report. |
| Topic minimums | None. Strong topics can offset weak ones. |
| Ethics adjustment | May move borderline candidates to either side of the MPS. |
| Unscored items | Trial questions are embedded and unidentified. At Level III, 1 item set + 1 essay set are unscored. |
| Retabulation / appeal | **Not available.** |
| Results delivery | Email notification and a results portal sign-in. Official window: **5–9 weeks after the exam window closes**. The exam page says 5–7 weeks (L1/L2) and 6–8 weeks (L3) "of taking the exam and completion of your PSM". Results are viewable for about 1 year; after that a pass/fail verification letter is available from the Account Profile. |
| PSM gate | The PSM must be completed **by results release day**, or no result is issued that day. The PSM FAQ also says an unfinished PSM means the result "will be voided". |
| Privacy | Individual results go only to the candidate. Lists of passers are shared with local societies for membership. |
| Pass-rate publication | Each window: a press release on results day (overall, first-time and deferred pass rates, candidate count, venues), plus updates to the pass-rate infographic PDF and the 1963–present historical PDF. See `statistics.md`. |
| Example 2026 release dates | L3 Feb 2026 → 7 Apr 2026. Aug 2026 window: L1 24 Sep, L2 30 Sep, L3 20 Oct 2026. |

## 9. Testing accommodations

- Disability accommodations are available in line with legal requirements. Candidates **must not schedule** their appointment until CFA Institute decides on the request.
- Requests are submitted via an online portal by the window's deadline (see §3b). Late requests are not reviewed.
- **Documentation:**
  - Medical Professional Questionnaire and personal statement.
  - Comprehensive diagnostic evaluation, within ~3 years, with a DSM-5 code where applicable.
  - Proof of prior accommodations (SAT/GRE/GMAT/university, etc.).
  - Everything in English, within 10 business days of the request.
- **Returning or deferred candidates** must confirm or re-request each time; accommodations do not transfer automatically.
- **Comfort requests** (e.g., nursing) are handled by email, case by case. Items on Prometric's pre-approved list need no request.
- QWERTY keyboards are standard. A different keyboard requires an accommodation.

## 10. Product implications

1. **Calendar-driven onboarding.** Each user's plan should anchor to a specific level and window, with early-deadline reminders (~7 months out), the registration close, the scheduling deadline, and a PSM-completion reminder before results day.
2. **Six-month rule and attempt-limit logic** in any "next attempt" planner: 2 per calendar year, no consecutive windows, ≥6 months apart, 6 lifetime attempts.
3. **Deferral is no longer a safety valve from 2027.** Messaging can lean into "sit when scheduled", which CFA Institute's own data supports.
4. **Mock UX** should mirror the Prometric layout: L2/L3 side-by-side vignettes, a L3 essay text box plus a numeric-entry box, 132/135-minute session timers, and optional-break logic.
5. **Calculator training** for the BA II Plus and HP 12C only.
