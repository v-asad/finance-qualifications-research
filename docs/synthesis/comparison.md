# CFA vs ACCA vs CA: Side-by-Side Comparison

> **As of:** 22 Oct 2026. This file compares the qualifications using the detailed docs in `docs/cfa`, `docs/acca` and `docs/ca`. Figures are summarised from those files; see them for sources and confidence markers.

## 1. What each qualification is for

| | **CFA Program** | **ACCA** | **CA (ICAP / ICAI / ICAEW)** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Domain | Investment analysis, valuation, portfolio management, wealth and private markets | Accounting, audit, tax, financial management, business strategy | Accounting, audit, tax, corporate law and reporting, with jurisdiction-specific content |
| Typical career | Asset management, equity/credit research, IB, wealth, risk, private markets | Finance functions, audit, advisory, tax (global, especially UK, Commonwealth and Asia) | Statutory audit/practice, CFO track, local tax and regulatory roles |
| Awarding body | CFA Institute (US not-for-profit) | ACCA (UK, Royal Charter) | National institutes under statute or charter |
| Geographic model | One global exam; no local variants | One global syllabus with **country variants** for Law and Tax | National. Tax and law are fully local |
| Employer training contract | No (work experience can be any time) | No (PER is self-arranged, supervisor sign-off) | **Yes**: ICAP 2.5–4 yrs, ICAI 2 yrs articleship, ICAEW 450 days over 3–5 yrs |
| Ethics | Code & Standards I(A)–VII(B), tested at every level | Ethics and Professional Skills Module + ethics in exams | Ethics modules and embedded ethics (ICAP Governance & Ethics module; ICAEW Sustainability & Ethics) |

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Exams | 3 levels | 13 exams (3 AK + 6 AS + 2 SP Essentials + 2 of 4 Options) | New levels; one Option; new codes (see `acca/future-qualification-2027.md`) | 18 papers: 3 PRC + 8 CAF + 6 CFAP + 1 SCS | 16 papers: Foundation 4, Intermediate 6, Final 6 | 14 exams: 6 Certificate, 6 Professional, 2 Advanced |
| Other components | ≥1 Practical Skills Module per level | EPSM; PER (36 months, 9 POs) | Essential Employability Modules | Hands-on courses; AI & Data Analytics and Governance & Ethics modules | SPOM Sets A–D; ICITSS; AICITSS | 30 units Specialised Learning; 25 skills |

## 3. Exam format and scoring

| | **CFA** | **ACCA** | **ICAP** | **ICAI** | **ICAEW** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Delivery | Prometric CBT, test centres only | CBE: on-demand (AK, LW) or 4 sessions/yr (Mar, Jun, Sep, Dec); some remote | Mix of CBE and paper by stage (see `ca/icap`) | Pen-and-paper (no official move to CBE found), with MCQ component | Certificate on-demand CBE; Professional/Advanced in sittings |
| Item types | L1: 180 MCQ (3 options). L2: 22 item sets × 4 MCQ. L3: 11 item sets + 11 essay sets | OT (MCQ, MR, number entry, drag-drop, hot spot…), OT cases, constructed response with spreadsheet/word-processor | Objective and long-form; case study | 30% MCQ in Intermediate papers and Final papers 1–5; 40% MCQ in Final P6; rest descriptive | OT at Certificate; scenario-based written, permitted texts; open-book cases |
| Pass standard | Criterion-referenced MPS (modified Angoff + equating), **not published**. Scale score reported (e.g. L1 pass = 1600) | **50%** per exam | Per-paper pass (see `ca/icap/training-and-exams.md`) | **40% per paper and 50% aggregate per group**; 60% in a paper gives exemption for 3 attempts | 55% Certificate & Professional; 50% Advanced |
| Recent pass rates | L1 ~40–45%, L2 ~45%, L3 ~47–50% (Aug 2026: 43 / 45 / 47%) | AK 64–89%, AS ~40–55%, SP ~38–53% | CAF ~37–40% (computed from counts) | Varies widely by group and session | See `ca/icaew/exams-fees-stats.md` |
| Attempt limits | Max 6 per level, ≤2 per calendar year, ≥6 months apart | None per exam, but 7-yr limit for Strategic Professional | QAT max 3; PRC within 15 months; CAF/CFAP/SCS no limit (older docs conflict; flagged) | Registration validity: Foundation 4 yrs, Intermediate 5 yrs, Final 10 yrs | 4 attempts per exam |

## 5. Overlap and dual-qualification paths

These matter for audience targeting, especially in Pakistan and India, where students often stack qualifications.

- **ACCA ↔ ICAP:** ICAP exempts fully qualified ACCA members from PRC and most of CAF (all except CAF-1, 5, 6, 8; the CAF-8 exemption needs ACCA AAA).
- **CFA ↔ ACCA / CA:** no formal exemptions found in either direction. ICAP treats CFA case by case.
- **Content overlap** (useful for shared content and cross-sell):

  | CFA topic | ACCA overlap | CA overlap |
  |---|---|---|
  | Financial Statement Analysis | FA, FR, SBR (IFRS) | ICAP CAF/CFAP reporting; ICAI Accounting / Financial Reporting |
  | Corporate Issuers / Corporate Finance | FM, AFM | ICAP CFAP-4 Strategic Business Finance; ICAI Financial Management |
  | Economics | FM (macro), BT | ICAI Foundation Business Economics |
  | Quantitative Methods | MA, PM (basic stats) | ICAI Foundation Quantitative Aptitude |
  | Ethics | EPSM, SBL ethics | Professional ethics modules |
  | Fixed income / derivatives / portfolio | AFM (partial) | Limited |

  Accounting standards material is the largest overlap. Note that CFA FSA is analyst-focused (IFRS **and** US GAAP comparisons) while ACCA and CA are preparer-focused (IFRS or local adaptations).

## 6. Difficulty profile, from the candidate's point of view

| | Biggest hurdle | Why |
|---|---|---|
| CFA L1 | Breadth (10 topics, ~93–102 modules) | Volume; first exposure to finance maths |
| CFA L2 | Depth: item-set vignettes | Long vignettes, multi-step calculations, FSA and equity valuation |
| CFA L3 | Essay (constructed response) answers | Hard to self-grade; "justify" style answers |
| ACCA | PM, AA, FM, AAA, APM, SBR | Applied written answers; time pressure; professional-skills marks |
| ICAP | CAF and CFAP pass rates | Long technical papers; local law and tax |
| ICAI | Group aggregate rules, Final volume | 40%/50% aggregate rules; huge candidate volumes |
| ICAEW | Advanced case studies | Integrated, open-book, time-pressured |
