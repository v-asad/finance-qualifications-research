# ICAS — CA Qualification (Institute of Chartered Accountants of Scotland)

> **As of:** 22 Oct 2026. The main source is ICAS's *Guide to the ICAS syllabus 2026* (v1.3, May 2026) and its 2026–28 term planner, with icas.com pages for fees, routes and results. Some ICAS help-centre pages blocked automated fetching, so the items taken from them are marked **[snippet]**. Anything still unconfirmed is marked **[unverified]**.

## 1. Key change: TC / TPS / TPE has been retired

The old structure was:
- **Test of Competence (TC)**
- **Test of Professional Skills (TPS)**
- **Test of Professional Expertise (TPE)**

This was **replaced by the "CA24" syllabus**, which launched on 22 March 2024. It has three levels: **Knowledge → Skills → Integration**.

- All three levels were running during 2024.
- The old curriculum was phased out by 2025. The 2025 fee schedule still had a line for TC/TPS/TPE resits; the 2026 schedule does not.

## 2. Qualification at a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Designation | **CA** (ICAS says the title is exclusive to ICAS members in the UK and EU). ICAS received its Royal Charter in 1854 and was the first body to use "Chartered Accountant". |
| Level | SCQF Level 11 (master's level) / EQF 7; 485 SCQF credit points |
| Requirements to qualify | 11 core technical courses + 1 elective + the Professional Ethics course, all assessments passed, and **450 days of Relevant Practical Experience (RPE)** under a training agreement with an Authorised Training Office (ATO) |
| Time to qualify | Normally **3–5 years**. The school-leaver training contract is at least 3 years. |
| Cross-cutting themes | Ethics and professional scepticism, sustainability, technology. Teaching uses cloud accounting software and Power BI. |
| Pass mark | **50% for every assessment** |
| Attempts | 4 per assessment, plus a possible extra attempt under Section 10 of the Student Handbook. Employers may set lower limits. **[snippet]** |
| Terms | Knowledge and Skills: 4 terms a year of 12 weeks each. Integration: 3 terms a year of 15 weeks each (from 2026). |
| Results turnaround | About 2 weeks for Knowledge and Skills; about 7 weeks for Integration (from the planner dates) |

**Progression rules:**
- Students can go level by level or follow a subject pathway (for example a taxation route).
- Before Integration, a student must complete all Knowledge courses, all Skills core courses and one elective.

**Delivery:**
- Remote finals are online-proctored: one screen, ID check, onboarding test.
- Integration can be taken at a centre or remotely.
- Pre-seen analysis and templates are uploaded to a "locker" on the Advantage platform.
- Pre-reads are released at 08:00 on exam day for Skills, and at 08:00 on the preceding Friday for Integration.
- **[unverified]** Whether the Knowledge exams are truly on-demand. The planner shows fixed final dates but includes a note about "on demand".

**Question types and weightings:**
- Knowledge exams use objective tests; Reporting & Performance 1 adds a constructed financial statements question.
- Skills and Integration exams use scenario-based written answers, modelling and coursework.
- **ICAS does not publish percentage topic weightings.** Instead the syllabus is set out as 7 programme learning outcomes, then syllabus, module and performance-indicator outcomes. Each outcome is tagged with a cognitive level: Knowledge, Comprehension, Application, Analysis, Synthesis or Evaluation.
- For a content taxonomy, tag items by outcome and cognitive level rather than by weight.
- The 2026 syllabus adds IFRS 18 and UK sustainability-reporting developments.

## 6. Exemptions, practical experience and routes

**Exemptions:**
- ICAS-accredited degrees can give up to **5 Knowledge-level exemptions** (£115 each in 2026).
- Heriot-Watt's MA (Hons) Professional Accountancy, starting Sep 2026 in Edinburgh and Dubai, gives exemption from **both Knowledge and Skills**. This is the first time ICAS has exempted Skills courses.

**Practical experience:**
- **450 days of RPE**, logged against **39 core competencies** in 6 areas:
  - ethics and integrity
  - communication
  - teamwork and leadership
  - personal effectiveness
  - problem solving and decision making
  - technical competence
- One third-party summary cited "750 days". Official pages say 450, so treat 750 as outdated or wrong.

**Routes:**
- **Graduate** (any degree; about 3 years)
- **School leaver** (strong Highers or A Levels; usually 3–5 years)
- **RGU Graduate Apprenticeship** (Scotland; 5 years funded; BA (Hons) in years 1–3, then CA in years 4–5)
- **Level 7 apprenticeship in England**, assessed through EPA1 and EPA2 (details **[unverified]**)
- **CA–CTA Joint Programme** (with CIOT)
- **Entry for members of other bodies:**
  - reciprocal membership with ICAEW, CAI, CPA Canada, CA ANZ, SAICA, HKICPA and ISCA, with no further exams
  - PAEE route
  - ACCA/CIPFA pathways
  - special entrant route

No ICAS programme called "CA Accelerate" was found.

## 9. Product notes

- **Knowledge level:** an objective-test bank works here. Reporting & Performance 1 also needs a financial-statements builder.
- **Skills level:**
  - scenario-based written exams
  - **a spreadsheet modelling coursework** (Corporate Finance & Modelling)
  - **data/BI coursework** (Data, Risk & Technology; Data Analytics & Insights)
- **Integration level:** a pre-seen case study (open book, 3h) and a short open-book ethics case (2h).
- **Exam calendar:** the term-based calendar fits a "term plan" progress tracker well.
