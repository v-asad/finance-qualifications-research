## 1. Key point: the module list in the brief is the old program

These modules belong to the **pre-2022 CA Program**:
- Financial Accounting & Reporting (FAR)
- Audit & Assurance (AAA)
- Taxation (TAX)
- Management Accounting & Applied Finance (MAAF)
- Capstone (CAP)

The old program used ~3.5-hour open-book exams.

- CA ANZ announced a **new CA Program** in October 2021 ("11 October 2021 – The New CA Program"). It replaced the old structure from about 2022, with subject codes CACC15xx. The exact transition term is **[unverified]**.
- "Ethics and Business Application (EBA)" does not appear in any current source found.
- A **further revision took effect from Term 3 2026**:
  - New subject versions carry CACC25xx codes.
  - **Sustainability and AI are integrated in every subject.**
  - AI-assisted ("human-in-the-loop") marking was introduced in one subject (Accountants Daily, Oct 2026).
- Reported renames (per Acuity and search extracts, **[unverified]** on the official site):
  - *Ethics and Business* → **Ethics and Sustainability**
  - *Audit and Risk* → **Audit and Analytics**
  - Core 2 *Risk and Technology* → **Risk, Technology and Artificial Intelligence**. This one is inferred from the subject names; confirm it.
- IFAC's revised International Education Standards (IES 2 and 3, with sustainability content) take effect 1 Jul 2026. CA ANZ is expected to align with them.

## 2. Qualification at a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Designation | **CA** (Chartered Accountant, CA ANZ) |
| Components | (1) **Graduate Diploma of Chartered Accounting (GradDipCA)**, (2) **3 years of Mentored Practical Experience (MPE)**, (3) entry requirements met through an accredited degree or CA Foundations |
| GradDipCA | 9 subjects (7 core + 2 electives) = **120 credit points**. CA ANZ is a TEQSA-registered higher-education provider. Australian students can use FEE-HELP (per Wikipedia). |
| Terms | **4 terms a year (T1–T4)** |
| Workload | About 10 directed hours + 5 personal-study hours per week. CA ANZ recommends at most 2 subjects per term, plus Ethics in the first term. |
| Minimum duration | About 1 year full-time for the GradDipCA. The CA overall takes at least 3 years because of the MPE. |
| Time limit | GradDipCA and MPE must both be completed within a **maximum 8-year provisional membership period** (Regulation CR5 s5.11). The same window governs when subject credit expires. |

**General assessment model:**
- Only **FAR, Tax, Audit and ICAP** have final exams.
- Exams are **online and invigilated**, taken on the candidate's own computer through the **Janison** platform.
- **Current exams are not described as open book.** Open book was a feature of the legacy program.
- Every subject has an **early low-stakes assessment (~10%)** to give feedback.
- Tasks are built on business case studies and scenarios.
- To pass, a candidate must:
  - complete all assessments;
  - pass the final assessment, where it is a hurdle;
  - pass the subject overall.
- Results are given as a grade and a mark.
- **Pass mark:** published per term in a results dashboard rather than fixed. The commonly cited "scaled 50/100" comes from the legacy program and is **[unverified]** for the current program.
- **Awards:** a Certificate of Merit (top 5% in a subject) and Top of Subject.
- From T4 2026, core-subject workshops move to a new format.

**Implications for an exam-prep product:**
- The main mock-exam engine targets should be **FAR (2.5h)**, **Tax (2h scenario-based)**, **Audit and Analytics** and the **ICAP capstone**.
- Other subjects depend on assignments and coursework. Support for these means writing feedback and case-analysis practice, not timed mocks.
- The early 10% online quizzes, including Tax's 60-minute MCQ, suit an objective-test practice bank.
- Tax content needs separate AU and NZ variants.

## 5. Entry: accredited degrees, CA Foundations, CA Fundamentals

**Accredited degree.** Covers the **9 required competency areas**:
- Accounting Fundamentals
- Audit & Assurance
- Business Law
- Economics
- Financial Accounting & Reporting
- Finance
- ICT
- Management Accounting
- Taxation

**Non-accredited degree.** Must be assessed as at least equivalent to an AU/NZ bachelor's degree. Gaps are filled through CA Foundations or an accredited provider.

**CA Foundations** (launched July 2025):
- **What it is:** a bridging program with **6 subjects**; candidates take only the ones that fill their gaps.
- **Eligibility:** non-accredited degree holders; accredited degrees with gaps; TAFE/polytechnic study; 3+ years post-school work; Accounting Technicians.
- **Enrolment and exams:** enrol at any time; the final exam must be taken within 12 months; **6 exam periods a year**.
- **Subjects:** confirmed so far are Tax and Business Law (AU and NZ versions) and Audit and Governance. New versions were due from 7, 14 and 21 Sep 2026. The full list of 6 is **[unverified]**.
- **Fees (AU, incl. GST):** full subject A$736 (6 months' access); exam only A$283; optional workshop A$227. The period these apply to is **[unverified]**.
- **Exam format:** **[unverified]**. Older documents mention 80 MCQs over 3–4 hours.
- **Uptake:** 4,355 subject enrolments in FY26 (121% of budget).

**CA Fundamentals:**
- A 10-month "earn and learn" pathway for school leavers aged 18+, formally launched in February 2026.
- Costs A$2,209 incl. GST; intakes in February and June.
- More than 60 hiring-partner employers.

## 6. Mentored Practical Experience (MPE)

- **Duration:** **3 years** of approved employment, totalling **at least 500 FTE days**, in a relevant accounting role. A 2021 document also set a minimum of 17.5 hours/week; that is not on the current page.
- **Prior experience:** up to **1 year** of full-time prior experience can be claimed.
- **Employer types:**
  - **Approved Training Employer (ATE):** offers work and training that meet CA ANZ requirements.
  - **Recognised Training Employer (RTE):** runs an in-house program approved by CA ANZ, and must already be an ATE.
- **Mentor:**
  - must be a full CA ANZ member, or a full member of a GAA body or other body with a full recognition agreement;
  - ideally at the same employer, though an external mentor is allowed;
  - must meet the candidate **every 6 months**.
- **Competency framework:** the current MPE competency framework was not captured **[unverified]**.

## 7. Size, statistics and international links

- **Members:** **140,590** across 17 countries (Annual Report 2025, year to 30 Jun 2025). The 2026 annual report total was not retrieved.
- **FY26 intake:**
  - 6,348 new provisional members (FY25: 5,663);
  - 2,987 members admitted through alternative pathways.
- **Pass rates:** **not published** (none found).
- **History:** formed on 31 Dec 2014 by the merger of ICAA (Australia) and NZICA (New Zealand).
- **Global Accounting Alliance (GAA) member.** Reciprocal membership arrangements cover AICPA, ICAEW, CPA Canada, ICAS, CAI (requires 2 years post-qualification experience), HKICPA and SAICA. JICPA and IDW are not covered.
- **Other overseas bodies:**
  - Members of ICAI, ICAP, ICAN, ICAB and CA Sri Lanka use the **International Pathway Program** (5+ years post-qualification experience, including a senior role).
  - ISCA and ICAZ members use special admission.
- **Chartered Accountants Worldwide member.** CA ANZ's CEO chairs CAW (per Wikipedia).
- **Partner program:** MICPA × CA ANZ in Malaysia uses the same subject set, with Audit & Risk and Taxation (MY).
