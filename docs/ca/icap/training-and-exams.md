# ICAP: Training, Exams, Fees, Pass Rates and Membership

**As of: 2026-10-22**

> All rules here follow the **Education and Training Scheme 2025**, summarised in [README.md](./README.md). Legacy terms such as AFC, MSA-1/MSA-2, and the old ITC/CSS-style courses come from earlier schemes. Where the official sources disagree, the conflict is flagged.

---

## 1. Practical training (training contract)

### 1a. Training options (Scheme 2025; FAQ 9.1 and Directive 1.01)

| Base qualification | Option | Duration | Exams during training? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CAF passed or exempt** (incl. HSSC/A-Level route) | Standard | **3.5 years** | Yes. CFAP and SCS can be taken during training. |
| | Uninterrupted | **2.5 years** | **No.** No CFAP or SCS exams and no study leave during training. CFAP starts after training ends. |
| | Deferred start | **3 years** | You take CFAP first. Training starts after passing at least 4 CFAP papers or 2 years after passing CAF, whichever comes first. Study leave is reduced. |
| **16-yr relevant degree from SDAI** | Uninterrupted | 2.5 years | No |
| | Deferred start | 3 years | After 4 CFAP papers or 2 years |
| | Standard | 3 years (stipend table) | Yes |
| **16-yr from RDAI, or 4-yr degree majoring in accounting/finance** | Uninterrupted | 2.5 years, after passing CAF | No |
| | Deferred start | 3 years | After 4 CFAP papers or 2 years post-CAF |
| | Standard | 3 years | Yes, including CAF during training |
| **Other 16-yr graduates and 14-yr associate degree holders** | Uninterrupted | 2.5 years, after passing CAF | No |
| | Deferred start | 3 years | After 4 CFAP papers or 2 years post-CAF |
| | Standard | **4 years** | Yes, including CAF, CFAP and SCS during training |

- **The choice is irrevocable.** ICAP states that once you pick an option, you cannot change it.
- **When you can start training:**
  - CAF-route students start after passing all of CAF and completing the PPE and MS Office hands-on courses.
  - Graduates (14 or 16 years) and ICMAP passers can register as trainees straight away. Non-relevant associate degree holders must pass PRC (or get exempted) first.
  - Training begins with a **2-month probation**. The training contract must be sent for registration within one month after probation ends.
- **CFAP eligibility during training:** you need **12 months of training** completed by the 1st of the exam month. Under the deferred-start option, the remaining CFAP papers and SCS are taken after 12 months of training.
- **Exam sessions per year for trainees:** two per **calendar year**, as approved by the Member Responsible for Students (MRS) (FAQ 4.10).
- **Breaks in training:**
  - Female trainees may take up to **1 year** for marriage or maternity.
  - Other trainees may take up to **6 months**.
- **Transfers:** you can move to another Training Organization (TO) with mutual consent. The current TO must issue an NOC within 30 working days, and a transfer fee applies.
- **Workplace assessment:** a designated person at the TO must assess the trainee's output and performance.

### 1b. Training Organization types (practice vs industry)

The categories come from the ICAP fee schedule.

| Type | Meaning | Fee to be authorised (PKR, from 1 Jul 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| **TOIP**: Training Organization Inside Practice | CA firms (audit practice) | 71,500 |
| **TOOP**: Training Organization Outside Practice | **Industry or corporate** employers authorised to train CA students. This is the "industry training" route. | 275,000 |
| **OTOIP**: Overseas TO Inside Practice | Overseas CA practice offices | 71,500 or foreign-currency equivalent |
| **TOPS**: Public Sector Training Organization | Government or public-sector bodies | 275,000 |

- ICAP keeps a page on "Training outside practice" (TOOP) and runs a **Trainee Induction Portal** for placements.
- ICAP's own term is "Training Organization". "Approved Training Organization (ATO)" is not the term ICAP uses.
- **Note:** to practise as a CA, a member needs at least **2 years of training in practice**, per the reciprocal-membership rules and long-standing practice. The exact rule for TOOP-trained members who later want a Certificate of Practice was **not verified** in the Scheme 2025 directive.

## 2. Exam sessions and format

| Stage | Frequency (FAQ 7.15 and Exam Schedule 2027) | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **QAT** | Mar, Jun, Sep, Nov, Dec | Computer-based test (CBT) |
| **PRC** | Every two months: Jan, Mar, May, Jul, Sep, Nov | Centre-based. Calculators allowed. PRC-3 is a 120-minute, 100-mark paper. |
| **CAF / GCC** | Twice a year. Spring was March and Autumn was September through 2026. **For 2027 ICAP has moved CAF to 1–10 Feb and 2–11 Aug.** | 3 h 15 min including 15 min reading time. Starts 9:00 am. Remote proctoring has been offered for CAF. **GCC-1 is computer-based**, held right after CAF. |
| **CFAP** | **Quarterly from Summer 2026**: Spring (Mar), Summer (Jun), Autumn (Sep), Winter (Dec) | Computer-based environment. Remote proctoring is also offered. Some papers are open-book: CFAP-1 allows the IFRS handbook and Code of Ethics; CFAP-2 allows corporate laws and regulations; CFAP-5 tax papers allow specified bare acts. |
| **SCS** | Twice a year: Summer (Jun) and Winter (Dec) | Open book. Pre-seen material is released 20–30 days before the exam. Computer-based. |
| **Hands-on Courses / Integrated Modules** | Own schedules (e.g. MOB and PPE monthly or periodic; G&E quarterly) | Assessed by ICAP or third parties. English Communication Skills is assessed by the British Council. |

- **Remote proctored exams** are open to candidates abroad (FAQ 7.10).
- **"CBE on demand" does not exist.** Every stage, including QAT and PRC, runs on fixed scheduled windows. No on-demand CBE model was found.

## 3. Passing standard, attempts and time limits

| Rule | Scheme 2025 (Directive 1.01, current version) |
|---|---|
| Pass mark | **50% in each paper** |
| QAT | **Maximum 3 attempts**. You must register for PRC within 12 months of passing. |
| PRC | All papers must be passed within **15 months** of the first attempt |
| CAF / GCC | **No limit on attempts** |
| CFAP | **No limit on attempts** |
| SCS | **No limit on attempts**. Eligible once all CFAP papers are passed, or with only 1 left. |
| Counting an attempt | An attempt counts once an admit card is issued **and** the candidate signs the attendance sheet |
| Max papers per sitting | CAF: 4. PRC, GCC and CFAP: no maximum. |
| Legacy MSA-1 / SPM | Final sittings were **Summer 2026 and Winter 2026** only. After that, CFAP-3 Sustainability Reporting and Assurance is required instead. |

**Conflicts between ICAP documents. Verify these with ICAP:**
- The 2024 "Principles and Structure" booklet set a **5-year CAF limit** (plus 1 year if 2 or fewer papers are left), a **4-year GCC limit** and a **10-year CFAP limit**.
- The FAQ PDF says CAF and GCC have **no time limit**. It says CFAP requires **4 papers within 10 years** of starting training, or of the first CFAP attempt under the deferred option, with no limit after that.
- The current Directive 1.01 says there is **no limitation of attempts** for CAF, GCC, CFAP or SCS. It does not mention a period limit for these stages.
- An Indian site (Taxscan, 26 Apr 2026) reported that ICAP's **415th Council meeting (23–24 Apr 2026)** removed attempt restrictions "effective immediately" and re-admitted previously disqualified candidates. The report's wording was ambiguous ("CA Final (CAF)"), and the full text was paywalled.
- **Best reading:** for CAF, CFAP and SCS there is currently no attempt cap. Whether the CFAP "4 papers in 10 years" period benchmark survives is **uncertain**.

## 6. Requirements to qualify (become an ACA)

1. Pass PRC or be exempt. QAT is required first if your HSSC or A-Level score was 50–59%.
2. Pass CAF (or GCC), or be exempt.
3. Complete the hands-on courses: English Communication Skills, PPE, and MS Office for Business.
4. Pass all **6 CFAP** papers.
5. Complete the integrated modules: **AI and Data Analytics**, and **Governance and Ethics**.
6. Pass the **Strategic Case Study**.
7. Complete the **practical training** (2.5, 3, 3.5 or 4 years) and get the training completion certificate (Form S).
8. Apply for membership online on Form A, with your certificates. On admission you become an **ACA**.

**Transition rule for older trainees:** those who joined training before 1 March 2022 are exempt from the hands-on courses and integrated modules.

## 7. Membership designations

| Designation | Basis (CA Ordinance 1961, s.5, as amended 1983) |
|---|---|
| **ACA** (Associate) | Granted when your name is entered in the Register on admission |
| **FCA** (Fellow) | An associate who has **5 years in practice**, or **10 years of membership**, can transfer to fellow on paying the fee. Two non-practising years count as one year of practice. |
| **Certificate of Practice (CoP)** | Needed to practise, for example as auditor of a public company. Renewed annually. |
| Certified Business Accountant (CBA) | A separate ICAP scheme. Certification fee PKR 3,500; annual fee PKR 8,600. This is **not** the CA designation. |

## 8. CPD for members

- Members must file an **annual CPD declaration** online when they renew membership.
- CPD is run through a CPD portal and calendar. Regional committees (SRC, NRC), the PAIB and SMP committees, and DAAB offer programmes.
- **Hour requirements were not verified.** The CPD compliance pages returned only navigation, and the CPD directive was not retrieved. Get the current verifiable and non-verifiable hour requirements from the ICAP CPD directive in Members' Handbook Part IV. Any specific figure would be a guess and is not given here.

## 9. Mandatory non-exam components

| Scheme 2025 component | Replaces (Scheme 2021) | When | Recommended hours |
|---|---|---|---|
| English Communication Skills (British Council assessed) | PRC-1 Business Writing and Comprehension Skills | Before training or CFAP | n/a |
| Presentation and Personal Effectiveness (PPE) | PPE | Before training or CFAP | 70 |
| MS Office for Business (Excel, Word and PowerPoint, assessed separately) | MS Office | Before training or CFAP | 70 |
| AI and Data Analytics (integrated module) | Data Analytics and Fintech | After CAF and before SCS. Advised before CFAP-4 and CFAP-6. | 90 |
| Governance and Ethics (integrated module) | PVEA (Professional Values, Ethics and Attitude) | Before SCS. Advised in year 1 of training. | 40 |

- **Legacy "ITC / CSS":** older ICAP schemes had separate IT training and communication/soft-skills requirements. Under Scheme 2025 the components above are the current equivalents.
- **Exemptions:** possible with at least 70% syllabus match and at least 60% marks. Technology courses must have been passed within the last 3 years. Four-year graduates are exempt from MS Office and PPE.
