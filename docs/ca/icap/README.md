# ICAP: Institute of Chartered Accountants of Pakistan (CA Pakistan)

**As of: 2026-10-22**

> Scope: this is an overview. Paper-by-paper syllabus detail belongs in a separate curriculum file. Training, exams, fees and pass rates are in [training-and-exams.md](./training-and-exams.md).
>
> **Important update:** ICAP replaced its 2021 scheme with the **Education and Training Scheme 2025**. Directive 1.01 says it is effective 16 July 2025, but the ICAP "Principles and Structure" booklet says **16 January 2026** (with training rules effective at once). Several terms you may see in older guides are now **legacy**:
> - **AFC** (Assessment of Fundamental Competencies) is from the 2013 scheme. PRC replaced it in 2021, and ICAP's last AFC statistics are from 2022.
> - **MSA-1 / MSA-2** (Multi-Subject Assessments) have been replaced by the **Strategic Case Study (SCS)**.
> - **ITC / CSS-style soft-skill courses** have been replaced by "Hands-on Courses" and "Integrated Modules".
>
> Under Scheme 2025 the stages are **QAT, PRC, CAF (or GCC), CFAP, then SCS**, plus practical training.

### 2b. Arrangements with other bodies

| Counterparty | Nature of arrangement | Key terms (as stated by ICAP) |
|---|---|---|
| **ICAEW** (UK) | Top-Up Programme, plus Pathways to Membership | ICAP members qualified under the **Scheme 2021 syllabi** are exempt from ICAEW Certificate level, Professional level, two Advanced-level papers and training. | **CA ANZ** | MoU signed **2018** | ICAP members with **5+ years post-membership experience** who live in Australia or New Zealand can complete the CA ANZ **International Pathway Programme** to become eligible. |
| **CPA Canada** | Revised MoU | ICAP members must pass the **CFE** and meet Canadian practical-experience requirements. The experience review is waived for those with a recognised degree plus 2 years post-designation experience, or with no degree plus 5 years. |
| **SOCPA** (Saudi Arabia) | MoU | ICAP members **and CAF students** are offered SOCPA associate membership. |
| **ISCA** (Singapore) | Recognition | ICAP members who qualified by the normal route can apply for **Associate (ISCA)**. |
| **EAAA** (UAE) | Recognition | Fellows of IFAC member bodies, including ICAP, can obtain EAAA fellowship, subject to EAAA membership and exams. |
| **IIA** | ICAP is listed as an "Approved Accounting Body" | ICAP members can take the **CIA Challenge Exam**, with a 20% fee discount for Pakistan residents until 31 Dec 2026. |
| **AICPA / US CPA** | **No formal arrangement** | NASBA typically evaluates CA Pakistan as about **120–126 US credit hours**. Members follow the standard state-board route. |
| **ECCTIS** (UK benchmarking) | Benchmarking, not membership | **CAF = RQF Level 5** and **CA = RQF Level 7** (also mapped to QFEmirates). Based on Scheme 2021 and valid for 5 years. |

**Reverse direction (other bodies' members joining ICAP):** ICAP's "How to become a member" page lists members of **ICAEW, Chartered Accountants Ireland, ICAS, CA ANZ and CPA Canada** as eligible to apply for ICAP membership. To practise in Pakistan they must pass ICAP's Advanced Taxation and Corporate Laws papers and meet a requirement of two years' training in practice.

**Not verified / possibly lapsed:**
- No current ICAP-to-**ICAS** outbound pathway appears on ICAP's Recognitions page. ICAS appears only in the reverse-direction list above.
- No current ICAP-to-**CPA Australia** arrangement appears on the Recognitions page.
- Older MoUs (for example with ICAI or CIMA) were not confirmed.

Check directly with the counterpart body before relying on any of these.

## 3. Routes of entry (Scheme 2025)

| Base qualification | Registers as | QAT? | PRC? | Next stage | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **HSSC (Intermediate) / A-Level, 60%+** (A-Level: 3 subjects, 60% equivalence) | Full-time student | Exempt | Required (exemptions possible) | CAF | This is the "Matric/Intermediate route". PRC exemption needs HSSC 70% aggregate and 75% in the subject, or A-Level two Bs including a B in the subject, plus at least 70% syllabus match. |
| **HSSC / A-Level, 50–59%** | Full-time student after passing QAT | **Required** (maximum 3 attempts) | Required | CAF | QAT covers English, Mathematics and Analytical Reasoning. It is computer-based. You must register for PRC within 12 months of passing. |
| **Non-relevant associate degree (14 yrs), 45%+** | Trainee (after PRC) or full-time student | Exempt | Required, unless exempt (60% aggregate and 75% in subject) | CAF | Can start training after passing PRC or getting exemption from it. |
| **Relevant associate degree (14 yrs), 45%+** (at least 12 credit hours in accounting and at least 12 in business) | Trainee or full-time student | Exempt | **Exempt** | CAF | |
| **16-yr degree from an SDAI** (Specified Degree Awarding Institute), or an AACSB-accredited foreign university with HEC equivalence | Trainee | Exempt | Exempt | **All CAF exempt** (criteria apply) | Goes straight to CFAP (and training). |
| **16-yr relevant degree from an RDAI** (Relevant DAI), or a local NBEAC "W"-category institute | Trainee | Exempt | Exempt | CAF, with **up to 5 CAF exemptions**: CAF-1, 3, 4, 6, 7 | Needs at least 60% in each subject and at least 70% syllabus match. May choose GCC instead (irrevocable), but ICAP advises CAF. |
| **Other 16-yr graduates** (any HEC-recognised degree, including BBA/BCom (Hons) 4-yr, not from an RDAI/SDAI) | Trainee | Exempt | Exempt | **Graduate Conversion Course (GCC)** in place of CAF | GCC has 5 papers. An NBEAC "X"-category degree gives an exemption from GCC-1. |
| **ICMAP** (all exams passed) | Trainee | Exempt | Exempt | **All PRC and CAF exempt** | |
| **ACCA** (all exams passed) | Full-time student | Exempt | Exempt | CAF minus **CAF-1, 5, 6, 8** | The CAF-8 exemption applies only if ACCA AAA was passed. |
| **CIMA (UK)** (all exams passed) | Full-time student | Exempt | Exempt | CAF minus **CAF-1, 4, 5, 6, 7** | |
| **PIPFA** (all exams passed) | Full-time student | Exempt | Exempt | CAF minus **CAF-4** | |
| **Other relevant professional bodies** | Full-time student | n/a | Case by case | Case by case | **CFA charterholders are not named** in Scheme 2025 Directive 1.21 or the FAQs. Any CFA-based exemption would be **case by case**. Not verified. |

**Older routes:** 2-year BCom/BA (14-year) holders now fall under the "associate degree" rows. A 2-year BCom with at least 12 accounting and 12 business credit hours counts as **relevant**. The older idea of exemptions for "graduates, BCom or BBA" is now the RDAI/SDAI/GCC framework above.

## 4. Qualification structure at a glance (Scheme 2025)

| Stage | Papers / components | Delivery and frequency | Time limit / attempts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **QAT** (Qualifying Assessment Test) | English, Mathematics, Analytical Reasoning | CBT. Held Mar, Jun, Sep, Nov, Dec | Max 3 attempts |
| **PRC** (Pre-Requisite Competencies) | PRC-1 Fundamentals of Accounting; PRC-2 Quantitative Analysis for Business; PRC-3 Business and Economic Insights | Every two months (Jan, Mar, May, Jul, Sep, Nov) | All papers within **15 months** of the first attempt |
| **CAF** (Certificate in Accounting and Finance), 8 papers | **Group A:** CAF-1 Financial Accounting and Reporting; CAF-2 Taxation Principles and Compliance; CAF-3 Data, Systems and Risks; CAF-4 Business Law Dynamics. **Group B:** CAF-5 Management Accounting; CAF-6 Corporate Reporting; CAF-7 Business Insights and Analysis; CAF-8 Audit and Assurance Essentials | Twice a year. Max 4 papers per sitting. Group B opens after 2 Group A passes | No attempt limit (Directive 1.01). See the caveats in training-and-exams.md. |
| **GCC** (Graduate Conversion Course), 5 papers, taken *instead of* CAF by graduates | GCC-1 Business Concept and Environment (CBT); GCC-2 equals CAF-2; GCC-3 equals CAF-5; GCC-4 equals CAF-6; GCC-5 equals CAF-8 | Twice a year, alongside CAF | No attempt limit |
| **Hands-on Courses** | English Communication Skills (assessed by the British Council); Presentation and Personal Effectiveness (PPE); MS Office for Business (Excel, Word and PowerPoint, assessed separately) | Before training starts, or before CFAP | |
| **CFAP** (Certified Finance and Accounting Professional), 6 papers | CFAP-1 Advanced Corporate Reporting; CFAP-2 Corporate Laws and Governance; CFAP-3 Sustainability Reporting and Assurance (new); CFAP-4 Strategic Business Finance; CFAP-5 Tax Practices and Planning; CFAP-6 Audit, Assurance and Data | **Quarterly from Summer 2026** (Mar, Jun, Sep, Dec). Computer-based. Some papers are open-book. | No attempt limit (Directive 1.01) |
| **Integrated Modules** | AI and Data Analytics (90 hours recommended; pass before SCS, ideally before CFAP-4 and CFAP-6); Governance and Ethics (40 hours; pass before SCS) | Assessed by ICAP | |
| **SCS** (Strategic Case Study) | One integrated, open-book case with **pre-seen material released 20–30 days before** the exam. Replaces MSA-1 and MSA-2. | Twice a year (Jun, Dec) | Taken after all CFAP papers, or with 1 CFAP paper left. No attempt limit. |
| **Practical training** | 2.5, 3, 3.5 or 4 years with an ICAP-authorised Training Organization (TO) | See training-and-exams.md | |
| **Membership** | ACA, then FCA | | |
| **Post-qualification specialisations** (members only, planned) | Digital Transformation and Technology Assurance; Financial Services and Asset Management; Strategic Business Advisory; Islamic Finance, Reporting and Assurance | | Not yet confirmed as launched |

Scheme 2025 has **18 papers in total** (3 PRC, 8 CAF, 6 CFAP and 1 SCS), down from 21 under Scheme 2021.

**Passing standard:** **50% in each paper**. A pass is credited per paper, with no aggregate scheme (Directive 1.01, para 8(5)).

## 5. The CAF intermediate certificate (and legacy AFC)

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| What it is | The **Certificate in Accounting and Finance** is a standalone, recognised intermediate certificate awarded after passing all 8 CAF papers. Under Scheme 2025 you must also complete the PPE and MS Office hands-on courses before training or CFAP. |
| Level | ECCTIS benchmarked it at **UK RQF Level 5**. That benchmark was based on CAF under Scheme 2021. |
| Academic credit | HEC Undergraduate Policy 2020 allows up to **60 credit hours** of exemption in a 120-credit-hour BS Accounting & Finance. Universities with such programmes include NUST, LSE (Lahore), SZABIST, UCP, VU and others. |
| International | SOCPA offers associate membership to CAF students. |
| Career use | Passing CAF is the usual trigger to start a training contract. Trainee stipends also depend on CAF status. |
| **AFC (legacy)** | The **Assessment of Fundamental Competencies** was the entry-level stage of the 2013 scheme. It was **not** an intermediate certificate. ICAP's statistics page holds AFC data only up to 2022. It has been replaced by **PRC**, so new students no longer take AFC. |

## 6. Approximate time to qualify

From ICAP's own comparison for a **CAF-passed HSSC/A-Level student** (Scheme 2025 booklet, p.17):

| Training option | PRC | CAF | CFAP | Training | SCS | **Total (ICAP estimate)** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Option 1: standard 3.5-yr training, exams during training | 2 months | 18 months | During training | 42 months | During training | **~5 yrs 2 months** |
| Option 2: 2.5-yr uninterrupted training, no exams during it | 2 months | 18 months | 18 months, after training | 30 months | 6 months | **~6 yrs 2 months** |
| Option 3: 4 CFAP papers first (or 2 yrs post-CAF), then 3-yr training | 2 months | 18 months | 12 months (4 papers), rest during training | 36 months | During training | **~5 yrs 8 months** |

These are **minimum, no-fail figures**. With CAF pass rates around 30–50% per paper (see training-and-exams.md), real-world completion is usually longer. ICAP does not publish a median completion time, and none was found. Graduates (RDAI/SDAI/GCC) skip PRC and some or all of CAF, which shortens the path.
