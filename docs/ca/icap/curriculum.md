# ICAP (Pakistan) Chartered Accountancy: Current Curriculum

**As of: 2026-10-22**

**Scope:** The ICAP CA qualification under the **Education and Training Scheme 2025** ("Scheme 2025"), which replaced the Education and Training Scheme 2021. Primary sources are ICAP's official PDFs (Directive 1.01, Directive 1.21, the Scheme 2025 principles booklet, the Scheme 2025 FAQs, the standalone PRC/CAF/CFAP/SCS/GCC syllabi, model papers and the 2026 question papers). All URLs are listed under Sources at the end.

**Legend:**
- **P1/P2/P3** are ICAP proficiency levels: foundation, intermediate and advanced.
- **T1/T2/T3** are ICAP testing levels, meaning the maximum marks per topic in one attempt:
  - **T1:** at most 8 marks per topic and at most 35 marks in total across all T1 topics. T1 also covers topics examined at earlier stages and topics new to the syllabus.
  - **T2:** at most 20 marks per topic.
  - **T3:** at most 25 marks per topic.
  - Any topic new to the syllabus is tested at T1 in its first attempt, whatever level the syllabus gives it.
- **Weightage** is ICAP's official % range for each syllabus area. **Hours** are ICAP's teaching or learning-hour guidance.
- "Unverified" means I could not confirm the item in an official ICAP source.

**Passing rule.** Directive 1.01 para 8(5)(i) says "The passing marks shall be 50% in each subject." I found no aggregate or compensation rule and no partial "permanent credit" rule in Scheme 2025. Press coverage of the June 2026 results mentions "permanent credits", which is legacy terminology. Whether any aggregate pass route survives in Scheme 2025 is **unverified**. Only failed papers have their marks disclosed.

**Conflict on time and attempt limits.** The principles booklet says:
- CAF: 5 years from the first attempt, plus 1 extra year if no more than 2 papers remain.
- Group B: can only be attempted after passing 2 Group A papers.
- GCC: 4 years from the first attempt, plus 1 extra year if 1 paper remains.
- CFAP: 10 years.

The later, amended Directive 1.01 (para 9) and FAQ 7.6 say there is **no attempt limit** for CAF, GCC, CFAP or SCS, and allow up to 4 CAF papers per sitting "from any group". The FAQ keeps the rule of at least 4 CFAP papers within 10 years. **Treat the Directive as authoritative.**

### 1.2 Progression and eligibility rules (Directive 1.01 para 9; FAQs)

| Step | Rule |
|---|---|
| PRC | Open to anyone registered as a student, i.e. who meets the entry criteria or has passed the QAT. |
| CAF | Requires PRC to be completed or exempted. Max 4 papers per sitting. |
| GCC | Requires registration as a 16-year graduate who does not qualify for CAF exemption. |
| CFAP | Requires CAF or GCC to be complete, **plus one training option** (the choice is irrevocable): (i) 12 months of training completed before the first day of the exam month; (ii) the 2.5-year uninterrupted-training option, after which exams can only be taken once training is complete; or (iii) sit CFAP before starting training. Under (iii), training can start after passing 4 CFAP papers or 2 years after passing CAF, whichever is earlier. The remaining CFAP papers and the SCS must then wait until 12 months of training are done. |
| SCS | Requires all CFAP papers passed, or only one left, plus the Governance & Ethics and AI & Data Analytics integrated modules. |
| Hands-on courses | PPE and MS Office for Business: before training (CAF route) or before CFAP (graduate route, or the CFAP-before-training option). All 3 MS Office papers (Word, Excel, PowerPoint) must be passed. English Communication Skills: before training or before CFAP. |
| Integrated modules | AI & Data Analytics: can be taken after passing CAF and must be passed before the SCS. FAQ advice is to finish it before CFAP-6 or CFAP-4; the principles booklet says it is a prerequisite for CFAP-6. Governance & Ethics: must be passed before the SCS; advised in the first year of training. Both are assessed quarterly. |
| Training length | CAF-passed students: 2.5 years uninterrupted (no exams), 3 years (option iii) or 3.5 years (exams during training). Other 16-year or 14-year graduates without CAF exemption: up to 4 years if they sit CAF, CFAP or SCS during training (FAQ 9.1). |

### 1.4 Syllabus currency rule (Directive 1.01 para 4(4); FAQ 2.4)

- **Standards and codes** (IFRS, ISAs, Code of Ethics) become examinable 6 months after the later of two dates: ICAP's publication of the study material, or the issuer's application date.
- **Non-fiscal laws** become examinable 6 months after publication.
- **Fiscal laws:**
  - The standard lag is also 6 months.
  - For **CFAP and SCS**, a Finance Act or Ordinance issued 4 months before the exam month is examinable. So the December CFAP/SCS sitting uses the current Finance Act, while the September CFAP sitting uses the previous one.
- Applicable editions for the Winter 2026 CFAP attempt: CFAP-1 study text 2026, CFAP-2 2025, CFAP-4 2025, CFAP-5 2026, CFAP-6 2025. For CFAP-6, ISA editions 2023-24 and 2025 are both accepted.

## 3. PRC: Pre-Requisite Competencies (3 papers)

Format: computer-based, objective items, 50% to pass, all papers within 15 months. PRC-3 is one 120-minute, 100-mark paper with Business and Economics marked together. Legacy students who already passed PRC-3 Economics or PRC-5 Business under Scheme 2021 sit a 60-minute, 50-mark component paper.

## 7. Strategic Case Study (replaces MSA-1 and MSA-2)

**Competency:** Evaluate and advise on all aspects of business strategy and management, upholding ethics, transparency and accountability.

**Format:**
- Computer-based and open book (pre-seen material, notes and three books).
- Pre-seen material is released 20–30 days before the exam.
- 100 marks; June 2026: 4 h 15 min, including reading time and 30 min extra for CBE.
- All questions are compulsory. The June 2026 paper had 3 case-themed questions with 9 sub-requirements of 5–14 marks.
- Held twice a year (June and December). Pass mark 50%.

| Ref | Area | Teaching hours | Weightage |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Strategic Management | 50–55 | 40–50% |

- **A** Formulating and implementing non-financial strategy (P3, T3).
- **B** Financial strategy (P3, T3).
- **C**
  - C1 Direct and indirect tax strategy (P2, T2).

## 12. Gaps and items to verify

1. **Effective date:** Directive 1.01 says 16 Jul 2025; the booklet and FAQ say 16 Jan 2026.
2. **CAF-6 area A weighting:** 20–30% in the CAF syllabus vs 30–40% in the GCC-4 syllabus.
3. **Time and attempt limits:** the principles booklet (CAF 5 years, GCC 4 years, CFAP 10 years, Group B after 2 Group A papers) conflicts with Directive 1.01 and FAQ (no limits; any group). This file follows the Directive.
4. **SCS duration:** model paper I says 4 h including 30 min reading; the June 2026 paper and the CBE FAQ say 4 h 15 min.
5. **Durations not found:** PRC-1 and PRC-2, QAT, and the GCC papers. Number of PRC items is also not found.
6. **Pass thresholds not found:** QAT and English Communication Skills. No aggregate or partial-credit rule for CAF or CFAP was found in Scheme 2025 documents.
7. **Missing competency statements:** none printed for CAF-4, CFAP-2, CFAP-3 or CFAP-5.
8. **Syllabus versions:** the Directive 1.01 annexure copies differ slightly in teaching hours from the standalone syllabi; weightages match except item 2. Always check the latest attempt-specific syllabus PDF (CAF "Spring 2027", CFAP and SCS "Winter 2026") before each sitting.
