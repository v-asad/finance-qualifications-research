## 1. What the ACA is

The ACA is the chartered accountancy qualification of the **Institute of Chartered Accountants in England and Wales (ICAEW)**. Qualified members use the designatory letters **ACA** (and later **FCA** for Fellows). ICAEW was founded in 1880. It is a founding member of **Chartered Accountants Worldwide (CAW)** and a member of the **Global Accounting Alliance (GAA)**.

### IMPORTANT: two syllabuses are running in parallel (2025–2027)

ICAEW is replacing the ACA syllabus with the **Next Generation ACA**. A product built in late 2026 has to handle both:

| Item | Legacy ("current") ACA | Next Generation ACA |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Training agreements that started **on or before 30 Jun 2025** (they may opt to transfer) | Training agreements starting **from 1 Jul 2025** |
| Exams | 15 (6 Certificate + 6 Professional + 3 Advanced) | **14** (6 Certificate + 6 Professional + 2 Advanced) |
| Advanced Level | Corporate Reporting, Strategic Business Management, Case Study | **Technical Case Study**, **Strategic Case Study** |
| Ethics | Ethics Learning Programme (online, 70% pass) + Ethics in Practice scenarios | New Certificate exam **Sustainability and Ethics**. The ELP is available to existing students only until 31 Dec 2026. |
| Skills | Professional development ladders | Framework of 25 professional skills across 5 competency areas, plus at least 30 units of **Specialised Learning** |
| Work experience | 450 days | 450 days (unchanged) |

**Dual-running timeline (official ICAEW):**

| Level / exam | Last sitting of legacy module | First sitting of Next Gen module |
|---|---|---|
| Certificate Level | March 2026 | September 2025 (Tax Fundamentals from December 2025) |
| Professional Level | September 2026 | March 2026 |
| Case Study (legacy) | November 2026 | — |
| Corporate Reporting (legacy) | July 2027 | — |
| Strategic Business Management (legacy) | November 2027 | — |
| Technical Case Study / Strategic Case Study | — | July 2027 |

**What this means at 22 Oct 2026:**
- Certificate and Professional Level are now **Next Gen only**. The last legacy Professional sitting was September 2026.
- Legacy Advanced Level is still examined until Nov 2027.
- The new Advanced Level case studies first sit in July 2027.

## 2. Components of the (Next Generation) ACA

According to the syllabus handbook, the ACA has three integrated components:

1. **Business, finance and accountancy exams.** There are 14 exams across Certificate, Professional and Advanced Levels. See [curriculum.md](./curriculum.md).
2. **Specialised Learning.**
   - At least **30 units** on ICAEW's e-learning platform; each unit is roughly 1 hour of learning.
   - Content covers specialised technical, professional skills, sector-specific, and emerging issues and updates.
   - Units include "checkpoint assessments". These have no pass mark and are not extra exams.
   - Students and employers choose the units, and employers can see progress.
   - It must be completed by the end of the training agreement.
3. **Professional skills and experience.**
   - **Professional skills:** 25 skills in 5 competency areas: Communication, Critical Thinking, Emotional Intelligence, Leadership, Professionalism. A three-stage proficiency model applies. Next Gen students must reach "experienced" in all skills and "accomplished" in at least five by the end of the training agreement (ICAEW FAQ).
   - **Professional work experience:** at least **450 days** (1 day = 7 hours) under a training agreement with an authorised training employer or principal. This normally takes **3–5 years**. Responsibility should increase over time, experience must be recorded throughout, and it should cover a range of assignments, functions and sectors.
   - **Six-monthly reviews** with the employer happen throughout the training agreement (training-file mechanics).

Cross-cutting themes are **sustainability, technology and ethics**, which are embedded at every level. Technology is framed as "ABCD": AI, Automation, Blockchain, Cyber security, Cloud, Data and Digital transformation.

## 3. Routes to the ACA

| Route | Summary | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Training agreement with an Authorised Training Employer (ATE)** | The standard route. A 3–5 year training agreement (school leaver or graduate). The employer usually pays exam and registration fees and gives study leave and tuition. | Training agreements can be extended to a maximum of 60 months in total (FAQ). |
| **University first (graduate entry)** | Complete a degree, then start a training agreement. A relevant degree may earn **credit for prior learning (CPL)**. | Under Next Gen, up to **8 exams** can be credited for academic qualifications. |
| **Start your ACA at university** | ACA-accredited university degrees embed some ACA modules. | — |
| **ICAEW Apprenticeships (England only)** | Level 4 (Accounting/Professional Accounting Technician, which includes ICAEW CFAB) and Level 7 (Accountancy/Taxation Professional, which includes the ACA). | FAQ: open to people 16+ living in England and not in full-time education. Funding caps are £12,000 (L4) and £21,000 (L7). L7 funding excludes resit, exemption and ICAEW registration/exam fees. **[unverified]** The UK government announced that Level 7 apprenticeship levy funding would be restricted to younger apprentices (under 22) from January 2026. Check the current Skills England rules. |
| **AAT-ACA Fast Track** | A partnership route for AAT-qualified students that gives credit towards the ACA. | — |
| **ACA-CTA Joint Programme (with CIOT)** | Earns both ACA and CTA, specialising in tax. | Joint Programme students who started by 30 Jun 2025 can be exempt from up to 11 ACA exams. CTA exams have no attempt limit. |
| **Study independently** | Sit exams before getting work experience or a training employer. A training agreement is still needed to qualify. | Independent students pay their own fees. |
| **ICAEW CFAB (Certificate in Finance, Accounting and Business)** | A standalone qualification made up of the **six Certificate Level exams**. It counts as credit towards the ACA Certificate Level. | Accounting Fundamentals is mandatory for all CFAB students (no CPL). |
| **Pathways to Membership** | For experienced, qualified members of other eligible bodies. Membership is based on experience. | Reciprocal membership exists with CA ANZ, CPA Canada, HKICPA, SAICA, ICAS, CAI, ISCA, BICA and ICAZ (see `../README.md`). |
| **International** | ACA studied overseas through Partners in Learning and international ATEs. | — |

**Credit for prior learning (CPL), Next Gen:**
- Up to **8 exams** can be credited for academic qualifications, and up to **12** for professional qualifications.
- Credit is not available for Advanced Level.
- CPL is charged at the exam fee: £99 Certificate, £133 Professional (2026).

## 4. Time to qualify

- Typically **3–5 years** (the length of the training agreement). The 450 days of experience is the binding constraint.
- Exams can generally be taken in **any order**, with these exceptions:
  - **Fundamental Case Study:** recommended after Certificate Level and before the other Professional exams.
  - **Strategic Case Study:** must be attempted **last** and only in the **last 18 months** of the training agreement.
  - **Legacy Case Study:** must be taken last, in the final year.
- Certificate exams are **on-demand** all year round.
- Professional Level exams have 4 sittings a year: Mar, Jun, Sep, Dec.
- Advanced Level exams have 2 sittings a year: Jul, Nov.

## 6. Product implications (summary)

- **Two content trees:** Next Gen (primary) and legacy Advanced Level (CR/SBM, until Nov 2027). Legacy Certificate and Professional content can be retired.
- **Different engines per level:**
  - **Certificate:** objective-test engine (MCQ, multi-part MCQ, multiple response, numeric entry) plus a few short long-form or scenario items.
  - **Professional:** written, scenario-based, spreadsheet-capable answer engine with permitted-text/open-book mode. Corporate Reporting, Data and Assurance (CRDA) also needs data-analytics tasks.
  - **Advanced:** 4-hour case studies built on a shared **Advance Information** pack. Marking is skills-based (assimilating information, structuring, judgement, concluding/communicating).
- **Progress tracking** should cover:
  - exams passed or credited (CPL);
  - Specialised Learning units out of 30;
  - professional-skills proficiency levels;
  - work-experience days out of 450;
  - Ethics Learning Programme status for legacy students;
  - attempts used out of 4 per exam.
