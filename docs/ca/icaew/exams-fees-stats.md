## 1. Exam sessions and windows

| Level | Availability | Delivery | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Certificate (Next Gen) | **On demand all year** (from Sept 2025; Tax Fundamentals from Dec 2025) | Computer-based at ICAEW-approved test centres (subject to availability) | Results are typically immediate or provisional at the centre for objective tests **[unverified for the Next Gen long-form items]** |
| Professional (Next Gen) | **4 sittings a year: March, June, September, December** (from March 2026) | Exam centre **or remote invigilation** | Results are typically released around 4 weeks later. 2025 legacy examples: March 2025 sitting results on 17 Apr 2025; September 2025 results on 17 Oct 2025. |
| Advanced (legacy CR/SBM/Case Study, and Next Gen TCS/SCS from Jul 2027) | **2 sittings a year: July and November** | Exam centre **or remote invigilation** | Results around 5–6 weeks later. Examples: July 2025 results on 28 Aug 2025; November 2025 results on 11 Dec 2025. |

## 2. Fees (GBP)

### 2.1 Exam and credit fees: 2026 (ICAEW "ACA fees" page)

| Item | 2026 fee |
|---|---|
| Certificate Level exam | **£99** |
| Professional Level exam | **£133** |
| Advanced Level exam (Corporate Reporting, Strategic Business Management) | **£220** |
| Case Study exam | **£297** |
| Credit for prior learning, per Certificate exam | £99 |
| Credit for prior learning, per Professional exam | £133 |
| Credit for Advanced Level | Not available |
| Annual student registration fee | **£216 + VAT** (independent students registering 1 Jul–31 Oct pay a half-year fee) |

- The fees page lists legacy Advanced names. Fees for TCS and SCS (from 2027) had not been published at the time of research **[check later]**.
- **Who pays.** "The authorised training employer is responsible for paying the student's annual registration fees and exam entry fees, unless these have been explicitly passed on to the student."
- **Not covered here:**
  - Study materials. These are digital-only ICAEW learning materials, priced separately.
  - Tuition from Partners in Learning such as BPP, Kaplan and First Intuition.
- **Indicative third-party price.** One provider listed Advanced Level materials for July 2026 at £140.40 per paper. That is a provider price, not an ICAEW price.

### 2.2 Apprenticeship funding (England)

Figures are from the ICAEW FAQ.

- **Funding caps.** £12,000 for Level 4 and £21,000 for Level 7.
- **Employer contribution.**
  - Non-levy employers contribute 5%.
  - Non-levy employers with 50 or fewer staff get 100% funding for apprentices aged 16–21.
- **What Level 7 funding excludes.** Resit fees, exemption fees, and ICAEW or CIOT registration and exam fees.
- **[unverified]** The UK government restricted Level 7 apprenticeship funding to younger apprentices from January 2026. Confirm with Skills England before relying on it.

## 3. Attempt and progression rules

| Rule | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Attempts per exam | **Maximum of 4 attempts** at ACA exams. CTA exams on the Joint Programme have no limit. Employers may set stricter internal limits. | ICAEW FAQ |
| Order | Any order, with two exceptions: FCS is recommended first at Professional Level, and the **SCS must be attempted last, in the last 18 months of the training agreement**. Legacy Case Study must be last, in the final year. | ICAEW exams page and FAQ |
| Training agreement length | Normally 3–5 years. Can be extended to 60 months in total; students can self-extend online by up to 3 months. | FAQ |
| Work experience | At least **450 days** (7 hours = 1 day). Re-registered students need at least 65 days with the new employer, or enough to reach 450 days in total, whichever is greater. | FAQ, handbook |
| Specialised Learning | At least **30 units** (about 1 hour each), completed before the training agreement ends. Checkpoint assessments carry no pass mark. | FAQ, handbook |
| Professional skills | 25 skills across 5 competencies. The student must reach "experienced" in all of them and "accomplished" in at least 5 by the end of the training agreement. | FAQ |
| Legacy ethics | Ethics Learning Programme: 70% pass mark, within the first 12 months, with no attempt limit unless the employer sets one. Available until 31 Dec 2026, after which Sustainability and Ethics replaces it. | FAQ |
| Pass marks | Certificate 55%; Professional 55%; Advanced 50% | Handbooks |
| Switching to Next Gen | Legacy students may transfer. Transferring students "must have passed six Professional Level and six Certificate Level exams overall". ICAEW says no extra exams should be required, although an **SBM top-up** of about 15 units may apply to some. Legacy development-ladder progress does not carry over to the new skills framework. | Next Gen overview; FAQ; First Intuition |

**What the product should track:**
- Attempts used, out of 4, per module.
- Credited exams, flagged as CPL.
- The deadline for the SCS window.
- Remaining legacy deadlines: CR in Jul 2027 and SBM in Nov 2027.

## 4. Credit for prior learning (CPL)

- **What it is.** ICAEW's term for exemptions. Applicants qualify through an accounting or business-related degree, or a professional accountancy qualification.
- **Limits under Next Gen.**
  - Up to **8 exams** for academic qualifications.
  - Up to **12 exams** for professional qualifications.
  - The overall cap is "up to 12" of the 14 exams.
- **Advanced Level.** Never credited.
- **Joint Programme.** Students who started by 30 Jun 2025 can receive up to 11 ACA exemptions.
- **CFAB.** Accounting Fundamentals is mandatory for CFAB students.
- **Cost.** CPL is charged at the same fee as the exam itself. Some countries have reduced or free credit.
- **Checking eligibility.** ICAEW's online CPL search tool lists accredited degrees and qualifications. A product could ingest this so users can see their own exemptions.

### 5.3 2026 results (Next Gen Certificate/Professional, legacy Advanced)

- **Not found.** ICAEW's 2026 news-release listing (checked 22 Oct 2026) has no results press releases.
- ICAEW may now publish results only on its student "Exam results" page (https://www.icaew.com/students/exams/exam-results) or in student communications.
- Pass rates for the first Next Gen Professional sittings (March, June and September 2026) are therefore **unknown**. These figures matter for calibrating difficulty, so they should be collected when available.

**Observations for calibrating mock exams:**
- Advanced Level pass rates are high, at about 79–87%.
- Most Professional exams pass at about 75–90%.
- Niche papers are the outliers: FAR UK GAAP at about 43–50%, and Business Planning Banking and Insurance at about 53–74%.
- These are first-attempt plus resit pooled figures for employer-sponsored candidates. They are not comparable with the pass rates of open-access bodies such as ACCA.
