## 1. What "Chartered Accountant" means

- **No single global CA qualification.** "Chartered Accountant" (CA) is a professional title that national institutes grant under a **Royal Charter** or a national statute.
  - The title began in Scotland. The Society of Accountants in Edinburgh received its charter in 1854; it is a predecessor of ICAS.
  - Each body sets its own exams, practical-experience rules and ethics/CPD regime, and regulates its members.
- **Designation letters and protection vary by body:**

  | Body | Letters |
  |---|---|
  | ICAEW | ACA / FCA |
  | ICAS | CA |
  | CA ANZ | CA |
  | SAICA | CA(SA) |
  | Chartered Accountants Ireland | ACA / FCA |
  | ICAI and ICAP | ACA / FCA |

  The title is usually protected locally. ICAS states that "CA" is exclusive to ICAS members in the UK and EU.
- **Common features across CA qualifications:**
  - a degree-level entry point, or bridging studies;
  - **3 years or more of supervised, employer-based training** under a training contract or agreement;
  - a tiered exam structure ending in an **integrated case study**;
  - embedded ethics, with mandatory CPD after qualifying.
- **In practice:** "CA" generally signals that the holder trained inside a firm or business and passed a multi-level professional exam suite. Exam-only routes such as ACCA or CPA programs without employer training contracts are different.

## 2. Umbrella networks and mutual recognition

### 2.1 Chartered Accountants Worldwide (CAW)

- **What it is:** a network that promotes the CA designation and CA leadership. It says it is *not* a qualification body or a regulator.
- **Size:** **2m+ CAs and students in 190+ countries**, per CAW's own site. ICAEW material still quotes the older figure of "over 1.8m".
- **16 member institutes:**

  | Region | Institutes |
  |---|---|
  | UK and Ireland | ICAEW, ICAS, Chartered Accountants Ireland |
  | Australia and NZ | CA ANZ |
  | Africa | SAICA, ICAN (Namibia), BICA (Botswana), ZICA (Zambia), ICAZ (Zimbabwe) |
  | Asia | ISCA (Singapore), ICAI (India), ICAP (Pakistan), IAI (Indonesia), ICAB (Bangladesh), CA Sri Lanka, ICAN (Nepal) |

- ICAEW is a founding member. CA ANZ's CEO chairs CAW (per Wikipedia).
- No associate-member category appears on CAW's current site **[unverified whether it still exists]**.

### 2.2 Global Accounting Alliance (GAA)

- **When formed:** November 2005.
- **What it is:** a forum of leading bodies in major capital markets. It focuses on the public interest, quality, information-sharing and advocacy.
- **10 members:**
  - AICPA & CIMA (US)
  - CPA Canada
  - Chartered Accountants Ireland
  - SAICA
  - IDW (Germany)
  - CA ANZ
  - ICAEW
  - ICAS
  - HKICPA
  - JICPA (Japan)
- **Size:** more than 1.4m professionals.
- **No GAA-wide mutual recognition agreement.** Recognition works through bilateral reciprocal-membership agreements, plus the **GAA Passport**. The Passport gives "host" member support when a member works temporarily in another GAA country; it is not a membership right.

### 2.3 Key bilateral reciprocity (membership, not automatically audit or practising rights)

| Body | Reciprocal membership with | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| ICAEW | CA ANZ, CPA Canada, HKICPA, SAICA, ICAS, CAI, ISCA, BICA, ICAZ | Audit qualification needs extra exams or experience. SAICA members must keep their SAICA membership. |
| ICAS | ICAEW, CAI, CPA Canada, CA ANZ, SAICA, HKICPA, ISCA | No extra exams, if the applicant is in good standing |
| CA ANZ | AICPA, ICAEW, CPA Canada, ICAS, CAI (2 years post-qualification), HKICPA, SAICA | ICAI, ICAP, ICAN, ICAB and CA Sri Lanka members go through the International Pathway Program (5+ years post-qualification, including a senior role) |
| SAICA | CA ANZ, CAI, CPA Canada, HKICPA, ICAEW, ICAS (+ AICPA/NASBA MRA) | CAI may require local law and tax exams |
| CPA Canada | ICAS, CAI, CA ANZ, SAICA (since 1 Jan 2018) | Administered through the provincial bodies |
| ICAI | MoU/MRA with CA ANZ (signed 2021 for 3 years; renewal **[unverified]**); also lists ICAEW, CPA Canada, SAICA | — |

**Product implication:** reciprocal and pathway members are a niche audience that needs **gap modules** (local tax, law and audit) rather than full exam prep.

## 4. Design implications (across bodies)

1. **Syllabus versioning is essential.** All three in-scope bodies have recently rebuilt their syllabus:
   - ICAEW Next Gen: 2025–2027.
   - ICAS CA24: 2024.
   - CA ANZ revision: 2022, with a further update from T3 2026.

   The product needs `qualification → syllabus_version → level → module → syllabus area → learning outcome` with effective dates and transition mappings, such as ICAEW legacy→Next Gen exemption mapping.
2. **Three assessment archetypes recur:**
   - (a) on-demand **objective tests** (ICAEW Certificate, ICAS Knowledge, CA Foundations);
   - (b) timed **scenario-based written exams**, often with permitted texts or spreadsheets (ICAEW Professional, ICAS Skills, CA ANZ FAR, Tax and Audit);
   - (c) **pre-seen integrated case studies** (ICAEW TCS/SCS, ICAS Integrated Case Study, CA ANZ ICAP).

   A single mock engine should support all three. Archetype (c) needs rubric or AI-assisted marking against skill dimensions.
3. **Data and technology skills are now examined.** Examples include ICAEW CRDA data-analytics software, ICAS modelling and Power BI coursework, and the CA ANZ Data Analytics elective. Spreadsheet and dataset tasks should be first-class question types.
4. **Ethics and sustainability are cross-cutting.** Tag every item with them rather than treating them as stand-alone modules, although ICAEW's Sustainability and Ethics module and ICAS Professional Ethics also exist as standalone assessments.
5. **Progress tracking must go beyond exams.** It should also cover:
   - practical experience (days or competencies);
   - mentor or six-monthly reviews;
   - Specialised Learning units (ICAEW);
   - attempt limits (ICAEW and ICAS: 4);
   - time limits (CA ANZ: 8 years);
   - order constraints (ICAEW SCS last; ICAS Integration last; CA ANZ prerequisites).
6. **Localisation:** tax and law are jurisdiction-specific (UK; AU vs NZ). Tax content must be versioned by tax year or Finance Act.
