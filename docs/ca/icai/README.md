## 1. The body at a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Legal basis | Statutory body set up under the **Chartered Accountants Act, 1949** (Act No. XXXVIII of 1949). Operates under the **Ministry of Corporate Affairs**. The detailed rules are in the **Chartered Accountants Regulations, 1988** (exams are held under Reg. 22; scheme notifications under Regs. 25F / 28F / 31). [Official] |
| Founded | 1 July 1949. 1 July is "CA Day", and the current scheme was launched on CA Day 2023. [Official] |
| HQ | ICAI Bhawan, New Delhi |
| Governance | Elected Central Council (the 26th Council is in office in 2025–26) plus 5 Regional Councils. ICAI reports 186 branches in India, 54 overseas chapters and 31 representative offices (ICAI press release, July 2026). [Official] |
| Designations | **ACA** (Associate) and **FCA** (Fellow). A Certificate of Practice (COP) is needed to practise. See training-and-exams.md §10. |
| Statutory monopoly | Statutory audit of companies in India is reserved for CAs who hold a COP (Companies Act, 2013). [Background, not re-verified] |

## 3. Qualification structure (New Scheme of Education & Training, launched 1 Jul 2023)

The CA course has three levels (Foundation, Intermediate, Final) plus Self-Paced Online Modules (SPOM), two IT/soft-skills courses (ICITSS and AICITSS), and 2 years of practical training. [Official FAQ, updated 29 Jul 2026]

| Stage | What it is | Papers / content (headline only) | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Foundation** | Entry level for school leavers | 4 papers × 100: Accounting; Business Laws; Quantitative Aptitude (Business Maths, Logical Reasoning, Statistics); Business Economics | Papers 1–2 descriptive; Papers 3–4 objective with **−0.25 negative marking** |
| **Intermediate** | Two groups, 3 papers each | G-I: Advanced Accounting; Corporate & Other Laws; Taxation (Income-tax 50 + GST 50). G-II: Cost & Management Accounting; Auditing & Ethics; FM & SM (50 + 50) | 30% case-based MCQs + 70% descriptive. No negative marking. |
| **ICITSS** | Orientation Course + IT Training | 4 weeks in total | Must be completed **before** articleship starts |
| **Practical training (articleship)** | 2 years, uninterrupted | Optional 9–12 months of industrial training in the final part | Starts after passing **both** Intermediate groups and completing ICITSS |
| **Self-Paced Online Modules** | 4 modules, registered together with Final | Set A Corporate & Economic Laws (mandatory); Set B Strategic Cost & Performance Mgmt (mandatory); Set C elective (1 of 10); Set D multidisciplinary (1 of 4) | 50% to pass each module, unlimited attempts, marks not added to the Final aggregate |
| **AICITSS** | Advanced IT + Management & Communication Skills | 4 weeks in total | Taken **after** practical training and **before** the Final exam |
| **Final** | Two groups, 3 papers each | G-I: Financial Reporting; Advanced FM; Advanced Auditing, Assurance & Professional Ethics. G-II: Direct Tax Laws & International Taxation; Indirect Tax Laws; **Integrated Business Solutions** (multidisciplinary case study with SM, **open book**, 4 hours) | 30% MCQs + 70% descriptive. No negative marking. |
| **Membership** | Apply to ICAI after passing Final with all requirements met | — | ACA, then FCA later |

**Interim credential:** a **Business Accounting Associate (BAA)** certificate is available to students who have passed both Intermediate groups, completed practical training and AICITSS, and qualified the applicable SPOMs. [Official FAQ]

**International curriculum:** for students living outside India. Country-specific papers replace some Indian papers, IT and soft-skills training is virtual, and practical training can be done under members of IFAC-recognised bodies abroad. [Official FAQ]

## 4. Routes of entry

| Route | Who | Requirements | Enters at |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Foundation route** | School students | May **register after Class 10**. First Foundation attempt only **after appearing in the Class 12** exam. Registration must allow a **4-month study period** before the 1st of the exam month (register by 1 Jan for May, 1 May for Sep, 1 Sep of the previous year for Jan). | Foundation |
| **Direct entry: commerce graduates/postgraduates** | B.Com / M.Com etc. | **≥55%** marks | Intermediate (skips Foundation) |
| **Direct entry: non-commerce graduates/postgraduates** | Any other discipline | **≥60%** marks | Intermediate |
| **Direct entry: final-year students** | Last year of UG or PG | **Provisional** registration. The graduation marksheet with the required % must be submitted within 6 months of the final exam, otherwise the registration is cancelled with no credit. | Intermediate |
| **Direct entry: CS / CMA** | Passed the Intermediate level of **ICSI** (CS Executive) or **ICMAI** (CMA Intermediate) | Pass certificate | Intermediate |

All Intermediate entrants (any route) need **8 months of registration** before the 1st of the exam month: register by 1 Sep (previous year) for May, by 1 Jan for Sep, and by 1 May (previous year) for Jan. [Official FAQ]

> Note: some coaching sites say direct-entry graduates must complete ICITSS and then 9 months of articleship before sitting Intermediate. That was a feature of the **old (2017) scheme**. Under the current FAQ, practical training starts only after passing both Intermediate groups. [Official FAQ, 29 Jul 2026]

## 5. Time to qualify (indicative)

| Path | Minimum (no failures) | Rough breakdown |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation route (after Class 12) | **~4.5–5 years** | Foundation (4-month study minimum, ~2 months to result) → Intermediate (8-month registration minimum, result ~2 months later) → ICITSS (4 weeks) → 2-year articleship → AICITSS + Final (practical training must be complete; see eligibility in training-and-exams.md) |
| Direct entry (graduate) | **~3.5–4 years** after graduation | Skips Foundation. Otherwise the same. |

- These are **structural minimums**. Real-world times are much longer because pass rates are low. Since 2024, "both groups" pass rates have been roughly **5.7–18.4% at Intermediate** and **11–20% at Final** (see training-and-exams.md §8).
- ICAI itself does not publish an official "average time to qualify". [Unverified for any figure you may see quoted, e.g. "5 years".]
