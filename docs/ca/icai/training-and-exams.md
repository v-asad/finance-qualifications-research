# ICAI CA — Training, Exams, Fees, Pass Rates

**As of: 2026-10-22**

> Applies to the current ("New") Scheme of Education & Training (launched 1 Jul 2023). The core reference is ICAI's **FAQ on the Current Scheme, last updated 29 Jul 2026** ("FAQ" below).
> Legend: **[Official]** = icai.org / ICAI PDFs; **[Secondary]** = news/coaching sites; **[Unverified]** = could not confirm.

## 1. Practical training (articleship)

| Item | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | **2 years** (it was 3 years under the old scheme) | [Official] FAQ Q5.2 |
| When it can start | After passing **both groups** of Intermediate **and** completing the 4-week **ICITSS**. Passing a single group is not enough under the current scheme. | [Official] FAQ Q5.1 |
| Industrial training (optional) | **9–12 months**, allowed during the **last part** of the 2-year training (in industry rather than a CA firm) | [Official] FAQ Q5.3 |
| Leave | **12 days per year** of training. Excess leave must be served as extra training. | [Official] FAQ Q5.4 |
| Industrial trainee stipend | Minimum **₹15,000/month** (w.e.f. 1 Jul 2023) | [Official] FAQ Q5.6 |
| Transition | Old-scheme students had to start 3-year training by 30 Jun 2025. Other transition rules are in the BoS announcement of 23 Jun 2023. | [Secondary/Official mix] |

### Minimum monthly stipend (articled assistants)

**2-year articleship (current scheme)**, per the 29 Jul 2026 FAQ:

| Place of service (population) | Year 1 | Year 2 (and any excess-leave period) |
|---|---|---|
| ≥ 20 lakh | ₹5,000 | ₹6,000 |
| 5 lakh – < 20 lakh | ₹4,000 | ₹5,000 |
| < 5 lakh | ₹3,000 | ₹4,000 |

> **Status caveat:** the FAQ footnote says these rates were "notified through the notification dated 25th June 2026". Secondary reports (JurisHour, BCAJ, TaxGuru) describe the 25 Jun 2026 Gazette item as a **draft amendment to Regulation 48** with a 30-day comment window (copies public from 4 Jul 2026, comments due about 5 Aug 2026). I found no confirmation of final notification. Treat these as **proposed, possibly final**, and check the Gazette.

**3-year articleship (old scheme, still running for some students):** ≥20 lakh: ₹4,000 / ₹5,000 / ₹6,000. 5–20 lakh: ₹3,000 / ₹4,000 / ₹5,000. <5 lakh: ₹2,000 / ₹3,000 / ₹4,000 (years 1 / 2 / 3). [Official] FAQ Q5.5

## 2. ICITSS and AICITSS

| Course | Components | Duration | Timing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ICITSS** (Integrated Course on IT & Soft Skills) | Orientation Course (OC) + Information Technology Training (ITT) | **4 weeks in total** (commonly described as 2 × ~15 days; the split is [Secondary]) | Must be completed **before practical training starts**, i.e. after passing Intermediate |
| **AICITSS** (Advanced ICITSS) | Management & Communication Skills (MCS) + Advanced ITT | **4 weeks in total** | **After** completing practical training and **before** sitting the Final exam |

Source: [Official] FAQ Q6.1–6.3. The international curriculum allows these to be done virtually.

| Level | Frequency | Months | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|

**Timings:** all papers start at 2 PM IST and run 3 hours. Exceptions: Foundation Papers 3 & 4 run 2 hours, and Final Paper 6 (IBS, open book) runs 4 hours. There is 15 minutes of reading time, except for Foundation P3/P4. Overseas centres for Sep/Nov 2026 are **Bahrain, Dubai and Kathmandu**. [Official]

**Medium:** candidates at Foundation, Intermediate and Final may answer in **English or Hindi**. Post-qualification course exams are English only. [Official, 2 May 2026 notice]

**Computer-based exams:** I found **no official announcement** of a move to CBE for the main CA exams. Foundation Papers 3–4 remain objective pen-and-paper papers, and the Final is offline per the Sep/Nov 2026 notice. SPOM exams are online. [Gap. ## 4. Eligibility timelines

| Step | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation registration | Allowed after Class 10. First exam attempt only after **appearing in Class 12**. | [Official] FAQ Q2.1, Q2.5 |
| Foundation study period | Register **≥4 months before** the 1st day of the exam month: by 1 Jan for May, by 1 May for Sep, by 1 Sep (previous year) for Jan. | [Official] FAQ Q2.6 |
| Intermediate study period | Registered **≥8 months before** the 1st day of the exam month: by 1 Sep (previous year) for May, by 1 Jan for Sep, by 1 May (previous year) for Jan. | [Official] FAQ Q3.9 |
| Foundation → Intermediate gap | No separate rule beyond the 8-month registration. Foundation results come about 2 months after the exam, so in practice the earliest Intermediate attempt is about 12 months after the Foundation exam (e.g. Foundation Jan → result Mar → register by 1 May → Intermediate the following Jan). | Derived from the FAQ |
| Final registration | Any time after passing **both** Intermediate groups. SPOM registration happens at the same time. | [Official] FAQ Q4.1 |
| Final exam eligibility | Registered for Final. Practical training complete per the timing rule (see note). AICITSS done. **All 4 SPOMs qualified.** | [Official] FAQ Q4.5 |

> **Final timing rule (ambiguous):** the FAQ says the student must "have completed Practical Training six months before the first day of the month in which the examination is to be held". Secondary sites read this differently. Some say training must be fully complete by the last day of the month before the exam month. Others say a student may sit while in the **last 6 months** of training. Several coaching pages still repeat old-scheme rules. **Verify against the current Final exam notice before planning.**

## 7. Passing criteria and exemptions [Official]

| Level | Rule |
|---|---|
| Foundation | At one sitting: **≥40% in each paper** and **≥50% aggregate**. Papers 3–4 carry −0.25 negative marking. |
| Intermediate / Final, per group | At one sitting: ≥40% in each paper of the group and ≥50% aggregate for that group. Groups can be passed in different sittings. |
| Intermediate / Final, both groups together | Either each group passed as above, **or** ≥40% in every paper and ≥50% aggregate across **both groups combined** |
| **Paper exemption** | If a student appears in all papers of a group and fails it, any paper scored **≥60%** is exempted for the **next 3 exams**. Passing then needs ≥40% in the remaining papers and ≥50% aggregate including the exempted paper. |
| Permanent exemption | Once the 3 attempts are used up, the student can keep the exemption but must then score **≥50% in each remaining paper** of that group. |
| SPOM | ≥50% per module, unlimited attempts, not added to the Final aggregate |

## 9. Results announcement

- Results are published on **icai.nic.in** and **caresults.icai.org**. You need your roll number plus registration number (or PIN). ICAI also issues a Public Relations Committee press release with statistics and the All-India toppers (top 50 merit list). [Official]
- Typical lag from exam to result: about **6–8 weeks** (e.g. May 2026 Final → 18 Jun 2026; Sep 2025 → 3 Nov 2025).
- Verification and certified copies of answer books can be requested within a short window after results (details in each result notice; not compiled here).

## 10. Membership (ACA / FCA)

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Eligibility for membership (ACA) | Pass Final, complete practical training, AICITSS and SPOMs, then apply via the SSP. ICAI confers membership certificates at convocations (e.g. 8,964 at the 12 Sep 2026 convocation [Secondary]). |
| Fellow (FCA) | Under s.5 of the CA Act, an associate becomes eligible for fellowship after **5 continuous years** as an associate, subject to practice or other conditions set by the Council. [Background, exact conditions not re-verified] |
| Certificate of Practice (COP) | Required for public practice and statutory audit. [Background] |

## 11. CPE requirements (revised, effective calendar year 2026) [Secondary, citing the ICAI CPE FAQ updated 22 May 2026]

| Member category | Annual CPE hours |
|---|---|
| Under 60, **holding COP** | **40 hours**: at least 20 structured, the other 20 structured or unstructured. Up to 4 structured hours may be virtual via the Digital Learning Hub. |
| Under 60, **no COP** | 20 hours (structured or unstructured) |
| Under 60, residing abroad | 20 hours |
| 60 to under 70, with COP | 30 hours: 20 structured or unstructured + 10 structured (physical or DLH) |
| 70 to under 80, with COP | 20 hours |
| 80+, with COP | 10 hours |
| 60+, no COP | Exempt |

- From 2026 there is 1 mandatory structured hour each on Standards on Auditing, the Code of Ethics and Digital Technology (applies to COP holders under 70).
- A single age cut-off applies to the whole calendar year.
- Members who generate a UDIN in a year for which they claim exemption must still complete ≥10 hours.
- The CPE cycle moved from a rolling 3-year block to an **annual (calendar-year)** requirement. [Secondary]
