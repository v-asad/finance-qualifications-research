# ICAI Chartered Accountancy (CA) Curriculum: New Scheme of Education and Training

**As of: 2026-10-22**

Scope: the CA course under ICAI's **New Scheme of Education and Training**. Recent ICAI exam documents (2026) call it the "Existing Scheme". Key facts:

- The CA Regulations were amended by Notification No. 1-CA(7)/201/2023 dated 21 June 2023. Regulation 38D sets the Final passing requirements.
- The first exams under this scheme were Intermediate and Final in **May 2024** and Foundation in **June 2024**.
- The section-wise weightages were **issued 26 October 2023** and apply to "May 2024 and subsequent exams till further changes are made".

**Confidence legend** (used throughout):
- **[OFFICIAL]**: read directly from an ICAI PDF or page.
- **[LAYOUT]**: the figure is official, but which chapters fall under a section label was inferred from the PDF table layout. Text extraction loses the merged-cell structure, so check against the PDF before relying on the mapping.
- **[SECONDARY]**: from a non-ICAI source only.
- **[UNVERIFIED]**: not confirmed.

**Global rule [OFFICIAL]:** each syllabus PDF states that "a deviation of maximum ±5% in the specified section-wise weightage is permitted". Specific inclusions and exclusions are issued each year through **Study Guidelines**. Tax papers also change with each annual Finance Act.

## 1. Structure at a glance

| Level | Papers | Marks / paper | Exam sittings | Pass rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation | 4 (no groups) | 100 | Thrice a year: Jan / May / Sep [OFFICIAL, ICAI FAQ] | 40% in each paper and 50% aggregate, in one sitting. No paper exemptions. |
| Intermediate | 6, in 2 groups of 3 | 100 | Thrice a year: Jan / May / Sep [OFFICIAL, ICAI FAQ] | 40% in each paper and 50% aggregate per group (or across both groups together). 60%+ in a paper gives an exemption. |
| Final | 6, in 2 groups of 3 | 100 | 2025–26 cycles were Sep 2025, Jan 2026, May 2026 and **Nov 2026** (per the ICAI Nov 2026 guide). The forward pattern is [UNVERIFIED]. | Same as Intermediate. |
| Self-Paced Online Modules (SPOM) | Sets A, B, C, D | 100 each | On demand (SPMT portal) | 50% per module. No negative marking. Unlimited attempts. Not added to Final marks. |

Other requirements in the scheme (named here for context, not detailed):
- ICITSS (IT & Soft Skills) before articleship.
- Practical training (articleship).
- Advanced ICITSS before the Final exam.
- All 4 SPOMs qualified before the Final exam, for most cohorts (see §5).

### 1.1 Question format summary [OFFICIAL]

| Level / Paper | Objective (MCQ) share | Negative marking | Book | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation P1 Accounting, P2 Business Laws | 0% (fully subjective) | n/a | Closed | 3 h, plus 15 min advance reading (1:45–2:00 PM) |
| Foundation P3 Quantitative Aptitude, P4 Business Economics | 100% objective (MCQ, OMR) | **Yes: −0.25 per wrong answer.** Multiple darkened circles count as wrong. | Closed | 2 h, no advance reading time |
| Intermediate P1–P6 (all six) | **30 marks MCQ + 70 marks descriptive** | No | Closed | 3 h (2–5 PM) |
| Final P1–P5 | **30 marks case-scenario/case-study MCQ + 70 marks descriptive** | No | Closed | 3 h (2–5 PM) |
| Final P6 Integrated Business Solutions (IBS) | **40% MCQ / 60% descriptive** | No | **Open book** | **4 h** (2–6 PM) |

MCQ rules for the 30% component (Intermediate and Final) [OFFICIAL, ICAI exam guides Sep/Nov 2026]:
- MCQs carry 1 or 2 marks each.
- Each has 4 options with 1 correct answer.
- They are compulsory, with no internal or external choice.
- They are knowledge-based or application-based. Skill levels are "knowledge & comprehension" or "application & analysis".
- No negative marking. No reasoning is required.
- Answers are marked on the OMR portion of the answer-book cover.

Foundation rounding [OFFICIAL]: in P1 and P2, fractions of half a mark or more round up. In P3 and P4, any fraction rounds up.

Split papers [OFFICIAL]: Inter P3 (Income-tax / GST) and Inter P6 (FM / SM) use **two separate answer books** (Section A and Section B). Each is still treated as **one paper** for passing and exemption.

Medium: English or Hindi. The choice is made per group, not per paper.

## 2. FOUNDATION (4 papers, 100 marks each)

Pass: 40% in each paper and 50% aggregate across all 4, in one sitting. "There is No Concept of Exemption in a paper(s) in the Foundation Exam." [OFFICIAL]

Foundation rank certificates: 60% aggregate minimum, up to rank 20 [OFFICIAL, Foundation guide Sep 2026].

## 5. Self-Paced Online Modules (SPOM)

Who and when [OFFICIAL, ICAI FAQ last updated 27 Sep 2024; Final guide Nov 2026]:
- Students register for the modules at Final-course registration; no separate fee. Sets C and D choices are made on the Final registration form.
- **All four must be qualified before appearing in the Final exam.** For candidates admitted to Final from Nov 2024 onwards, the deadline for the Nov 2026 exam was 10 Oct 2026.
- Each module requires minimum prescribed learning hours before its test (SPMT portal, spmt.icai.org).
- Pass: **50%** per module. **No negative marking.** **No limit on attempts.** Marks are **not aggregated** with Final marks.
- Expected depth: **expert knowledge for Sets A and B**; **working knowledge for Sets C and D**.
- Study material: Sets A and B have printed material plus e-books and video lectures. Sets C and D have e-books and video lectures.

## 6. Passing and exemption rules (Intermediate and Final) [OFFICIAL: Regulation 38D; exam guides; FAQ]

1. **Group pass:** 40% in each paper of the group and 50% aggregate of the group, in one sitting.
2. **Both groups together:** either each group meets rule 1, **or** 40% in every paper and 50% aggregate across both groups combined.
3. **Paper exemption (60% rule): confirmed under the new scheme.**
   - Applies to a candidate who appears in **all papers of a group**, fails the group, but scores **60% or more in any paper**.
   - That paper is exempt for the **immediately next three examinations**. The exemption is automatic and shown on the marksheet.
   - To pass the group later, the candidate needs 40% in each remaining paper and 50% aggregate across all papers of the group. The exempted paper's marks count toward the aggregate.
   - No further exemptions in that group until the existing one is exhausted.
   - Exemption is granted only if the candidate was present in all papers of the group or unit.
4. **Continuing an exhausted exemption:** after the three attempts, the candidate may opt to continue the exemption ("permanent exemption" window). This comes with conditions on the remaining papers (see the ICAI guidance notes).
5. **Surrender:** a valid 60% exemption may be surrendered, in full, irrevocably, by affidavit before a cut-off date.
6. **Rank certificates (Intermediate and Final):** passed in one sitting, no exemption availed, at least 55% aggregate. Issued up to rank 50.
7. **Foundation:** 40% in each paper and 50% aggregate in one sitting. **No exemptions.**
