# ACCA exams: delivery modes, formats, question types, calendar, marking and results

> **As of:** 22 October 2026. This covers the current exams (2026/27 syllabus year). New-qualification formats from July/September 2027 are summarised in §8 and detailed in [future-qualification-2027.md](future-qualification-2027.md). Per-exam detail, including typical requirement patterns, is in [syllabus/](syllabus/).

## 1. Delivery modes

All ACCA exams are now **computer-based exams (CBEs)**.

| Mode | Exams | When | Results |
|---|---|---|---|
| **On-demand CBE (OD CBE)** | BT, MA, FA; LW-ENG and LW-GLO; FIA FA1, MA1, FA2, MA2, FBT, FMA, FFA | Any time, booked directly with a licensed centre (or remotely where no centre exists) | **Provisional result on screen immediately**; uploaded to MyACCA within **72 hours**. No administrative review. |
| **Session CBE** | PM, TX, FR, AA, FM; LW (other variants); SBL, SBR, AFM, APM, ATX, AAA; FIA FAU, FTX, FFM; DipIFR | Four sessions a year: **March, June, September, December** (one exam week each) | About **6 weeks** after the exam, released on a fixed date |
| **Remote invigilation** | Both OD and session exams | **Only in countries without exam-centre provision.** ACCA "withdrew remote exams in countries where we have exam centre provision". Taking a remote exam from a country other than the one booked means **result nullified, no refund**. | Same as centre |

- There are centres in **100+ countries**. Remote and centre exams use the "same format and … same exam software".
- **Session variants:** March and September sessions offer only Applied Skills and Strategic Professional in **limited variants**. June and December offer **all variants**.
- Booking: session exams are booked via **Exam Planner** (MyACCA). OD exams are booked with a centre. You need to have no outstanding fees.
- Caps: **4 exams per exam cycle** and **8 distinct exams per calendar year** (see [qualification-structure.md](qualification-structure.md) §2).

## 2. Exam format by exam (current, 2026/27)

Pass mark is **50%** for every exam. All questions are compulsory (no optional-question choice in any current exam).

| Exam | Duration | Structure | Auto-marked share | Professional skills marks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **BT** | 2h | Section A: 46 OTs (30 × 2 + 16 × 1 = 76). Section B: 6 multi-task questions (MTQs) × 4 = 24. | 100% | — |
| **MA** | 2h | Section A: 35 OTs × 2 = 70. Section B: 3 MTQs × 10 = 30 (Budgeting, Standard costing, Performance measurement). | 100% | — |
| **FA** | 2h | Section A: 35 OTs × 2 = 70. Section B: 2 MTQs × 15 = 30 (one consolidation, one accounts preparation). | 100% | — |
| **LW-GLO / ENG** | 2h (on-demand) | Section A: 45 OTs (25 × 2 + 20 × 1 = 70). Section B: 5 MTQs × 6 = 30. | 100% | — |
| **LW (other variants)** | Session | Section B uses **written / constructed-response** answers (not MTQs). See [syllabus/LW.md](syllabus/LW.md). | Partial | — |
| **PM** | 3h (+10 min instructions) | A: 15 OTs × 2 = 30. B: 3 OT cases × 10 = 30. C: 2 constructed-response (CR) × 20 = 40. | 60% | — |
| **TX-UK** | 3h | A: 15 OTs × 2 = 30. B: 3 OT cases × 10. C: **1 × 10 + 2 × 15** (the 15-markers focus on income tax and corporation tax). Tax tables provided. | 60% | — |
| **TX (SGP, ZAF, MYS, etc.)** | 3h, June and December only | A: 15 MCQs × 2. B: 4 × 10 + 2 × 15 written questions (per agent research; other variants not all checked). | ~30% | — |
| **FR** | 3h | A: 15 OTs × 2. B: 3 OT cases × 10. C: 2 CR × 20. | 60% | — |
| **AA** | 3h (+10 min) | **Different from the rest.** A: **3 OT cases × 10 = 30** (no standalone OTs). B: **1 × 30 + 2 × 20** CR. | 30% | — |
| **FM** | 3h (+10 min) | A: 15 OTs × 2. B: 3 OT cases × 10. C: 2 CR × 20. Formulae sheet provided. | 60% | — |
| **SBL** | **3h15m** (includes "Reading, Planning and Reflection Time"; was 4h until the Sept 2023 change) | **Integrated case study.** **Pre-seen** released about **2 weeks** before the exam (also available in the exam). **3 compulsory tasks**, with exhibits (board papers, emails, spreadsheets). 100 marks. | 0% | **20** (communication, commercial acumen, analysis, scepticism, evaluation; each tested once, worth 4 marks) |
| **SBR (INT, IRL)** | 3h15m | A: **Q1 30** (group accounting, adjusting a **pre-populated spreadsheet** of a draft consolidated statement) + **Q2 20** (reporting and ethics). B: **2 × 25**. | 0% | **4** (2 in Q2, 2 in Q4) |
| **AFM** | 3h15m | A: 1 × 50 (40 + 10 professional). B: 2 × 25 (20 + 5 each). | 0% | 20 |
| **APM** | 3h15m | A: 1 × 50 (40 + 10). B: 2 × 25 (20 + 5). Restructured syllabus from Sept 2026. | 0% | 20 |
| **ATX (all variants)** | 3h15m | A: **1 × 50** (35 technical + **5 ethics** + 10 professional). B: **2 × 25** (20 + 5). This format has applied since June 2023. | 0% | 20 (+ 5 ethics) |
| **AAA (INT, UK)** | 3h15m | A: 1 × 50 at the audit **planning** stage (40 + 10). B: 2 × 25 (20 + 5); one is usually completion and reporting. | 0% | 20 |
| **FIA FA1, MA1, FA2, MA2** | 2h on-demand | OT-based. *Exact structures not verified in this research.* | 100% | — |

**Caveat:** the formats above were compiled from the 2026/27 syllabus and study guide PDFs by the per-exam research (see each syllabus file's Sources). The professional-skills mark counts for SBR (4) and the options (20) were read directly from those guides.

### Professional skills (Strategic Professional)
The Options exams assess four professional skills: **Communication; Analysis and evaluation; Professional scepticism (and judgement); Commercial acumen**. SBL adds **Evaluation** and treats **Analysis** separately. Section A of each Option examines all four. Each Section B question examines at least two of analysis and evaluation, scepticism, and commercial acumen.

## 3. Question-type catalogue (session CBE software)

From ACCA's *Guide to session CBEs*:

### 3.1 Objective Test (OT) question types (auto-marked; **2 marks each, all-or-nothing, no partial credit**)

| Type (ACCA name) | Behaviour | Engine notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Multiple choice (MCQ)** | Choose one of N options | — |
| **Multiple response (MRQ)** | Choose a specified number of options. The software **does not stop** you selecting more than required. | Validate the count; show a warning |
| **Fill in the blank** (number entry) | Type a number. Allowed characters: digits, **one decimal point**, **one leading minus**. **No commas.** Units shown outside the box; rounding instructions shown. | e.g. `-10234.35`. Tolerance and rounding rules per item |
| **Drag and drop** | Drag answers to response areas. There may be more choices than areas, and possibly multiple matches. | Shows "incomplete" until every area is filled |
| **Drop-down list** | Select from one or more drop-downs | — |
| **Hot spot** | Place an "X" on one point of an image. Correct if the X touches the correct region and no other candidate region. | — |
| **Hot area** | Select one or more areas of an image | Navigator shows "complete" even if partially answered |

Older ACCA material and providers also mention **multiple-response matching** and **enhanced matching** (grid or matrix matching). These are best modelled as matrix and drop-down variants. *The current CBE guide doesn't list them separately.*

AK, LW and FIA exams also use **1-mark OTs** (BT and LW) and **MTQs**: multi-part auto-marked questions worth 4–15 marks, with partial credit per task.

### 3.2 OT cases (Applied Skills Section B)
- **5 OTs × 2 marks = 10 marks** on one scenario. Any mix of OT types.
- **No dependencies** between the five items (a wrong first answer doesn't affect the others).
- The scenario stays on screen, with a movable **splitter bar** between scenario and questions.
- AA uses OT cases for its whole Section A.

### 3.3 Constructed response (CR) questions (human-marked)
- Answers go into a **word-processing** area or a **spreadsheet** area. Either can be blank or a **pre-formatted template**.
- **Markers see spreadsheet formulae**, not just values, so method marks are available.
- **Spreadsheet limits:** single worksheet only, no inserting or deleting rows and columns, protected cells in templates, autofill copies rather than extends a series, no function shortcuts (formulae must be typed). There is a list of supported formulae in Help.
- **Word processor:** cut, copy, paste, undo and redo, find and replace, bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, sub/superscript, six font sizes, tables, alignment, bullet and numbered lists, indent, currency symbols. **Reset** clears the whole response; Undo restores word-processing text but not spreadsheets.
- At Strategic Professional, answers are often required in a **specific format** (report to the board, memo, briefing notes, email), and some professional-skills marks reward this.

### 3.4 Exam-software functions (to replicate in a mock engine)
- **Flag for review**, Next and Previous, **Navigator**, and an **Item Review** screen at the end. Statuses: Complete / Incomplete / Unseen. MRQ and hot-area show complete even when partial; drag-and-drop and multi-blank shows incomplete until full.
- Timer and progress (can be hidden). A **15-minutes-remaining** warning.
- **Scratch pad**, which persists across questions and is **NOT marked**. Paper scrap is also provided at centres.
- On-screen **standard or scientific calculator**. Own calculator allowed if it can't store or display text.
- **Highlight and strikethrough** on the scenario. Copy and paste from the scenario or scratch pad into answers (Ctrl-C / Ctrl-V; double-click a cell to paste).
- **Help** with reference materials: **PM and FM formulae sheet**, **TX tax tables**, and FR and AA help. Strategic Professional: "permitted reference materials" (e.g. ATX tax tables; whether AFM provides a formulae sheet was *not confirmed*). See the syllabus files.
- Forced scrolling: you can't navigate away until you've scrolled through or opened all exhibits.
- **SBL pre-seen**: a separate exhibit-heavy CBE layout. ACCA publishes a separate SP CBE guide for SBL.

ACCA's **Practice Platform** replicates all of this, with self-marking against marking guides and sample answers.

## 5. Marking and quality assurance

- Each exam has an **Examiner**, an **Assessor**, a **Subject Co-ordinator** and an **Exam Sitter** (who sits the paper to check it can be done in time). ACCA Education Advisers coordinate.
- CR scripts are marked by **Qualified Markers** under the Examiner. Markers' meetings set the strategy, marking is **anonymous**, and there are extra checks on **borderline scripts**.
- **No quotas:** "We do not set a target percentage of candidates to be successful at each exam sitting."
- OTs and MTQs are machine-marked (all-or-nothing per 2-mark OT).
- **Post-results options:**
  - **Administrative review**: checks the result is accurate. It is "not a re-marking service" and is not available for on-demand CBEs.
  - **Mitigating circumstances**.
  - **Nullified results** and appeals.
- After results, ACCA offers **"My Exam Performance"** (strengths and weaknesses by area) and **Back on Track** support.
- Examiner's reports and sample answers are published after sessions and feed into the Practice Platform.

## 7. Mock exam engine checklist (derived)
1. Item types: MCQ, MRQ (with select-N), numeric entry (with the CBE character rules), drag-and-drop, drop-down, hot spot, hot area, matrix matching, and MTQ (multi-task with partial credit).
2. OT case container: 5 independent items sharing a scenario, with a splitter bar.
3. CR workspace: a word processor with the listed toolbar and a single-sheet spreadsheet with formula capture. Template (pre-populated) mode with locked cells.
4. Exam-level features: timer and warnings, navigator and item review with CBE status semantics, flags, scratchpad (unmarked), calculator, highlight and strikethrough, reference-material panel per exam.
5. Exam blueprints per exam (sections, marks, durations), versioned per syllabus year and for the **2027 formats**.
6. Marking: auto-mark OTs all-or-nothing; rubric-based CR marking aligned to published marking guides, plus **professional-skills marks** for Strategic Professional.

## 8. Formats from 2027 (redesign), summary
- **Foundations:** 2h; 45 × 2-mark OTs plus 1–2 MTQs (10 marks).
- **Knowledge:** 2h. K1: 35 OTs + 2 × 15 MTQs. K2: 35 OTs + 3 × 10 MTQs. K3: 50 × 2-mark OTs.
- **Expertise E1–E5:** 3h; 15 OTs (30) + 3 OT cases (30) + **2 × 20 CR** (40). Uniform, including E1.
- **Strategic Professional:** 3h15m, 80 technical + 20 professional marks, with reference materials provided.
  - S1: 50 / 30 / 20, with an ethics requirement in Section A and a sustainability question in Section B.
  - S2: pre-seen plus 3 tasks.
  - Options: 50 + 2 × 25.
