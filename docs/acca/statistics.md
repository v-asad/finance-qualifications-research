> **As of:** 22 October 2026. The latest session is **September 2026**, with results released 19 Oct 2026.
>
> **Source quality:**
> - **Mar 2023 – Jun 2025:** taken from OpenTuition's machine-readable compilation of ACCA's official global pass rates (dataset dated 2026-07-17).
> - **Sep 2025, Dec 2025, Mar 2026, Jun 2026 and Sep 2026:** read directly from ACCA press releases. All four overlapping sessions match the OpenTuition data exactly for every exam, so the earlier columns are high-confidence but **secondary**.
> - "—" means not published for that session. Applied Knowledge and LW are on-demand, and ACCA reports their rates only in the June and December releases.

### Observations for product design
- **The hardest exams are consistently AAA, APM, PM, AA and AFM** (roughly 35–47%). PM has the lowest Applied Skills rate (40–45%). AAA and APM have improved by about 5–8 points since 2023, but remain the lowest.
- **TX, SBL and ATX** sit at or just above 50%. **FR, FM and SBR** hover around 47–52%.
- **Applied Knowledge rates are high** (BT about 85–89%, FA about 68–72%), but **MA is the weakest AK exam** (63–68%).
- Pass rates are very **stable** session to session (±3 points). ACCA states it does "not set a target percentage of candidates to be successful at each exam sitting".
- Note: pass rate means the share of completed exams that passed. It is not an average mark. The pass mark for every exam is 50%.

## 2. Session volumes (ACCA press releases)

| Session | Students entered | Exams completed | On-demand exams (AK, FIA, LW) included / noted | New affiliates (completed all exams) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sep 2025 | 98,551 | 112,799 | "a further 37,662" on-demand | 3,807 |
| Dec 2025 | 120,633 | 137,609 ("largest volume since Dec 2021") | "a further 43,674" on-demand | 4,852 |
| Mar 2026 | 92,224 | 103,785 | n/a | 3,334 |
| Jun 2026 | 119,662 | 136,544 (includes 39,072 on-demand) | 39,072 | 4,690 |
| Sep 2026 | 102,263 | 114,239 | n/a | 4,042 |

Pattern: **June and December are the big sessions** (about 120k entrants), with all variants available. March and September are about 20% smaller (about 92–102k) and offer limited variants. Releases differ on whether the on-demand counts are inside or on top of the "exams completed" total, so treat that column as approximate. Roughly **15,000–17,000 students reach affiliate status per year** (sum of four sessions).

- ACCA was founded in **1904** and headquartered in London. It describes itself as "the only truly global professional accountancy body".
- Exam centres operate in **100+ countries**. Remote invigilation is offered only where there is no centre provision (see [exams-and-format.md](exams-and-format.md)).
- *Not verified in this research:* the number of Approved Employers and offices, and a country breakdown of students. Large markets (by public reporting and secondary sources) include the UK, Pakistan, India, China, Malaysia, Nigeria, the UAE and Vietnam; this is unquantified.
