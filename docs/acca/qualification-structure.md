# ACCA Qualification structure (current route, plus the 2027 transition)

> **As of:** 22 October 2026. This covers the **current** ACCA Qualification (13 exams). Its last sittings are in **June 2027** for Applied Skills and Strategic Professional. A **redesigned qualification** (11 exams plus Essential Employability Modules) starts in July and September 2027. See [future-qualification-2027.md](future-qualification-2027.md). Anything built now has to model **both** structures and the mapping between them.

---

## 1. The current route at a glance

```
Applied Knowledge (3 exams, on-demand CBE)          → ACCA Diploma in Accounting & Business (RQF L4)
   BT · MA · FA                                        (+ Foundations in Professionalism module)
        │
Applied Skills (6 exams; LW on-demand for ENG/GLO;   → ACCA Advanced Diploma in Accounting & Business
   the rest are session CBE)                           (+ Ethics & Professional Skills Module)
   LW · PM · TX · FR · AA · FM
        │
Ethics and Professional Skills Module (EPSM) — recommended before Strategic Professional
        │
Strategic Professional (session CBE)
   Essentials (both):  SBL · SBR
   Options (any 2 of): AFM · APM · ATX · AAA
        │
Practical Experience Requirement (PER): 36 months + 9 performance objectives
        │
ACCA membership (affiliate status on finishing the exams; member status once PER is complete)
```

Membership requires **13 exams** (or exemptions), **EPSM**, and **PER** (36 months plus 9 performance objectives). Source: the ACCA "About your results" page: "To be eligible for membership you must complete all 13 examinations, 9 Essential and 4 Options performance objectives, have a minimum of 36 months experience … and complete the Ethics and Professional Skills module." That wording is ACCA's own legacy phrasing. The current PER rule is **5 Essential and 4 Technical** POs (see §5).

## 2. Exam ordering (progression) rules

From ACCA's "Exam progression rules" page (current):

- **Modular order.** "Exams must be taken in modular order … however you can attempt the papers within each module in any order. If you wish to enter to sit exams in the next module, you must also enter for all outstanding exams in your current module."
- Module order: **Applied Knowledge → Applied Skills → EPSM → Strategic Professional (Essentials and Options)**.
- **EPSM** "can be taken at any point after completion of the Applied Knowledge exams". ACCA recommends finishing it before attempting any Strategic Professional exam, and says students who do "significantly increase their chances of passing".
- **Caps.** Maximum **4 exams per exam cycle**, and a maximum of **8 distinct exams per calendar year**.
- **Exam cycles** (used for the caps and for exemption closing dates): March = 1 Jan–31 Mar; June = 1 Apr–30 Jun; September = 1 Jul–30 Sep; December = 1 Oct–31 Dec.
- On-demand CBEs: the first seven Foundations-level papers, the Applied Knowledge exams, and LW-ENG and LW-GLO. Students must be registered before booking.

**Product implication:** a study planner should enforce the "enter all outstanding exams in the current module before moving up" rule, the 4-per-cycle and 8-per-year caps, and the EPSM-before-SP recommendation.

## 3. Time limits

- Applied Knowledge and Applied Skills have **no time limit**.
- **Strategic Professional has a seven-year rule.** "Once students pass their first Strategic Professional exam, they have seven years to pass their remaining exams." ACCA says this rule is **unchanged** in the 2027 redesign.
- ACCA "strongly recommend[s]" that PER experience be gained within the past five years. Since August 2023, legacy "import approved" PER time needs employer sign-off.

## 4. Ethics modules

| Module | Where it sits | Duration / cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Foundations in Professionalism (FiP)** | Needed for the ACCA Diploma in Accounting & Business, and mandatory for FIA qualifications | n/a | Interactive and online |
| **Ethics and Professional Skills Module (EPSM)** | After Applied Knowledge, before Strategic Professional | About **20 hours**; **£83** one-off fee, no resit fee | Realistic business simulations (leadership, communication, commercial awareness, etc.). Not invigilated. ACCA rules forbid sharing answers and forbid using module content to "build a product or service which competes with or is substantially similar to the Ethics Modules". |

EPSM's transition: it is **not part** of the redesigned qualification. If you complete it by about mid-July 2027 (exact date to be confirmed), you get credit for the new **Ethical, Sustainable Leadership EEM**. Otherwise you must take that EEM to become a member.

## 5. Practical Experience Requirement (PER)

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Time | **36 months** supervised experience in a relevant accounting or finance role. It can be gained before, during or after the exams, with multiple employers, part-time (pro-rata), or in voluntary roles. |
| Performance objectives | **9 in total: all 5 Essentials plus any 4 of 17 Technical.** |
| Evidence | For each PO, a **200–500 word statement** with workplace examples, signed off by a practical experience supervisor. |
| Supervisor | Must be a current member of an **IFAC member body**, or recognised by law in the trainee's country. Supervisors have had to upload IFAC membership evidence since 24 Aug 2023. |
| Recording tool | **MyExperience**, inside MyACCA |
| Approved Employers | Trainees still need 36 months, but can claim a **performance-objective exemption** where the employer's programme is approved |
| Non-relevant roles | Time does not count, but POs 2, 3 and 5 may still be achievable if supervised |

**Essentials (all five are mandatory):**
1. Ethics and professionalism
2. Stakeholder relationship management
3. Strategy, innovation and sustainable value creation (renamed and expanded with sustainability on 24 Aug 2023)
4. Governance, risk and control
5. Leadership and management

**Technical (choose four of 17):**

| Area | Performance objectives |
|---|---|
| Corporate and business reporting | Record and process transactions and events · Prepare external financial reports · Analyse and interpret financial reports |
| Financial management | Evaluate investment and financing decisions · Manage and control working capital · Identify and manage financial risk |
| Management accounting | Evaluate management accounting systems · Plan and control performance · Monitor performance |
| Taxation | Tax computations and assessments · Tax compliance and verification · Tax planning and advice |
| Audit and assurance | Prepare for and plan the audit and assurance process · Collect and evaluate evidence for an audit or assurance engagement · Review and report on the findings of an audit or assurance engagement |
| Advisory and consultancy | Business advisory |
| Data, digital and technology | Data analysis and decision support |

Each PO has a description and **five elements**, plus the statement. Since 24 Aug 2023, elements are no longer signed off one by one; only whole POs go to the supervisor. A **strict-liability clause** for trainees and supervisors was added on 6 Sep 2023.

**PER in the 2027 redesign:** "There will be no changes to the existing PER in 2027." Later, up to **6 months** may be gained through simulated experience in selected Optional Employability Modules. This will not apply from September 2027, and timing is still to be announced. Simulated experience will **not** count towards the UK audit qualification or practising certificates.

## 6. Embedded and academic awards

| Award | How to get it | Status (Oct 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| **ACCA Diploma in Accounting and Business (RQF Level 4)** | Pass BT, MA and FA, plus FiP | Current. Under the redesign, complete FA, MA, BT and FiP by about **mid-July 2027**. Students with all three exams but without FiP at transition get the Diploma only after completing K3. |
| **ACCA Advanced Diploma in Accounting and Business** | All Applied Knowledge and Applied Skills exams, plus EPSM | Current. All Applied Skills exams must be complete **in or before the June 2027 session**. LW-ENG and LW-GLO on-demand can be completed up to July 2027. |
| **BSc (Hons) Professional Accountancy, University of London** | For eligible students, affiliates and members. Eligibility requires **passing FR, TX and AA with ACCA**; exemptions for those three must be forfeited. Graduates get **PM and FM exemptions** automatically and free. | Current. ACCA says applications for the **March 2027 intake** "will open soon". Under the redesign the BSc route continues, and E4 and E5 are completed with the university partner. |
| **MSc Professional Accountancy, University of London** (academic direction by UCL) | For affiliates and members | Current. It continues as a Strategic Professional-level pathway in the redesign. |
| **Oxford Brookes BSc (Hons) Applied Accounting** | Legacy route: a Research and Analysis Project plus Applied Knowledge and Applied Skills exams | **Appears to be closed or legacy.** ACCA's current awards page lists only the UoL degrees, and the old BSc page no longer resolves. *Unverified: the exact final submission date was not confirmed in this research.* |
| **Certified Accounting Technician (CAT)** | FIA route: FA1, MA1, FA2, MA2, FAB/FBT, FMA, FFA, plus FAU, FTX and FFM; FiP; and a CAT experience requirement | Current. There is a £80 certification fee. The redesign keeps an optional CAT designation via Knowledge level plus CAT experience. |

## 7. Foundations in Accountancy (FIA)

There are no minimum entry requirements. FIA starts at roughly GCSE level (RQF 2) and runs to about first-year degree level (RQF 4).

| FIA award | Exams | Delivery |
|---|---|---|
| ACCA Diploma in Financial and Management Accounting (RQF Level 2) | FA1 Recording Financial Transactions; MA1 Management Information | On-demand CBE |
| ACCA Diploma in Financial and Management Accounting (RQF Level 3) | FA2 Maintaining Financial Records; MA2 Managing Costs and Finance | On-demand CBE |
| ACCA Diploma in Accounting and Business (RQF Level 4) | FBT, FMA, FFA (equivalent to BT, MA, FA) | On-demand CBE |
| Foundation Specialist Certificates (RQF Level 4), which with the above make up CAT | FAU Foundations in Audit; FTX Foundations in Taxation (UK; LSO; the MYS variant was withdrawn after June 2026); FFM Foundations in Financial Management | Session CBE |

FIA students must complete **Foundations in Professionalism**. FIA exemptions for FA1, FA2, MA1 and MA2 are free. FIA is replaced in the redesign by a **Foundations route**: F1 Accounts Preparation, F2 Management Information and Costing, F3 Decision Making with Data, and the Enterprise and Entrepreneurship EEM.

## 8. Variant (local) exams

Only **LW, TX and ATX** (and FTX) have country variants. AAA has INT, UK and IRL. SBR has INT and IRL; **SBR-UK was withdrawn after June 2025**. All other exams are global.

From ACCA's variant-exams page (Oct 2026). The table is reconstructed from a 3-column HTML layout, so please re-check before hard-coding.

| LW (law) | TX (tax) | ATX (advanced tax) |
|---|---|---|
| BWA (withdrawn after June 2027), CYP, CHN(?), **ENG\***, **GLO\***, HKG, IRL, LSO (withdrawn after June 2026), MWI, MYS, MLA, RUS, SGP, ZAF, VNM, ZWE (approx.) | BWA, CYP, CZE, HKG, IRL, MYS, MLA, PKN, POL, RUS, SGP, ZAF, UK, VNM, ZWE (TX-HUN withdrawn after June 2026) | CYP, HKG(?), IRL, MYS, MLA, SGP, ZAF, UK (approx.) |

\* LW-ENG and LW-GLO are **on-demand**. All other LW variants are session-based, and those exams are offered only in **June and December**. ACCA recommends **GLO** if there is no local LW. Joint Examination Scheme students without a local variant take UK (tax) and ENG or GLO (law).

**Session availability:** March and September sessions offer only Applied Skills and Strategic Professional exams in **limited variants**. June and December offer **all** variants.

## 9. Entry routes

- **ACCA Qualification minimum entry** (UK benchmark): **2 A-levels plus 3 GCSEs/O-levels in 5 separate subjects including English and Maths** (grades A–C / 9–4), or a local equivalent. ACCA publishes a per-country table of qualifications that are sufficient or insufficient for registration.
- Students who don't meet the minimum start with FIA or Foundations. Under the redesign, they complete Foundations and then transfer.
- Students with a relevant degree or professional qualification may claim exemptions from up to **9 exams** (Applied Knowledge plus Applied Skills). See [exemptions-fees-registration.md](exemptions-fees-registration.md).
- **Joint Examination Schemes** (dual membership with a national body) run in: Barbados, Belize, Botswana, Cambodia, Cyprus, Greece, Guyana, Jamaica, Lesotho, Malta, Sierra Leone, Swaziland/Eswatini, Trinidad and Tobago, and Vietnam.

## 10. How long it takes

- ACCA says: "If you [work and study at the same time], you could qualify in as little as three or four years." The PER floor of 36 months sets the minimum to membership, though PER can overlap with exams.
- At ACCA's planning default of **one exam per quarter** (Compass's default 12-week study period per exam), 13 exams take about 3.25 years without exemptions. With the 4-per-cycle cap, the theoretical exam minimum is far shorter, but this is rare.
- Diploma: "6–12 months". Advanced Diploma: "12–18 months" (ACCA estimates, depending on timing).
