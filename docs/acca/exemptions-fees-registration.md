# ACCA entry, registration, fees and exemptions

> **As of:** 22 October 2026. Fees were read from ACCA's "Fees and charges" page with **United Kingdom** selected. They are quoted in **GBP**, for **2026** subscriptions and the **December 2026** session.
>
> ACCA shows a country selector on the fee page. Exam centre fees for on-demand exams are set by the centres, and remote on-demand fees vary by country (see §3.2). Some markets may have different local pricing; *other countries were not checked*. Re-verify before showing prices in-app.

## 1. Entry requirements

| Route | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **ACCA Qualification** (standard) | UK benchmark: **2 A-levels plus 3 GCSEs/O-levels (grades A–C / 9–4) in 5 separate subjects, including English and Maths**, or a recognised national equivalent. ACCA's minimum-entry database lists, per country, which qualifications are "Sufficient" or "Insufficient for registration". Matriculation to a university bachelor's or master's programme is generally sufficient in many countries. |
| Part-complete degree | Can register: state your expected graduation date and submit a transcript. Some exemptions may be possible, but ACCA suggests waiting to finish the degree before claiming. |
| **Foundations in Accountancy (FIA)** | **No minimum entry requirements.** ACCA recommends starting at Level 2 if you have no formal qualifications. FIA is also the route for those who don't meet ACCA minimum entry. |
| Redesigned qualification (2027+) | Meet minimum entry → start at **Knowledge**. Otherwise → **Foundations**, then transfer. |
| Joint Examination Schemes | Barbados, Belize, Botswana, Cambodia, Cyprus, Greece, Guyana, Jamaica, Lesotho, Malta, Sierra Leone, Swaziland/Eswatini, Trinidad and Tobago, Vietnam. These may involve extra national-body fees. |

Registration is online at any time. The application asks for ID and qualification documents (with certified English translations if needed). ACCA aims to assess applications in **about 5 working days**. Applicants can consent to being registered on an alternative qualification (e.g. FIA) if they don't meet ACCA entry. There is a 14-day cooling-off period with a full refund (UK Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013).

Billing mechanics:

### 3.4 Indicative total cost (illustrative only)
Assumptions: no exemptions, UK pricing, standard entry, all first-time passes, AK at a centre at an assumed about £120 each, 4 years of student subscription.

£89 registration + 4 × £140 subscriptions + 3 × ~£120 (AK) + 6 × £160 (AS, assuming LW as a session variant; LW-ENG/GLO is on-demand) + £83 EPSM + £282 SBL + £208 SBR + 2 × £208 options ≈ **£2,950**. Tuition and study materials (often the larger cost) and resits are extra. *This is our estimate, not an ACCA figure.*

## 4. Exemptions

### 4.1 Policy (current qualification)
- Exemptions are available for **up to 9 exams**: all of **Applied Knowledge (BT, MA, FA)** and **Applied Skills (LW, PM, TX, FR, AA, FM)**.
- There are **no exemptions** for **Strategic Professional** exams, and **none on the basis of work experience** (except CAT, where practical experience can be used).
- Exemptions come from:
  1. **Assessed (accredited) programmes**: university or college degrees that ACCA has mapped module by module, for specific graduation years.
  2. **The exemption framework** for unassessed national qualifications. This uses an independent equivalency agency and subject-area bands. Example from ACCA: an unassessed programme "may be awarded up to a maximum of the first four exams".
  3. **Professional qualifications** (other accountancy bodies). See §4.3.
- **Conditional exemptions** apply to students still on an accredited course ("up to the FM exam"). They convert once you graduate.
- Fail, marginal-fail, compensated or condoned module passes **don't count**.
- **Tool:** ACCA's **Exemptions calculator** (https://www.accaglobal.com/gb/en/help/exemptions-calculator.html) is the authoritative per-qualification lookup.

### 4.2 Fees and administration
| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Fee per exemption: Applied Knowledge exam | **£86** |
| Fee per exemption: Applied Skills exam | **£114** |
| FIA: FAB/FBT, FMA, FFA, FTX, FFM, FAU | £86 each. **FA1, FA2, MA1, MA2 exemptions are free.** |
| Payment window | Within **30 days** of award |
| Cancellation or forfeit | Within **14 days** of award for a refund; after that, non-refundable |
| Partial claim | You can accept some exemptions now and claim others later |
| Fraud | Exemptions based on false documents mean registration is voided with no refund (and disciplinary action for pre-2019 registrants) |

### 4.3 Typical exemption patterns (indicative; confirm with the calculator)
| Background | Typical outcome | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| ACCA-accredited accounting / finance degree | Up to all 9 AK and AS exams (varies by university, programme and graduation year) | High (policy); the specific count varies |
| Unassessed relevant degree in a recognised national system | Banded by subject area; e.g. up to the first 4 exams for some cases | Medium |
| Non-relevant degree | Often few or none beyond what the framework assigns | Medium (*unverified*) |
| Members of other IFAC bodies (e.g. ICAEW, ICAS, CA ANZ, CPA Australia, CIMA, ICAI, ICAP and other national CA institutes) | Commonly significant exemptions, often all 9 for fully qualified CAs. ACCA also offers specific membership routes for some bodies. | **Unverified in this research.** Check the Exemptions calculator "professional qualifications" path. |
| CIMA qualifications | Partial exemptions depending on level achieved | **Unverified** |
| University of London BSc Professional Accountancy graduates | **PM and FM** exemptions applied automatically and free. Eligibility requires *passing* (not being exempt from) FR, TX and AA. | High (ACCA page) |

### 4.4 Audit-qualification caveats (UK and Ireland)
- To get the **UK or Irish practising certificate and audit qualification**, you must pass the **UK or IRL variants of LW, TX, SBR and AAA**. Exemptions from LW or TX based on degrees without UK/Irish law or tax coverage must be replaced by sitting the variant exams.
- Exemptions based on qualifications gained **more than 5 years** before registration must be forfeited (and the exams sat) if you want the UK audit qualification.
- **Note:** SBR-UK was withdrawn after June 2025 (see [syllabus/SBR.md](syllabus/SBR.md)), so the FAQ's mention of a UK SBR variant seems outdated. *Treat the current audit-qualification rule for SBR as unverified.*

### 4.5 Exemptions under the 2027 redesign
- **Foundations, Knowledge and Expertise exams:** all exemptible.
- **EEMs:** only Responsible Business Management is exemptible.
- **Strategic Professional:** nothing exemptible.
- The calculator and database update in **July 2027**. Details are in [future-qualification-2027.md](future-qualification-2027.md).
