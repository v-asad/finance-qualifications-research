# The redesigned ACCA Qualification (from 2027): what's changing and the transition

> **As of:** 22 October 2026. **Status:** ACCA has announced this officially and published syllabi and specimen exams. Syllabus PDFs for exams from July 2027 are dated "FINAL – March 2026".
>
> **Why it matters for the product:** the current 13-exam structure ends with the **June 2027** session for Applied Skills and Strategic Professional. From **July 2027** (Foundations and Knowledge) and **September 2027** (Expertise and Strategic Professional), students sit **new exam codes and syllabi**. Any content taxonomy, question bank or progress tracker built now needs:
> 1. a mapping from current to new exams (credit transfer), and
> 2. versioned syllabi (2026/27 for the current exams; 2027/28 for the new ones).

---

## 1. New structure

| Level | Exams | Essential Employability Module (EEM, mandatory) | RQF level | Exam format (from 2027/28 syllabus PDFs) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Foundations** (for students who don't meet minimum entry) | F1 Accounts Preparation · F2 Management Information and Costing · F3 Decision Making with Data | Enterprise and Entrepreneurship | (not stated; replaces FIA) | 2h CBE. **Section A: 45 × 2-mark OTs**. Section B: F1 = 1 × 10-mark MTQ (sole-trader FS); F2 = 2 × 5-mark MTQs (accounting for costs; costing techniques); F3 = 2 × 5-mark MTQs (short-term and long-term decisions). Pass mark 50%. |
| **Knowledge** | K1 Financial Accounting · K2 Management Accounting · K3 Business Law (ENG, GLO) | Responsible Business Management (replaces BT) | 4 | 2h CBE, on-demand from July 2027. **K1:** 35 × 2-mark OTs + 2 × 15-mark MTQs (one on individual FS, section F; one on consolidated FS, section G, which can include interpretation). **K2:** 35 × 2-mark OTs + 3 × 10-mark MTQs (budgeting; standard costing and variances; decision-making). **K3:** 50 × 2-mark OTs. Pass mark 50%. |
| **Expertise** | E1 Taxation (UK + variants) · E2 Financial Reporting · E3 Audit, Risk and Control · E4 Finance and Investment · E5 Performance with Data Analysis | Digital Tech and Innovation | 6 | 3h CBE, **all five use the same structure**: Section A 15 × 2-mark OTs (30); Section B 3 × 10-mark OT cases (5 × 2-mark OTs each); **Section C 2 × 20-mark constructed-response questions**. Pass mark 50%. (Note: E1 has 2 × 20 in Section C, unlike current TX's 1 × 10 + 2 × 15.) |
| **Strategic Professional** | **Essentials:** S1 Business and Sustainability Reporting (INT, IRL) · S2 Strategic Business Leader. **Options (choose ONE):** SAA Audit and Assurance Professional (INT, UK, IRL, ZAF) · SCF Corporate Finance Professional · **SDS Data Science Professional (new)** · SPI Performance and Insights Professional · STA Taxation Advisory Professional (UK + variants) | Ethical, Sustainable Leadership | 7 | **All 3h15m CBE**, 100 marks = **80 technical + 20 professional skills** marks. "ACCA will provide permitted reference materials for candidates to use during the examinations." Pass mark 50%. Details below. |

Practical Experience Requirement (PER): **36 months plus performance objectives, unchanged in 2027.**

### Strategic Professional exam designs (2027/28)

| Exam | Structure | Professional marks |
|---|---|---|
| **S1 Business and Sustainability Reporting** | Section A: 1 × 50-mark group-accounting question (36 technical + **8 professional ethics** + 6 professional skills). It requires correcting a draft consolidated statement in a **pre-populated spreadsheet**, and always includes an ethics requirement (D1: ACCA Code threats and safeguards). Section B: **1 × 30-mark** (26 + 4) and **1 × 20-mark** (18 + 2). The 20-mark question is always set in a **sustainability context** (syllabus section C). | Section A: communication, analysis and evaluation. Section B: analysis and evaluation, scepticism, commercial acumen. |
| **S2 Strategic Business Leader** | 100% integrated case study. **Pre-seen information is released two weeks before the exam.** The exam has **3 tasks** of varying marks totalling 100, with role-play (the candidate takes a specified role). | 80 technical + 20 professional skills |
| **SAA / SCF / SPI / SDS / STA** | Section A: 1 × 50-mark scenario (40 technical + 10 professional, all four professional skills). Section B: 2 × 25-mark (20 + 5 each, at least two of: analysis and evaluation, scepticism (and judgement for SAA), commercial acumen). | 20 in total |

Exam-specific rules:
- **SAA:** Section A focuses on audit **planning**. One Section B question is on **completion, review and reporting**.
- **SCF:** every exam has questions focusing on sections C (advanced investment appraisal) and E (treasury and risk). Section A draws on at least two of sections A–E.
- **SPI:** Section A covers sections A and B. One Section B question comes mainly from C (performance reporting), the other mainly from D (technology and data analytics).
- **SDS:** Section A includes a requirement from F (governance, ethics and risk). One Section B question always tests C (programming for business). Every exam has a G (data storytelling) requirement. **Each question's scenario uses an accounting and finance topic** (section E). The specimen ships with an **.xlsx appendix**.
- **STA:** multi-tax scenarios throughout.

### ACCA rollout timeline (relevant to content and tooling)

- Nov 2025: Foundations and Knowledge guidance pack released.
- Mar 2026: Expertise and SP guidance pack released, including SDS.
- Jul–Oct 2026: Study Hub and Educator Hub content updated for providers.
- **Jan 2027:** updated Study Hub for Foundations and Knowledge (students).
- **Mar 2027:** ACCA Practice Platform updated for all exams; Study Hub updated for Expertise and SP.
- Apr 2027: updated ACCA-X for Foundations and Knowledge.
- June 2027: EEM pre-launch webinars. July 2027: EEMs available.
- By **December 2026**, ACCA will publish a "comprehensive update" on the **Approved Learning Partner programme**.
- ACCA's **transition tool** (an interactive credit calculator) is live at /future-acca-qualification-transition-tool.html.

### Exemptions under the redesign

- **Foundations:** all exams exemptible; the Enterprise and Entrepreneurship EEM is **not**.
- **Knowledge:** all exams exemptible; the Responsible Business Management EEM **is** exemptible (BT-accredited programmes carry over).
- **Expertise:** all exams exemptible; the Digital Tech and Innovation EEM is **not**.
- **Strategic Professional:** **no** exemptions for exams or the EEM.
- The exemption database and calculator update in July 2027. Accreditation applications from July 2027 are assessed against the new design.

## 3. Product design takeaways
- **Dual-taxonomy period (now to about Sep 2027 and beyond).** Students sitting resits in March or June 2027 need old-syllabus content, while new starters from mid-2027 need new-syllabus content. Map topics so content can be reused where it's equivalent (e.g. FR to E2 is largely the same IFRS core, with sustainability, ISSB and IFRS 18 added).
- **Format changes to model in a mock engine:**
  - E1 Section C changes to 2 × 20.
  - S1 replaces SBR's 4-question format with 50 / 30 / 20.
  - S2 is 3 tasks with pre-seen.
  - Options move to 80 + 20 professional marks.
  - K3 becomes 50 × 2-mark OTs.
  - F-level exams have 45 OTs plus small MTQs.
- **New content areas** with little existing third-party material: SDS (Python-style programming for business, statistics, data storytelling), the DTI EEM, sustainability reporting in E2 and S1, and project management in S2.
- **A transition-credit calculator** for users is a natural feature. ACCA has its own tool, so ours would be a convenience.
